## Editorial note on names, places, and sources

This entry concerns one instrument, not every high-energy accelerator, quark
matter experiment, or weapon that uses a particle beam. The Projected Baryonic
Reheating-and-Quench Array was assembled at the Apsis Range, discharged once,
and destroyed with most of the range that could have reproduced its operating
geometry. Its scientific lineage is human and reconstructable. Its terminal
yield is not.

The principal record consists of Directorate design ledgers, separated energy
accounts, surviving timing relays, remote calorimetry, particle and neutrino
records, fragments of the prepared baryonic target, exclusion-zone collection
traps, proceedings that produced the Common Matter Interdict, and later cultic
copies of genuine but incomplete technical documents. Much of the instrumentation
nearest the focus saturated before it was destroyed. A large surviving number
is therefore not automatically an accurate number, and a repeated number is not
automatically independent evidence.

This entry uses the standard Codex evidentiary scale:

- **Grade A:** independent physical records, material evidence, and credible
  contemporary testimony agree;
- **Grade B:** strong technical or material evidence exists, but direct
  measurement is incomplete, model-dependent, or inaccessible;
- **Grade C:** several independent traditions agree while consequential details
  remain in conflict;
- **Grade D:** one substantial source or a dependent cluster supports the
  claim; and
- **Grade E:** folklore, propaganda, visionary testimony, rumor, or an
  unexplained anomaly.

The grade attaches to each claim. The existence of the array, the forty-seven
millisecond firing interval, the gross energy release, and the production of
unusually massive strange-rich charged tracks are Grade A. The quoted peak
temperature is Grade B because it is inferred from spectra and energy density,
not read from a thermometer. The production of long-lived strangelets is Grade
B. The escape of a catalytic seed is Grade E.

The distinction is the center of the subject. The Apsis Discharge certainly
happened. It certainly produced matter unlike the prepared target. It has not
been shown to have begun the end attributed to it.

## Abstract

The Rehearsal was not a heat ray. Temperature was never carried through space
from the instrument to its target. The array projected separately accelerated
energy and baryonic matter along several opposed paths. At their common focus,
the packets possessed little net momentum and an immense center-of-momentum
energy. Only there did they thermalize into a sequence of microscopic kernels
whose outgoing spectra were consistent with an effective temperature near

> **1.4 x 10^30 kelvin, approximately one hundredth of the Planck
> temperature.**

Each kernel existed for roughly `10^-41` to a few `10^-41` second. The complete
instrument did not sustain one such region for forty-seven milliseconds. It
made a train of separate regions, displaced in space and time so that no one
region was intended to enclose enough energy to form a trapped surface.

The distinction was necessary because a near-Planck thermal volume cannot be
made macroscopic merely by supplying more power. Its energy density makes its
own gravity important. A sufficiently large kernel lies within its own
Schwarzschild radius and becomes a microscopic horizon rather than an expanding
fireball. The array therefore operated against two competing thresholds. Too
little convergence produced an energetic but comparatively ordinary particle
shower. Too much produced a horizon-dominated burst. The desired **open quench**
lay between them: a kernel hot enough to erase nuclei and restore high-energy
particle symmetries, but still able to expand and cool through the regimes in
which quarks hadronize.

The array deliberately loaded that cooling path with a net excess of baryons.
As the kernel expanded, it entrained a colder baryonic sleeve and crossed the
poorly known high-density region of the quantum chromodynamic phase diagram.
The best reconstruction is that the result at Apsis was a large but unknowable
strangelet yield. Most of that yield must have decayed, annihilated, or broken
into ordinary hadrons. What
cannot be excluded is that some droplets entered a narrow stability island in
which strange quark matter was stable at zero pressure, could incorporate
ordinary nuclei, and divided into viable daughters as it grew.

That stability island is the Rehearsal's one indispensable departure from
established physics. The array did not create energy, baryon number, distance,
or time. Daughtering did not multiply matter. It divided converted matter into
more catalytic interfaces. If the island exists, if at least one suitable seed
survived the open quench, if it reached enough ordinary matter to begin growth,
and if each converted reservoir released more viable daughters than the larger
environment destroyed or lost, the chain entered **the Succession**. The number
of independent seed trials made at Apsis was large enough that, under those
conditions, extinction of every lineage would become negligible.

The terminal claim is precise even when common speech is not. The Succession
can convert ordinary baryonic matter in its future causal domain. It does not
convert photons, dark matter, vacuum, black holes, empty space, or regions
permanently beyond a cosmological horizon. It does not travel faster than light.

The Rehearsal's prompt destruction was vast.

Its feared consequence was not a larger explosion. It was inheritance.

## The name

The scientific programs from which the instrument descended used
**reheating** and **quench** as laboratory words. Reheating supplied a prepared
system with enough energy to restore states unavailable at ordinary temperature.
Quenching controlled, or attempted to control, the path by which that system
cooled. The program did not claim to reproduce the origin of the universe, the
cosmological expansion, or the unknown process by which an early universe may
have been reheated after inflation. It reconstructed selected local conditions
and watched them cease.

The name *Rehearsal* first described that modest distinction. A reconstruction
was not the event itself. A microscopic hot kernel was not creation. A
forty-seven millisecond train was not an epoch. The apparatus rehearsed only a
sequence: extreme energy, expansion, symmetry breaking, confinement, and
residue.

After Apsis the word acquired its threat. If the output included self-propagating
strange matter, the firing had not been the terminal performance. It had been a
rehearsal for a conversion that would continue without the instrument.

Range staff used **the Apsis Engine** without irony. Opponents used **the Last
Furnace**, although the focus was neither a furnace nor the last source of heat.
The Houses of the Lower State call it **the Proof**. Their usage does not mean
that stable strange matter has been proved. It means they believe the weapon
proved ordinary matter to be provisional.

In Common Matter proceedings, “to rehearse” now means to conduct a test whose
claimed limitation depends upon an unmeasured tail of its own yield. In range
engineering, “one more rehearsal” means that the next repetition is being
offered as a way to learn whether repetition remains safe.

## What the instrument is

The name covers a specific assembly and six separable functions:

1. **The reservoir:** distributed energy stores and generators kept far enough
   apart that the unfired instrument did not reproduce the compactness it was
   designed to create.
2. **The acceleration works:** opposed final-stage emitters that converted
   stored energy into particle and field packets with extreme center-of-momentum
   energy.
3. **The convergence crown:** the geometry, clocks, ranging systems, and
   independent control lines that brought those packets to one overlap region
   with nearly cancelling momentum.
4. **The baryon-bias system:** a separate injection that supplied net baryon
   number rather than relying upon baryon-antibaryon pair production.
5. **The quench sleeve:** prepared, colder baryonic material encountered by an
   expanding kernel after the peak state had already begun to decay.
6. **The kernel scheduler:** the firing solution that divided the discharge into
   distinct microscopic trials rather than making one continuous gravitating
   volume.

Removing any one function did not make the remaining apparatus harmless. An
unbiased convergence could still produce an extreme radiation and particle
burst. A baryon injector remained a relativistic weapon. A failed crown could
redirect packets into an emitter or an unintended body. The complete terminal
mechanism, however, required all six.

The instrument was not a family of compact weapons. No credible design places a
complete Rehearsal in a ship, projectile, or surface emplacement. Its components
occupied separated trajectories and installations across an interplanetary
range. The final geometry was a siege-scale commitment visible before firing and
largely unrecoverable afterward.

The instrument also was not the Succession. The array could make seeds. It could
not command them, recall them, select which stability state survived, or halt a
chain after it ceased to depend upon the array.

## Technical foundations

### Temperature is an outcome

Temperature describes a distribution of energy among degrees of freedom in a
chosen frame. It is not a substance that can be launched. The Rehearsal's
projectors carried energetic packets. Their arrangement made the total spatial
momentum at the focus small compared with the total energy, establishing a
short-lived center-of-momentum frame in which repeated interactions could
produce a roughly thermal spectrum.

This momentum closure mattered as much as raw energy. A single ultraenergetic
packet striking a stationary target gives most products a common forward boost.
They leave before exchanging enough energy to resemble a stationary hot region.
Opposed packets can place far more of their energy into invariant mass at the
focus. Additional axes reduce angular momentum and prevent one escaping jet
from carrying away the state the quench system is meant to study.

Even then, **temperature** is an approximation. A viable kernel contained too
few quanta and lived too briefly to be an ordinary thermodynamic body. The
Directorate used **spectral-equivalent temperature**: the temperature of an
idealized equilibrium distribution whose characteristic energies and relative
populations best matched the escaping products. The Codex retains that phrase
where the distinction matters.

### The calibrated scale

The Planck temperature is approximately `1.4168 x 10^32` kelvin. It is not a
demonstrated maximum temperature. It is the scale at which the thermal energy
`k_B T` equals the Planck energy and a complete quantum theory of gravity is
expected to become indispensable.

The Apsis setpoint was approximately one hundredth of that temperature:

> `T_R = T_P / 100`, approximately `1.4168 x 10^30 K`

At this temperature, `k_B T_R` is about `1.22 x 10^17` gigaelectronvolts,
or roughly `1.96 x 10^7` joules per characteristic thermal quantum. This is
far beyond any directly validated particle interaction. It is nevertheless
below the Planck energy. For an isolated collision, dimensional estimates do
not require quantum-gravity corrections to be of order unity merely because
the phrase *near-Planck* is used. The Directorate's unpredictability came from
the combination of an enormous untested energy interval, an unknown particle
inventory, deliberate baryon loading, and collective self-gravity near a
horizon threshold.

An ideal photon gas at `T_R` would have an energy density of approximately
`3.05 x 10^105` joules per cubic metre. Extrapolating the known high-temperature
particle inventory as an ideal relativistic plasma raises the estimate to about
`1.63 x 10^107` joules per cubic metre. Unknown high-energy particles could
raise it again. Interactions, chemical potential, geometry, and the failure of
exact equilibrium change the estimate, but not the conclusion: a visibly sized
volume at this temperature is not an engineering object. It is a gravitational
event.

The corresponding thermal length,

> `l_T = h-bar c / k_B T_R`

is approximately `1.62 x 10^-33` metre. A useful kernel had to span several
such lengths to support even a coarse spectral distribution. Under the known-
particle extrapolation, the equality between physical and Schwarzschild radius
occurs at approximately `9.42 x 10^-33` metre. The corresponding energy is
approximately `5.70 x 10^11` joules and the light-crossing time approximately
`3.14 x 10^-41` second. A useful open-quench kernel was therefore expected to
span from a few `10^-33` metre to order `10^-32` metre, with no safely viable
radius fixed more precisely than the extrapolation allowed.

Using only photons moves the formal crossing to approximately
`6.88 x 10^-32` metre and `4.17 x 10^12` joules. That is a lower-particle-count
model bracket, not permission to operate a `10^-31`-metre kernel. A cell of that
size under the known-particle extrapolation would already be deeply trapped.

For a spherical region of energy density `u`, the elementary compactness
estimate is

> `r_H` is approximately `[3 c^4 / (8 pi G u)]^(1/2)`.

At the Rehearsal setpoint, the competing equilibrium extrapolations therefore
bracket the trapped-surface threshold at roughly `10^12` joules per kernel,
within about one order of magnitude. The mass equivalent is milligrams to tens
of milligrams. Those figures are not the mass of strangelets produced. They are
the energy that must briefly occupy the correlation volume.

The expected light-crossing time of a viable kernel is order `10^-41` second.
This, not forty-seven milliseconds, is the duration of one kernel. A
near-threshold kernel contains only hundreds to tens of thousands of
characteristic quanta under familiar particle counts. Its temperature is
statistical, its geometry fluctuates, and changing a small number of
interactions can change which gravitational branch it enters.

### Compactness and the three branches

Array engineers tracked a dimensionless compactness parameter,

> `chi = 2 G E / (R c^4)`,

where `E` was the energy believed to occupy a region of effective radius
`R`. The expression is exact neither for a nonspherical collision nor for a
rapidly changing quantum state. It was useful because it described the
controller's central problem.

**Underconvergence** occurred when packets failed to exchange enough energy in
one center-of-momentum region. The focus produced jets, pair cascades, hard
radiation, neutrinos, and violent target ablation, but comparatively little
controlled cooling through a baryon-rich phase. It was a failed experiment and
a successful conventional catastrophe.

**Open quench** occurred when the hot correlation volume formed below its
effective trapped-surface threshold and expanded into the prepared sleeve. This
branch maximized the opportunity for a baryon-rich plasma to cool through the
quark confinement regime. It was the desired scientific branch and the branch
of greatest strangelet concern.

**Horizon poisoning** occurred when a kernel crossed its local compactness
threshold. Semiclassical treatment predicts a microscopic black hole with an
initial temperature of order `10^27` to `10^29` kelvin and a lifetime
of order `10^-32` to `10^-29` second for the relevant milligram-scale
mass, followed by an increasingly hard evaporation burst. The final stages are
not covered by a validated theory. A horizon-poisoned kernel generally removes
baryons from the intended open cooling path and therefore lowers recoverable
strangelet yield. It does not make the shot benign. Its energy reappears in a
hard, poorly directed burst, subject to the unresolved physics of the final
evaporation interval.

No evidence supports the popular claim that an Apsis-scale microscopic horizon
would quietly grow until it consumed a planet. A tiny hot hole loses energy far
faster than it accretes diffuse surrounding matter under the semiclassical
model. The horizon danger in the Rehearsal was not indefinite accretion. It was
that the open and horizon branches were interleaved below the instrument's
ability to measure them, so the prompt spectrum and seed yield could not be
predicted from total firing energy alone.

### Why the array made a kernel train

Adding energy to one kernel eventually raises compactness more effectively than
it raises useful open volume. The scheduler therefore divided the discharge
among an unknown number of microscopic overlaps. Each was separated from the
next in space, time, or outgoing expansion direction. The train swept through
the prepared target as a macroscopic firing envelope while never intentionally
making a macroscopic region share the peak temperature.

The number of kernels at Apsis is not recoverable. Surviving Directorate counts
refer variously to commanded overlaps, detected prompt peaks, inferred open
quenches, and clock cycles. None is a direct count of thermally established
kernels. The lowest compatible counts still imply an enormous total delivered
energy. The highest depend upon classifying weak underconvergences as kernels.

This uncertainty cannot be removed by dividing the gross energy by a preferred
energy per kernel. Some energy missed the target, some entered horizon-poisoned
branches, some remained in coherent jets, and some was deposited after an open
kernel had begun to expand. The range records close the overall energy account
within their uncertainties. They do not close the kernel census.

### Baryon loading and mass accounting

Extreme energy readily makes particle-antiparticle pairs, including quarks and
antiquarks. Pair production does not create net baryon number. A fireball made
from radiation alone can be rich in quarks at peak temperature and nearly empty
of surviving baryons after annihilation.

The Rehearsal avoided that result by injecting baryon number separately. Opposed
neutron-rich packets entered the overlap with the energy packets, giving the
plasma a nonzero baryon chemical potential. A colder sleeve surrounded the
later expansion path. Most of the baryonic mass available to become strange
matter was not compressed into the smallest peak-temperature volume. It was
entrained after that volume expanded and cooled. This distinction prevents a
common but impossible reconstruction in which a macroscopic target mass is
placed inside a `10^-32`-metre sphere without changing the energy density or
forming a horizon.

All versions of the account preserve three ledgers:

- delivered energy came from the distributed reservoirs and the kinetic energy
  of the injected packets;
- net baryon number came from the bias packets, the quench sleeve, and any
  target matter subsequently incorporated; and
- strangeness could change through particle production and weak interactions,
  while baryon number remained an excellent account outside the unresolved
  horizon endpoint.

If strange matter has a lower energy per baryon than ordinary nuclear matter,
conversion releases the difference as heat, radiation, neutrinos, kinetic
energy, and work done on the surrounding material. It does not release the
entire rest mass of each baryon. If the strange phase is not lower, the seed
requires continuing external energy and the autonomous chain stops.

Daughtering likewise creates no mass. A mature strangelet that divides produces
several smaller pieces whose baryon numbers sum, after ordinary ejecta and
radiation are counted, to that of the parent and its captured material. The
number of catalytic surfaces can grow while the amount of matter remains
accountable.

### The quench through quantum chromodynamics

At peak temperature, the labels *nucleus*, *atom*, and *strangelet* do not
describe persistent bound objects. The relevant state is an extreme mixture of
fields and relativistic particles. Strangelets, if any, form later.

An expanding kernel first passes through many untested high-energy regimes. By
the time its characteristic temperature falls to a few times `10^12` kelvin,
or roughly 100 to 200 megaelectronvolts, the strong interaction confines quarks
into hadrons under familiar low-density conditions. At low baryon chemical
potential, present physics favors a smooth crossover rather than the violent
first-order separation imagined in some older primordial-quark-nugget models.
At high baryon chemical potential, the phase diagram, surface tension, pairing
states, and nucleation dynamics remain incompletely known.

The baryon-bias system was designed to drive the cooling plume through that
unknown region. Directorate models sought a path on which quark-rich droplets
would separate faster than they evaporated, acquire comparable populations of
up, down, and strange quarks through strong strange-pair production and
selective loss of antistrangeness-bearing hadrons, and cool below their
finite-size stability threshold before ordinary hadronization consumed them.
Weak equilibration belongs to a droplet that survives this first separation; it
cannot complete during the shortest quench intervals.

A freely expanding near-threshold kernel could reach the confinement regime in
roughly `10^-23` second by scale estimates. Interaction with the sleeve,
continued injection, and overlapping wakes extended parts of the quench. No
surviving record resolves one universal time. The defensible range for the
droplet-forming interval is approximately `10^-23` to `10^-20` second,
many orders shorter than any material controller could observe and adjust.

The quench therefore was ballistic once begun. The array could choose initial
conditions. It could not watch a droplet form and decide whether to keep it.

## The catalytic stability island

Ordinary physics permits a hypothesis: sufficiently large matter containing
up, down, and strange quarks might, for some values of the strong-interaction
parameters, have less energy per baryon than the most stable ordinary nuclei.
Finite lumps could then be stable or metastable above some minimum baryon
number. Their actual stability, charge, surface behavior, and production rate
are unknown. Important classes of known models make intermediate strangelets
positively charged, which suppresses the capture of positive nuclei and removes
the familiar runaway scenario.

The Rehearsal's terminal mechanism requires more. The Codex records these
requirements as the **catalytic stability island**. They are explicit setting
postulates, not conclusions of established particle physics:

1. A finite range of baryon numbers and cooling histories produces strangelets
   stable at zero external pressure and ordinary ambient temperature.
2. Above a catalytic size, the dressed surface is effectively neutral or
   negative at the distance relevant to an approaching nucleus, so Coulomb
   repulsion does not protect ordinary matter.
3. Incorporation of ordinary baryons followed by weak equilibration into the
   strange phase is exothermic.
4. The conversion interface remains active faster than heating, ablation, and
   dilution can isolate it from all further matter.
5. Above a larger split size, surface and charge energetics favor division into
   two or more daughter strangelets that themselves remain above the stable and
   catalytic minima.
6. Some daughters leave the converted reservoir without being evaporated,
   spalled below stability, trapped behind a horizon, or carried permanently
   beyond other baryonic matter.

No single one is enough. A stable positive strangelet can be inert. A catalytic
strangelet that never divides can destroy one connected body and stop. A
dividing phase whose daughters evaporate cannot cross an interplanetary void. A
phase that is favored only at neutron-star pressure does not convert ordinary
planets at zero pressure.

The Directorate did not know the boundaries of the island. Its strategy was
statistical: produce many independent droplets across many quench histories,
charges, baryon numbers, angular momenta, and cooling rates. The desired output
was not one designed strangelet. It was a distribution broad enough that, if a
catalytic island existed anywhere within the accessible state space, some part
of the yield would land inside it.

That is why the quantity matters even though the mass may be small. A large
number of independent seeds samples a rare stability tail. If one surviving
seed is autocatalytic, surrounding matter supplies the later mass.

## The Apsis yield

No complete yield inventory exists.

Exclusion-zone traps recorded massive charged particles with unusually low
charge-to-mass ratios and decay products rich in strangeness. Those tracks are
Grade A evidence that the discharge produced heavy strange-rich objects. They
do not by themselves show that the objects were strangelets, stable in vacuum,
negatively charged after cooling, or capable of catalysis. Several tracks ended
in fragmentation or ordinary hadronic showers. Others left the instrumented
solid angle.

Recovered target fragments contained isotopic and lattice anomalies consistent
with an extreme neutron-rich particle bath. None retained a macroscopic region
of confirmed strange quark matter. Some Directorate ledgers predict a much
larger neutral yield than the charged traps could measure. Those predictions are
Grade B as models and no better than Grade D as counts.

The Codex accepts at Grade B that some of these objects were strangelets and
that their population was large compared with every prior controlled yield. It
does not infer from that finding that any member was stable at zero pressure,
catalytic, or capable of daughtering.

The surviving facts support three statements:

- the initial strange-rich yield was large in number compared with any prior
  controlled experiment;
- the number, mass spectrum, charge spectrum, and lifetime distribution are
  unknowable from the surviving observations; and
- no recovery campaign demonstrated that every possible seed decayed or was
  contained.

The first statement does not prove the Succession. The third prevents proof
that it was impossible.

## From seed to Succession

Technical and popular accounts divide the terminal chain into four stages.

### The Taking

A viable catalytic seed is slowed within a sufficient column of ordinary matter
and begins incorporating nuclei. In neutron-rich degenerate matter the absence
of a nuclear Coulomb barrier makes growth easiest. In ordinary matter the
setting's postulated neutral or negative surface state is essential.

Conversion releases binding energy and heats the surrounding material. If the
seed is small or the material unconfined, the first reactions may blow away the
remaining feed and end the event. A Taking becomes self-sustaining only when the
rate of incorporation and daughter production outruns evaporation, mechanical
separation, and loss.

### The Flowering

The growing strange phase reaches its split size and divides. The daughters
enter fresh material, repeat the process, and convert a connected reservoir
through many fronts rather than one expanding surface. Heat, radiation,
neutrinos, pressure, and fragmentation make the process destructive even before
most of the mass changes phase.

In a planet, habitat, or ordinary star, expansion can disperse both converted
and unconverted matter. Dispersal is not reliable termination because it may
also export daughters. In a neutron star, extreme density supplies rapid
growth, while gravity confines much of the reaction long enough for a large
fraction of the star to convert. The stellar conversion may take milliseconds
for deconfinement and much longer for weak equilibration, depending upon the
equation of state and front structure. The Codex does not assign one universal
burn speed.

### The Succession

A converted reservoir exports viable daughter seeds to more than one other
reservoir on average. Directorate descendants expressed the threshold as

> `R_s = N_d f_v f_e P_c`,

where `N_d` is the number of daughters made available for export, `f_v` the
fraction that remain viable, `f_e` the fraction that escape the parent
reservoir, and `P_c` the probability that an escaping daughter is eventually
captured by another usable baryonic reservoir.

The factors are not universal constants. They depend upon the daughter spectrum,
charge, magnetic fields, intervening matter, stellar population, reservoir
density, and cosmic expansion. The equation is a branching account, not a new
force.

If `R_s < 1`, most lineages end and a regional catastrophe can remain finite.
If `R_s > 1` across a connected population of usable reservoirs, the number of
independently infected reservoirs can grow. The vast number of potential Apsis
lineages then matters: the probability that every supercritical lineage dies
can become negligibly small even when any one seed is unlikely to succeed.
Supercritical reproduction alone does not prove that every reservoir will be
visited. The accessible reservoir network must also percolate through the
future causal domain, and `R_s` must remain above one as the frontier enters
sparser or more rapidly separating regions.

The **Succession threshold** is crossed only when open-quench production, the
catalytic stability island, daughtering, escape, future capture, a percolating
reservoir network, and sustained supercritical reproduction all obtain. Under
those conditions, the Rehearsal produced enough independent seed trials that
conversion of all ordinary baryonic matter in the connected future causal
domain becomes effectively inevitable. No operator can reverse the sign of
`R_s` after the daughter population has left causal containment.

### The Settlement

The Houses' word **Settlement** names the asymptotic result: accessible ordinary
baryonic reservoirs are replaced by stable strange matter, much of it divided
into a preferred strangelet size distribution by the same daughtering
instability that spread the chain.

The term is often mistranslated as conversion of *the entire universe*. The
technical boundary is narrower and more terrible because it requires no new
cosmology. Strangelets travel on timelike or null-limited paths. They cross
vacuum only as particles. They must encounter baryons to grow. A permanently
horizon-separated galaxy cannot be reached. Photons, neutrinos, dark matter,
vacuum energy, empty space, and the spacetime metric do not become strangelets.
Black holes do not become strange matter, and a strangelet lost through a large
event horizon is removed from the accessible chain.

The Settlement is the conversion of **all causally reachable baryonic matter
that remains connected through a percolating reservoir network**, not the
replacement of existence by one substance.

This entry does not invoke vacuum decay. A vacuum transition would propagate by
a different mechanism and would not leave a universe made of strangelets.

## Propagation between reservoirs

Daughter strangelets do not form a luminous conversion wave across empty space.
They disperse as unusual cosmic rays and compact grains.

Charged daughters are deflected by magnetic fields. Their high mass-to-charge
ratio can give them unusual rigidity, but they still lose energy, fragment in
collisions, and may be excluded from some stellar environments. Neutral or
nearly neutral daughters travel more directly and are harder to detect, but
they receive less magnetic guidance into dense targets. A broad daughter
spectrum is more dangerous than one optimized form because different fragments
survive different routes.

Most particles miss compact objects. Most that strike an atmosphere or diffuse
cloud do not necessarily stop in enough matter to begin a Taking. The numbers
available after a Flowering compensate for small individual capture
probabilities. One converted stellar-mass reservoir contains roughly enough
baryons that even an exceedingly small exported fraction could represent an
immense daughter count. The reproduction threshold depends on the product, not
upon any one favorable factor.

Propagation therefore has no fixed blast radius and no single speed. Within a
dense body, conversion may be rapid. Between stars, the interval can be years,
millennia, or longer. Across an expanding universe, some destinations are lost
forever. The chain is causal at every step even when its long-term extinction
probability is negligible.

## Physical form and engineering lineage

The Apsis instrument descended from legitimate attempts to reconstruct early-
universe particle states. Its oldest verified ancestors were accelerator and
quench facilities concerned with the strong-interaction phase diagram, baryon
asymmetry, high-energy symmetry restoration, and the behavior of dense matter.
They neither approached the Apsis setpoint nor possessed a distributed
convergence crown.

Three developments changed the field.

First, opposed interplanetary acceleration stages allowed energy to be delivered
with a nearly stationary center of momentum at a remote focus. Second,
distributed reservoirs separated energy storage from final compression. Third,
fast kernel scheduling made it possible to repeat microscopic convergence
without intentionally combining the whole firing energy into one horizon.

The Apsis Directorate added the baryon-bias system and quench sleeve. These were
presented as scientific necessities: a radiation-dominated plasma did not test
the dense region of the quantum chromodynamic phase diagram. The same additions
made the experiment a strangelet-yield instrument.

Surviving images show no single machine recognizable as the Rehearsal. They show
widely separated reservoirs, acceleration arcs, clock stations, disposable
terminal emitters, empty trajectories, and a target installation that appears
small beside the range. The weapon existed in their synchronized relationship.

No terminal emitter was expected to survive reflected particles or target
debris. Reservoir isolation doors closed before the firing sequence completed.
Several did not reopen. The array was expensive not only because of the energy
released but because its last stages were consumable and its alignment could not
be rehearsed at full fidelity without performing the forbidden act.

## The Apsis Discharge

**Date:** Compact Year +271<br>
**Location:** The external, uninhabited Apsis Range<br>
**Operator:** Apsis Directorate<br>
**Evidentiary grade:** Grade A for the instrument, firing, duration, and prompt
destruction; Grade B for the reconstructed peak state and long-lived strangelet
yield; Grade E for catalytic escape<br>

The Apsis Discharge is the only accepted full-energy use of the instrument. The
range's remoteness and lack of permanent inhabitants were part of its safety
case. The case treated distance as containment without first demonstrating the
charge, lifetime, or stopping behavior of every possible product.

Three remote clocks agree that commanded convergence persisted for forty-seven
milliseconds. Their agreement is meaningful because the clocks had independent
power, control, and collection paths. The nearest surviving spectral instruments
saturated in the opening interval. More distant instruments recorded a sequence
of hard particle, gamma, and neutrino structures consistent with an unresolved
kernel train rather than one continuous explosion.

The prepared target and terminal crown disappeared inside an expanding pair and
hadron plasma. Several acceleration works were destroyed by return showers.
Others failed after their timing references were lost. The prompt event rendered
the central range unrecoverable without exposing a recovery mission to suspected
yield.

The Directorate first reported successful primordial-state reconstruction and
denied stable residue. Its internal traffic, recovered later, used the terms
**open fraction**, **heavy tail**, and **unclosed yield**. None proves catalysis.
Together they show that the public assurance exceeded the evidence.

Collection traps outside the central destruction recorded the strange-rich
tracks described above. An automated quarantine order then severed ordinary
traffic. No accepted record shows that every preprogrammed collector obeyed the
order, that every neutral trajectory crossed an instrumented surface, or that
all debris remained gravitationally associated with the range.

The Apsis Directorate did not conduct a second discharge. Whether it chose not
to, lost the ability, or was prevented remains disputed. Its surviving
authority and facilities were dissolved during the proceedings that produced
the Common Matter Interdict.

The discharge was awesome in the older sense of the word: it defeated the scale
by which witnesses ordinarily decide whether an event belongs to engineering or
astronomy. The later cults did not invent that reaction. They made it sacred.

## Target and material response

The Rehearsal does not have one target effect.

**Vacuum** permits clean convergence but supplies little net baryon number. A
vacuum shot can produce an extreme particle event and horizon-poisoned kernels
while yielding few surviving droplets. Vacuum is useful for characterization,
not for the terminal mechanism.

**A prepared baryon sleeve** supplies known geometry, neutron richness, and a
chosen cooling column. It gives the array its highest modeled control over the
open quench. Apsis used such a sleeve.

**Diffuse gas and habitat matter** are readily ionized and accelerated away.
They suffer catastrophic prompt radiation and ablation, but dispersal may starve
early seeds before daughtering. The same dispersal can carry surviving seeds
into separate structures.

**Planetary matter** supplies a deep baryonic column and gravity but has
heterogeneous composition, voids, phase boundaries, electromagnetic fields, and
rapidly changing pressure after the prompt blast. A planetary shot is easier to
feed and harder to model.

**White-dwarf and neutron-star matter** provide the most dangerous amplifiers.
Degenerate matter confines the conversion and, in neutron-rich regions, removes
the nuclear Coulomb barrier. A successful Taking can become a rapid internal
front. Whether the converted star ejects viable daughters, collapses into a
black hole, or retains most strange matter depends upon its mass, rotation,
equation of state, and the postulated daughtering behavior.

**An existing black hole** is a sink, not a convertible target. Directing the
focus near a horizon also makes timing and energy reconstruction worse. No
credible Apsis design uses a black hole as an amplifier.

Living tissue has no special status. It is baryonic matter with a low density,
high water fraction, and delicate chemistry. Organisms near a prompt discharge
die from ordinary radiation, heat, pressure, and particle injury long before a
distinction between ordinary and strange biological matter is useful.

## Operational doctrine

The complete firing sequence required six commitments.

1. **Range closure:** ordinary traffic, untrusted clocks, and uncontrolled mass
   were removed from projected packet paths.
2. **Reservoir release:** separated stores discharged toward final acceleration
   works. After this point, aborting one path could redirect rather than cancel
   the remaining energy.
3. **Crown acquisition:** independent stations solved the target ephemeris and
   brought packet momenta toward closure in the intended rest frame.
4. **Baryon bias:** neutron-rich packets and the prepared sleeve established the
   quench inventory. Its timing was separately interlocked because an unbiased
   convergence remained destructive but was less likely to make a terminal
   yield.
5. **Kernel train:** the scheduler distributed microscopic overlaps through the
   target envelope while attempting to keep each below the trapped-surface
   threshold.
6. **Blind quench:** the products expanded and cooled faster than any
   observation-and-abort loop. All later control consisted of quarantine,
   collection, and inference.

The engagement range was interplanetary, not interstellar. Longer baselines
increased emittance, background interaction, clock error, and uncertainty in
the target's future position. A local terminal crown could in principle be fed
by remote energy, but that arrangement would be a new instrument requiring its
own evidence.

The tiny kernel scale did not require the range to know one classical point to
thirty decimal places. The opposed packets were extended quantum ensembles. The
controller maximized the rate of interactions with the desired invariant energy
inside their overlap distribution. Most packet constituents did not participate
in a successful kernel. This inefficiency is included in the gross energy
ledger.

No doctrine could select the strangelet outcome directly. Operators could raise
the number of independent quench trials, alter baryon chemical potential, change
the sleeve, and bias the compactness distribution. They could not specify the
charge and lifetime of every droplet. Increasing total energy could even reduce
terminal yield by pushing more kernels into horizon poisoning.

## Energy, cost, and scale

The energy at the target did not appear freely. Even the conservative
per-kernel estimates require `10^11` to `10^13` joules in a correlation
volume. A full train therefore demanded strategic energy stores before
inefficiency, missed packets, acceleration losses, and sacrificial hardware were
counted.

The exact Apsis total remains sealed and disputed because the surviving gross
account is also a guide to reconstructing the instrument. All credible
reconstructions require infrastructure capable of releasing a meaningful
fraction of stellar-scale power for a fraction of a second, after accumulating
the energy over a much longer interval. The phrase does not mean the array
created the output of a star. It means its instantaneous power belonged in the
same comparison.

Energy storage had to remain distributed. Concentrating the reservoir would
have created ordinary structural, thermal, electromagnetic, and gravitational
hazards before firing. Each station paid its own waste-heat and recoil account.
Momentum-balanced packets minimized recoil at the target, not at the emitters.

The convergence crown demanded clocks, ranging, and beam characterization at
limits where a small systematic error changed the branch distribution. The
terminal works were exposed to their own return products. A Rehearsal was
therefore not a reusable gun with expensive ammunition. It was a prepared range
whose successful firing consumed much of the means needed to evaluate success.

## Detection and forensic evidence

Before firing, the instrument is easier to detect than to identify. Relevant
signatures include widely separated energy accumulation, opposed acceleration
works, unusually independent clock networks, sacrificial terminal geometry,
neutron-rich packet preparation, and a target sleeve positioned where no
ordinary experiment requires it.

No single signature is conclusive. High-energy astronomy, propulsion research,
and compact-matter science use related components. The forbidden configuration
is their integrated timing and baryon-loaded convergence solution.

During firing, observers may detect:

- counterpropagating high-energy precursor packets;
- a prompt hard gamma and lepton flash;
- hadronic jets and pair cascades from underconverged kernels;
- a neutrino-rich quench signal;
- rapidly varying spectral hardness consistent with mixed open and horizon
  branches; and
- the destruction or simultaneous isolation of terminal emitters.

No remote detector directly measures `10^30` kelvin. Investigators infer a
spectral-equivalent temperature from particle energies, relative populations,
timing, and the independently constrained delivered energy. A claim based only
on brightness is inadequate.

After firing, strangelet searches look for anomalously massive particles or
grains with low charge-to-mass ratio, unusual penetration and stopping profiles,
strange-rich decay products, and matter conversions whose energy and isotope
accounts do not fit ordinary nuclear reactions. Charged-particle traps are blind
to a neutral yield. A clean trap is evidence about its acceptance cone, not
about all space.

A genuine Taking would show a moving conversion interface, energy release per
incorporated baryon, persistent strange-rich material, and daughter products
whose total baryon account closes. An explosion alone proves nothing. So does
one anomalous isotope. The standard requires independent calorimetry, mass
accounting, and material collection because fear of the Rehearsal has made
ordinary industrial accidents easy to misclassify.

## Countermeasures

The reliable defenses act before open quench.

**Disrupt timing.** Independent perturbation of crown clocks or target ephemeris
can prevent invariant-energy closure. This turns a terminal convergence into
missed or underconverged packets, which remain strategically destructive.

**Separate one axis.** Removing a momentum-balancing packet gives the product a
large boost and shortens the time available for thermalization. The escaping
shower may strike another target. Counterfire doctrine therefore includes a
safe momentum corridor, not merely destruction of the nearest emitter.

**Interrupt baryon bias.** Deflecting the bias packet or removing the prepared
sleeve reduces net baryon chemical potential and strangelet yield. It does not
prevent the high-energy burst. Supplying an improvised matter screen is unsafe:
the screen can become an unintended quench sleeve.

**Force range motion.** The array commits to a future overlap distribution.
Moving a mobile target or terminal sleeve outside that distribution before the
last correction window can waste the convergence. Planets and stars cannot use
this defense.

**Destroy integration, not archives indiscriminately.** Reservoirs,
accelerators, and primordial-state research have legitimate uses. The Common
Matter regime instead separates the timing solution, baryon-bias authority, and
full-energy controls so that no one system can assemble all six functions.

After suspected yield escapes, no universal sterilization procedure is
certified. Magnetic and electrostatic traps collect charged fragments but not
neutral ones. Heating can evaporate some strangelets and cause others to divide.
Bombardment can reduce a seed below stability or multiply viable daughters.
Dense sacrificial absorbers improve stopping while also supplying feed.

The safest available handling doctrine is therefore mass-poor isolation:
maintain suspected particles in a low-density, remotely monitored volume;
prevent contact with large baryonic reservoirs; avoid energetic intervention
until charge and fragmentation response are bounded; and, if capture is
certain, dispose of the material into a sufficiently large existing black hole.
The last measure is a terminal sink for accessible material, not a practical
search method and not evidence that uncaptured seeds are absent.

A Taking inside a compact star offers almost no intervention time. Proposed
containment by forcing prompt collapse into a black hole risks ejecting daughter
matter during the attempt. It remains an emergency theory, not a demonstrated
countermeasure.

## Failure modes

### Packet walkoff

The opposed packets overlap imperfectly. Energy leaves as directional jets,
destroying an unintended location or an emitter. Strangelet yield may fall while
the conventional damage grows.

### Momentum-open focus

The energy intersects but retains too much net momentum. A hot boosted plume
crosses the sleeve rather than dwelling within it. Detectors can mistake the
hard spectrum for the desired temperature while the relevant rest-frame density
was never achieved.

### Baryon-starved quench

The peak state forms without sufficient net baryon excess. Pair annihilation
leaves a bright, nearly baryon-free event. This is a scientific failure and a
less terminal weapon, not a harmless one.

### Sleeve preignition

Early particles strike the prepared sleeve before the intended focus, creating
an asymmetric hadronic shower and moving the target inventory. Later packets
then converge upon matter whose position and density no longer match the timing
solution.

### Horizon poisoning

Too many kernels form trapped surfaces. The strangelet-forming branch is
suppressed, the hard evaporation component grows, and the final spectrum becomes
more dependent upon unresolved high-energy degrees of freedom.

### Kernel coalescence

Separate expansion wakes overlap before their energy and compactness fall far
enough. A larger trapped region, an uncontrolled jet, or a quench history outside
the modeled ensemble results. More commanded kernels can therefore produce fewer
independent open trials.

### False closure

Instrument agreement is mistaken for independent confirmation even though
several sensors share one timing solution or saturated calibration. The Apsis
temperature and kernel counts remain vulnerable to this error; their grades are
kept below A where appropriate.

### Yield multiplication during recovery

A collector stops a strangelet in dense shielding, or an assay fragments it
above the daughter stability threshold. The system intended to prove
containment becomes the first confirmed Taking.

### Successful operation

The most serious failure mode is not malfunction. The array can achieve its
specified open quench, produce the intended broad strangelet distribution, and
thereby discover that the catalytic island exists. In that case, technical
success and universal baryonic loss are the same event viewed at different
times.

## Lesser-known and disputed properties

### Did Apsis cross the Succession threshold?

No accepted evidence demonstrates a second, causally separated converted
reservoir. No accepted survey excludes an undetected neutral seed on every
outbound trajectory. Apsis crossing remains Grade E.

The claim cannot be upgraded merely because enough time has passed without a
visible conversion. Interstellar capture times are long and conversion light is
not an advance warning. It also cannot be treated as true merely because no
complete containment proof exists. Absence of closure and evidence of escape
are different claims.

### Was the horizon branch engineered as a safety valve?

Some Directorate documents describe overfocus as **sterilizing closure**. Their
own models show only reduced open-quench yield, not zero yield. A mixed train can
contain both branches, and evaporation returns energy to the surrounding
plasma. The safety-valve interpretation is Grade D and technically incomplete.

### Could ordinary astrophysics already have made the same seeds?

Cosmic rays, stellar collapse, compact-star mergers, and the early universe
reach conditions favorable to quark matter in some models. Their continued
existence is strong evidence against easy production of universally dangerous
strangelets. The Directorate argued that Apsis differed by combining extreme
center-of-momentum energy, low net momentum, high baryon chemical potential,
rapid repeated quench paths, and a prepared sleeve. That combination is
physically meaningful. Whether it opens a unique stability island is Grade B as
a theoretical possibility and unverified as fact.

### Could a stable seed wait indefinitely?

If the strange phase is absolutely stable at zero pressure, radioactive decay
does not provide a deadline. Mechanical fragmentation, cosmic-ray collision,
capture by a black hole, and failure to encounter matter still can end a
lineage. Houses that call every delay proof of an approaching Settlement turn an
unfalsified timescale into doctrine.

### Could the array ignite vacuum decay?

The Apsis record contains no evidence of a vacuum transition. The continued
existence of the observed region is direct evidence that the discharge did not
launch a near-light vacuum bubble there. This entry excludes vacuum decay from
the Rehearsal mechanism. Adding it would replace the strangelet chain with a
different terminal phenomenon.

## Law, ethics, and prohibition

The Common Matter Interdict does not prohibit temperature, cosmology, particle
physics, compact-matter research, or every accelerator above a named energy. Its
central legal object is **integrated terminal capacity**: the ability to combine
near-Planck invariant-energy convergence, deliberate baryon bias, an open-quench
geometry, and repeated trials beyond a containment proof.

The Interdict prohibits:

- construction or coordinated possession of a complete convergence crown;
- full-energy opposed tests with a baryonic target or sleeve;
- retention of an executable kernel schedule and target solution in one
  authority;
- creation of a yield whose neutral fraction cannot be bounded;
- transport of suspected yield toward a planetary, stellar, or degenerate
  baryonic reservoir;
- destructive assay of suspected strange matter without daughter accounting;
  and
- claims of complete containment based only on charged-particle collection.

Lawful high-energy research must preserve independent separations among peak
invariant energy, baryon chemical potential, repetition count, and stopping
mass. Abort authority must exist outside the institution that benefits from a
successful shot. Research records are distributed so that defensive analysis
can continue without leaving one archive sufficient to reconstruct the Apsis
timing solution.

The deepest ethical dispute concerns experiments meant to prove that the
catalytic island does not exist. No finite set of negative trials excludes an
unknown stability tail. A positive trial may be the event the research was
intended to prevent. The Interdict therefore treats certain knowledge as
unobtainable by direct test within an inhabited causal domain.

Possession of cultic fragments is not itself prohibited. Possession becomes an
offense when fragments are assembled into timing, target, and baryon-bias
information sufficient to restore terminal capacity. This line is difficult to
enforce because some Houses preserve authentic scientific records more carefully
than lawful archives do.

## Cultural afterlife

Small cults dedicated to the Rehearsal survive under the collective name
**Houses of the Lower State**. They disagree about doctrine, history, and action,
but share one proposition: ordinary nuclear matter is a long-lived excited state
and the strange phase is the material toward which creation properly settles.

They call the weapon **the Proof**, the open quench **the Opening**, the first
successful capture **the Taking**, reproductive conversion **the Flowering**,
interstellar spread **the Succession**, and the final baryonic condition **the
Settlement**. The most eschatological Houses call the whole process **the
Performance**, making the Rehearsal's name a promise rather than a warning.

The Houses are not one conspiracy.

**Waiting Houses** collect observations, preserve fragments of Apsis telemetry,
and refuse intervention. They argue that a true lower state requires no help and
that attempting to hasten it expresses doubt.

**Custodial Houses** seek suspected yield in order to guard it from both
destruction and use. Their containment practices range from technically careful
mass-poor isolation to ceremonial vessels made of dangerously dense material.

**Open Yield Houses** seek a stable seed or the means to repeat the discharge.
They are few, fragmented, and responsible for most enforcement directed at the
larger tradition. Their doctrine treats every ordinary body as matter wrongfully
withheld from its ground state.

The forty-seven milliseconds of Apsis have become a ritual interval. Some Houses
keep forty-seven silent beats. Others recite the Planck temperature and omit two
decimal places to signify the descent by two orders of magnitude. Technical
errors in these observances do not make the devotion harmless; authentic
timing fragments have been hidden inside liturgical counts.

The Houses' best-known sentence is:

> *The universe is not ending. It is settling.*

Their most feared prayer is not a request that the weapon be fired:

> *May it have fired already, far enough away that its light has not yet
> arrived.*

Outside the Houses, **Apsis certainty** means confidence created by an experiment
that destroyed the instruments needed to measure its most important result.
Calling a political program **the Proof** accuses it of treating irreversible
consequence as evidence of resolve.

## Six-system mechanism firewall

The Rehearsal adds a sixth engineering crisis to the current landscape. It does
not reveal a common source behind the other five.

- **The Rzez** invalidates selected structural relationships. The Rehearsal
  destroys and converts through delivered energy, particle interactions, and a
  material seed. Its damage is not entropic relationship failure.
- **The Unhidden** directs living growth and functional assignment. Strangelet
  incorporation is not morphogenesis, does not preserve an organismal plan, and
  has no privileged relationship to life.
- **The Manifold** prevents local spaces and histories from composing one global
  account. Rehearsal packets travel through ordinary spacetime and every stage
  remains causally ordered, even when records are incomplete.
- **The Closed Night** controls radiative transport across an enclosure. The
  Rehearsal retains no radiation and requires no one-way boundary. Its prompt
  energy escapes all too readily.
- **The Agreement** couples active prediction and correction. A strangelet does
  not persuade, coordinate, or align its targets. Similar converted matter is a
  shared phase, not shared cognition.
- **The Rehearsal** concentrates ordinary mass-energy into near-Planck kernels,
  biases their baryon content, and depends upon a speculative quench product
  that spreads only through material contact and daughter transport.

Shared words such as *conversion*, *continuity*, *closure*, *settlement*,
*inheritance*, and *state* establish no shared physics. No interaction between
the Rehearsal and another forbidden system is canon merely because both defeat
ordinary containment.

The Rehearsal creates no energy, matter, hidden interior, topological shortcut,
collective mind, or living plan. Its one explicit fictional addition is the
catalytic, daughtering stability island. Everything that follows is constrained
by that postulate, conservation, and causal propagation.

## Engineering interpretation

The Rehearsal is often described as a weapon of temperature. That description
mistakes its spectacular intermediate state for its governing principle.

Its real technology was the conversion of one impossible experiment into many
microscopic trials, followed by the use of quantity to search an unknown space
of outcomes. Distributed reservoirs solved the energy problem. Opposed packets
solved the momentum problem. The kernel train worked around self-gravity. The
baryon bias made residue possible. None solved the knowledge problem.

The instrument could choose a distribution of beginnings. It could not choose
which ending the distribution contained.

That is why its most important threshold is not the Planck temperature, the
horizon radius, or the strangelet's minimum mass. It is the point at which an
experiment makes enough independent descendants that ignorance itself becomes
an irreversible actuator.

The Rehearsal lasted forty-seven milliseconds.

If the Succession began, the firing has not ended.
