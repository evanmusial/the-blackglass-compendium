# Editorial Voice and Method

The Blackglass recension speaks in a restrained technical-historical voice,
institutional without being fully official and learned without being omniscient.
It describes horror precisely, takes engineering consequences seriously, and
keeps skepticism from making folklore worthless. People, places, institutions,
and eras receive brief clarification so that an account can be read outside
its original archive.

Its evidence grades attach to claims and incidents. An event may be accepted
while its explanation remains disputed; a weakly supported claim may justify
extreme precautions without becoming fact. Technical, legal, religious,
cultural, and survivor accounts coexist, with the dominant explanation and its
reasons stated beside the unresolved evidence supporting minority theories.

The prose follows mechanism through propagation, material response, detection,
countermeasures, failure, and consequences for law, architecture, medicine,
ritual, language, trauma, fraud, and scholarship. Quotations preserve evidence
and memory with their authenticity assessed. An unresolved operator, origin,
or date does not replace the material history or the reason inference stops.

Original technical records, Compact summaries, Blackglass annotations,
translator notes, censor omissions, and recovered primary fragments form
bounded editorial layers. Their margins preserve witness relations, translation
choices, continuing restrictions, and dissent. The Prior Hands remain collective
and anonymous.

The later [civil record](civil-records.md) follows service papers, local
settlements, and procedural revisions through their consequences. Technical
findings, care decisions, and compensation retain distinct sources and limits.
Short working extracts appear beside a complete account of what happened and
what followed. The attributed later notes of Maret Sen supply one bounded
professional voice whose judgments can be corrected by the people affected.

The complete authorial guidance, including every voice constraint and rule for
editorial layers, is preserved in the
[editorial companion](../docs/editorial-notes/frame-editorial-voice.md).
This frame note describes the apparent practice; that companion retains the
full writing guidance.
