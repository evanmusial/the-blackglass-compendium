# Collection Frame

This directory contains **meta-lore**: the in-world history and identity of the
collection the reader appears to possess.

It answers questions such as:

- Is *The Blackglass Compendium* the same work as *The Codex of Arcane
  Engineering*?
- Who compiled, revised, censored, copied, or leaked it?
- Why does a collection of prohibited knowledge exist?
- Which sources can its editors access?
- Is the reader seeing an official edition, illicit recension, reconstruction,
  or composite?
- Why does the editorial voice explain some names while leaving other matters
  unresolved?

## Boundary with other directories

- `canon/` contains the worlds, people, technologies, laws, and history described
  by the collection.
- `frame/` contains the collection's own place within that history.
- `docs/` contains real-world instructions for maintaining this repository.
- `entries/` contains the actual entry manuscripts.

Do not place real repository procedure into the fictional frame. Do not place a
story about the compilers into operational documentation.

## Frame-change rule

Update this directory when an entry introduces:

- a compiler, redactor, translator, censor, or archive;
- a new edition or layer of annotation;
- a change in what the reader is presumed to possess;
- an explanation for missing, forbidden, or contradictory material;
- a new title for the corpus;
- or evidence that changes when or why the collection was assembled.

New frame material begins as provisional. The established Blackglass tradition
may be extended without resolving the identity of the Prior Hands, the cause of
the scattered leaves, the Narrow Proof, the reader's specific medium, or whether
the collection is responsive.
