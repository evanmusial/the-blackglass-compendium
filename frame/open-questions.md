# Open Collection-Frame Questions

These questions belong to the collection frame and should not be answered
incidentally while designing an unrelated weapon.

## Identity

- Is “Blackglass” ultimately a material description, a censorship metaphor, a
  philosophical metaphor, or a convergence of all three?
- Is the Blackglass Compendium one physical edition, a textual lineage, or the
  later name for every unauthorized Codex copy?
- Does the official Codex still exist in the current era?

## Compilation

- Who first assembled the Blackglass recension?
- Was it created inside the Compact Commission, stolen from it, or reconstructed
  independently?
- Are the present editors one group or a chain of editors across generations?
- How much material was removed because it was dangerous rather than false?

## Reader

- Is the reader an investigator, scholar, criminal, survivor, official, or an
  unspecified person outside the fiction?
- Is possession of the represented edition illegal?
- Is the collection encountered as a book, distributed archive, recovered data,
  blackglass object, or something else?

## Completeness and order

- Why does the surviving sequence begin where it does?
- Are missing books destroyed, withheld, untranslated, or simply not part of
  this recension?
- What does the `A` prefix in `A-7.01` encode?
- Does the current order reproduce the official Codex or a Blackglass editorial
  reconstruction?

## Reliability

- Have official sources deliberately planted false entries?
- Can the collection itself be contaminated by a technology it describes?
- Do later editors know that some source traditions are manipulated?
- What would count as evidence that the frame, rather than one entry, is lying?
