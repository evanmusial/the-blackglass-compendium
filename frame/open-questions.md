# Open Collection-Frame Questions

These questions belong to the collection frame and should not be answered
incidentally while designing an unrelated weapon.

## Identity

- Which of the material, censorship, and mirror meanings first produced the
  name, and did the meanings converge before the current recension?
- Does the official Codex still exist in the current era?
- Has any physically or informationally complete Blackglass recension ever
  existed?

## Compilation

- Who assembled the current Blackglass filing layer, as distinct from the much
  older textual tradition?
- Was that filing layer created inside the Compact Commission, stolen from it,
  or reconstructed independently?
- Are the present editors one group, a chain of editors across generations, or
  a later synthesis of several chains?
- How much material was removed because it was dangerous rather than false?
- Who or what were the Prior Hands, and why were their names removed before the
  earliest surviving witness families diverged?

## Reader

- Is the reader an investigator, scholar, criminal, survivor, official, or an
  unspecified person outside the fiction?
- Is possession of the represented edition illegal?
- In what medium does this particular reader encounter a lineage known to exist
  as books, distributed archives, recovered data, carvings, and isolated leaves?

## Completeness and order

- Why does the surviving sequence begin where it does?
- Which missing books were destroyed, withheld, untranslated, never recovered,
  or never part of the same historical corpus?
- What does the `A` prefix in `A-7.01` encode?
- Does the current order reproduce the official Codex or a Blackglass editorial
  reconstruction?

## Reliability

- Have official sources deliberately planted false entries?
- Can the collection itself be contaminated by a technology it describes?
- Do later editors know that some source traditions are manipulated?
- What would count as evidence that the frame, rather than one entry, is lying?

## Distribution and response

- Why do Blackglass fragments tend to appear in media plausible for their local
  technological environment?
- Which pages found in open space are ordinary debris, planted relics, or
  genuinely without a recoverable parent object?
- Are landscape correspondences natural analogues, ancient engineering,
  category errors, or a form of record not yet understood?
- Did remote dark-mirror traditions spread through lost contact, arise through
  recurrent encounters, or preserve something older than present routes?
- Does trouble follow Blackglass, does Blackglass follow trouble, or do both
  follow a third cause?
- Was the Narrow Proof an empty boast, a removed philological argument, or a
  dangerous completion whose absence is itself containment?
