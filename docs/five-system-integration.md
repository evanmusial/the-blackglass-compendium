# Five-System Integration Record

## Purpose

This record documents the direct adoption of The Closed Night and The Agreement
and their integration with the Rzez, The Unhidden, The Manifold, and the
Kelan–Ruva setting. It records repository and authorial decisions rather than an
in-world editorial layer.

The earlier [`three-weapon integration record`](three-weapon-integration.md)
remains the historical ledger for the provisional Unhidden and Manifold spine.
Nothing in this adoption promotes either developing concept to entry status.

## Subsequent canon expansion

This record preserves the boundaries in force when The Closed Night and The
Agreement were adopted. A later explicit authorial decision, recorded in the
[`Kelan–Ruva natural and human history integration`](kelan-ruva-natural-and-human-history.md),
supersedes only its former human-origin reservation: humanity is now established
as Earth-originating, and Avara is established as the oldest continuously
occupied human refuge in Kelan–Ruva. The numbered Earth year, migration motive,
transit method, and Earth's fate remain unresolved. No mechanism firewall or
weapon-specific boundary in this record changes.

The new identifiers establish the current Blackglass filing rule: the entry-ID
prefix is the alphabetic volume code, and the number before the decimal is the
book number. This resolves the earlier uncertainty around the `A` in `A-7.01`
for the represented recension without claiming every historical Codex edition
used the same filing system.

Entry metadata now records `provenance_classification` separately from original
purpose and manuscript source. The Rzez, The Closed Night, and The Agreement are
`known-human-lineage`. The existing Manifold reservation remains
`extrinsic-unknown` and does not become entry metadata until that concept is
promoted.

## Canonical core of The Closed Night

- Human radiant-return engineering first reduced heat loss in deep-space civil
  and industrial systems.
- A weaponized enclosure uses powered, physically established anchors to make
  electromagnetic transmission direction-selective across finite bands.
- Radiation denied egress is returned to or retained within the target. Energy
  is neither erased nor created.
- Light, radio, and thermal bands require actual spectral coverage. An
  incomplete shell leaks; a failing aperture can become a dangerous white seam.
- Matter can cross the boundary. Evacuation, ejected hot mass, conductive
  tethers, and physical destruction of anchors are real countermeasures.
- Industrial, siege, and strategic forms require different architectures. No
  casual planetary enclosure exists.
- The Articles of Open Radiance separately govern projected closure, thermal
  egress, anchor possession, and rescue authority.

## Canonical core of The Agreement

- Human cooperative-control engineering first redistributed prediction error
  among small rescue and industrial teams using consensual, bounded,
  low-latency reciprocal links.
- Weaponized lattices make independent sensor, motor, and controller responses
  increasingly costly until coupled people and machines converge.
- Victims remain separate conscious persons. The system creates neither
  telepathy nor a merged mind, and it cannot upload an arbitrary belief, skill,
  memory, or command.
- Establishment requires sustained two-way coupling. Recordings and reports are
  inert, and ordinary interstellar latency prevents a regional hive.
- Truly isolated instruments do not converge spontaneously. The forensic sign
  is impossible agreement among systems sharing a concealed active carrier.
- Hard separation, one-way channels, asynchronous clocks, controlled random
  stimuli, and physically uncoupled variance keepers resist establishment.
- Crew, local, industrial, tactical, and strategic forms require different
  coupling and carrier architectures.
- The Articles of Bodily Dissent separately govern involuntary coupling,
  contaminated unanimous evidence, protected dissent, and rehabilitation.

## Five-way mechanism firewall

- The Rzez invalidates relationships by which existing matter remains
  structurally continuous.
- The Unhidden directs the finite growth and functional assignment of living
  matter.
- The Manifold prevents locally exact spaces and histories from composing one
  global account.
- The Closed Night controls radiative transport across an ordinary-space,
  energy-accountable enclosure.
- The Agreement couples active prediction and error-correction loops until
  independence becomes unstable.

The Closed Night is not an entropic sink or a Manifold boundary. The Agreement
does not attack cohesion, create a distributed organism, or make incompatible
histories true. Similar words such as *boundary*, *agreement*, *continuity*,
*convergence*, and *independence* establish no common origin or physics.

No cross-system interaction is promoted by this record.

## Shared history and geography

The Agreement's civil lineage acquires a fixed Kelan–Ruva anchor in the
Three-Hand Rescue in Avaran orbit in Compact Year +164. Kharos later becomes the main
public venue for autonomy law, contaminated unanimous testimony, protected
dissent, and discord rehabilitation. Odran's independently operated standards
and the Kelan–Ruva Concordance Service supply defensive practice without gaining
jurisdiction over the weapon.

The Closed Night's civil lineage belongs to deep-space heat-recovery practice
and does not require a new inhabited system. Its defining Ninth Holding record
retains the redactions established by the entry. Kharos receives legal and
rescue consequences; Avara's managed environment makes thermal egress a civil
right with unusually visible stakes; Odran supplies calorimetric standards and
safe rescue training. No occupied, hostile, or full military enclosure trial is
placed on Odran.

These allocations do not make Kelan–Ruva the origin, battlefield, regulator,
and archive of every subject. No confirmed Manifold rupture, planetary Closed
Night, or interstellar Agreement is placed there.

## Separate legal regimes

The Sixth Compact remains Rzez-specific. The Articles of Bodily Dissent, the
Articles of Open Radiance, created-person law, and Manifold quarantine may borrow
procedure from one another, but none inherits another regime's jurisdiction by
analogy.

The adopted entries require authorities to distinguish:

- structural contamination from hidden reciprocal coupling;
- a conscious manufactured body from coerced convergence among existing people;
- contradictory authentic evidence from suspiciously identical evidence;
- ordinary communications blackout from a radiative enclosure;
- and heat retained by known transport physics from energy that has vanished.

## Retained boundaries

- The Unhidden and The Manifold remain developing concepts with unassigned
  permanent IDs and volumes.
- `phenomenon-complex` remains reserved rather than entering the schema early.
- At the time of this adoption, no new celestial system, route mechanism, human
  origin, or nonhuman civilization was established. The later Earth-origin
  decision is recorded in the subsequent-expansion note above.
- No planetary Closed Night deployment is accepted. The entry-local inverse-shell
  allegation remains explicitly Grade E.
- The Agreement does not spread through prose, recordings, memory, or ordinary
  remote communication.
- The Closed Night does not prevent physical transit, erase stored energy, or
  create a free energy source.
- The Agreement does not remove private consciousness merely because behavior
  and testimony converge.
- Neither system is automatically subject to the Sixth Compact or governed by
  the Compact Commission.
