# Known-World Physics and Expansion Integration Record

## Purpose

This record documents the author's direct adoption of:

- an established physical baseline for the known world;
- ordinary energy, propulsion, gravity, computation, materials, sensing,
  habitation, medicine, and longevity;
- reciprocal ligature transit and its causal boundary;
- the exploration sequence required to build a new shore;
- route capacity, counterweight, finance, trade, blockade, and evacuation;
- and the demographic, ecological, industrial, and political thresholds of human
  settlement.

The canonical material belongs in:

- [`canon/physics-of-the-known-world.md`](../canon/physics-of-the-known-world.md);
  and
- [`canon/spaceflight-economy-and-settlement.md`](../canon/spaceflight-economy-and-settlement.md).

chosen, how it relates to real 2026 science, which older deferrals it supersedes,
and what remains for a later entry-by-entry audit.

## The creative center: ligature transit

The earlier setting correctly required a sparse route network, days- or weeks-long
journeys, infrastructure, blockade, quarantine, loss, and no automatic
instantaneous communication. It intentionally left the mechanism undesigned.

The adopted mechanism is **reciprocal locality exchange**:

- rare pairs of stellar gravitational basins possess a natural two-site locality
  mode;
- locality occupancy is superselected: the usable sector contains one carriage
  support at each shore and only the paired assignment may exchange;
- endpoint weighhouses adiabatically exchange the locality support of two closed,
  matched carriages;
- there is no traversable middle, hidden dimension, or shipboard route drive;
- internal matter evolves continuously and is neither scanned nor copied;
- passage requires equal mass-energy occupancy at both shores;
- endpoint machinery pays local momentum and orbital reconciliation;
- the route's coupling gap imposes days or weeks of transit and cannot be made
  arbitrarily faster by adding power;
- the sequence field imposes one global causal order and prevents closed causal
  loops;
- and useful records cross in carriages, while a reserved empty cycle can expose
  only one delayed interlock outcome at full route depth.

This choice makes the economics inseparable from the physics. Ballast is route
capacity. Every arrival requires a departure. Refugee evacuation needs equal
counterweight at the receiving shore. A route company can own the scales without
owning spacetime.

## Why this is not a wormhole or transporter

No carriage follows a tunnel, crosses an intermediate coordinate, or emerges from
a reconstructed blueprint. The state remains one physical system with one
internal causal history while its macroscopic locality support changes
adiabatically.

The known real-physics analogy is controlled state transfer through an avoided
crossing, not a claim that present quantum systems permit interstellar exchange.
Macroscopic locality binding, usable stellar conjugacy, and the sequence field are
explicit fiction.

The setting accepts continuity through a successful crossing because:

- no destructive scan occurs;
- no destination copy is assembled;
- no abandoned original remains;
- internal proper time is continuous;
- and no successful passage produces two claimants.

Philosophical and religious disagreement may remain without changing the
authorial physical decision.

## Causality and communication decision

Ligature exchange is spacelike relative to ordinary metric distance. This is an
explicit fictional departure from the local causal structure known in 2026.

The sequence field prevents the ordinary faster-than-light closed-loop problem:
every viable worldline and exchange advances one physical ordering scalar. A
schedule that would close a causal loop loses the bound route state.

The setting therefore permits **carriage-borne faster-than-light displacement**
without permitting:

- instantaneous communication;
- an unscheduled signal sent through an idle route;
- a safe or high-bandwidth mid-transit update or recall;
- a live remote handshake;
- a message arriving before its sequence cause;
- or an Agreement loop spanning systems.

A letter may outrun ordinary light because the data store itself rides a matched
carriage. A scheduled participation-versus-refusal choice can communicate one
interlock bit per full mode cycle, but that low-rate channel cannot outrun the
safest certified carriage on the ligature or produce a live network.

## Expansion decision

The far weighhouse requirement creates the setting's expansion rate. An already
accessible system can build a cross-link, and surviving infrastructure can be
reopened. The slow sequence below governs a frontier shore with no other access.

Opening a new ligature normally requires:

1. remote astronomical and locality survey;
2. launch of an uncrewed receiver seed;
3. decades or centuries of ordinary sublight flight;
4. destination capture and shore search;
5. ten to forty years of industrial bootstrap;
6. a first Answer exchange;
7. three independent reciprocal proofs;
8. rescue, quarantine, ballast, and traffic infrastructure;
9. provisional crewed opening; and
10. generations of material, living, and civic foundation.

This is the principal reason the known region remains small. A measured affinity
does not make a new world cheap, immediate, habitable, or politically available.

The process intentionally leaves the Founding transit unresolved. Current route
physics neither proves nor disproves that Earth's carriers used a related method.
No modern shore reaches the Solar System.

## Economic decision

Interstellar trade balances in mass rather than value.

Every crossing needs a matched countercarriage. Water, regolith, slag, shielding,
and certified scrap can therefore have route value even when their commodity
value is low. A shore without ballast cannot accept relief or evacuate residents.

The route cost stack includes:

- surface lift and local transfer;
- weight-class capacity;
- counterweight;
- route mode occupancy;
- heat and internal-motion burden;
- endpoint work and momentum reconciliation;
- life support;
- rescue and quarantine;
- insurance and political risk;
- and onward transport after arrival.

This produces several established consequences:

- interstellar bulk water, grain, and common metal normally indicate emergency,
  dependency, or luxury;
- high-value physical goods retain importance because a design is not a trusted
  factory;
- calibration standards, clean lineages, and independent evidence are valuable
  exports;
- physical counterflow permits both legitimate backhaul and abusive waste
  colonialism;
- route finance clears by delayed authenticated receipt rather than one real-time
  regional market;
- and the two exchanged carriages are literal physical counterparties whose
  failures and hazardous manifests can injure one another.

## Settlement decision

The adopted model rejects the lone ark and the instant self-sufficient colony.

A permanent foundation requires independent power, pressure districts, food and
ecological pathways, medicine, reproductive diversity, machine tools, industrial
feedstock, schools, legal records, local transport, route repair, and resident
authority. A serious program moves hundreds of thousands of tonnes over decades.

Three ledgers determine survival:

1. **Material:** energy, heat, water, air, food, fabrication, transport, and
   replacement tools.
2. **Living:** human diversity, crops, microbes, commensals, decomposers,
   nutrients, and recovery after biological loss.
3. **Civic:** skills, childhood, law, archives, consent, dissent, and institutions
   capable of governing life support.

The authoring thresholds for expedition, dependent settlement, continuant
settlement, rooted polity, and mature system are ranges rather than universal
laws. Their purpose is to prevent a small outpost from becoming an inhabited
world by assertion.

## 2026 real-physics boundary

The baseline draws upon the following real footholds.

### Gravity and causality

- The Particle Data Group's [2026 gravity review](https://pdg.lbl.gov/2026/reviews/rpp2026-rev-gravity-tests.pdf)
  summarizes experimental tests of general relativity. An APS
  [2026 quantum-gravity overview](https://physics.aps.org/articles/v19/18)
  describes the continuing absence of experimental confirmation for a
  quantum-gravity account.
- The multimessenger observation of
  [GW170817 and GRB 170817A](https://doi.org/10.3847/2041-8213/aa920c)
  constrains ordinary gravitational and electromagnetic propagation to extremely
  similar speeds.

No real result establishes locality exchange, a sequence field, macroscopic
faster-than-light transport, gravity shielding, or inertial cancellation.

### Dark sectors and vacuum

- The Particle Data Group's [2026 dark-matter review](https://pdg.lbl.gov/2026/reviews/rpp2026-rev-dark-matter.pdf)
  records strong gravitational evidence and an unresolved fundamental identity.
- The [2026 dark-energy review](https://pdg.lbl.gov/2026/reviews/rpp2026-rev-dark-energy.pdf)
  preserves the distinction between observed cosmic acceleration and a locally
  engineerable substance.
- The [dynamical Casimir experiment](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature10561)
  demonstrates vacuum-related photon production driven by external work, not
  extraction of free vacuum energy.

The known world may measure more dark-sector structure than present science. It
does not use dark matter, dark energy, or vacuum energy as a free industrial
resource.

### Nonreciprocity, tissue engineering, and neural interfaces

- Real nonreciprocal devices use magnetic, temporal, motional, nonlinear, or
  pumped bias. A
  [2026 integrated isolator](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41566-026-01978-0)
  demonstrates the photonic foothold, while the
  [dynamic-reciprocity limit](https://www.nature.com/articles/nphoton.2015.79)
  illustrates why asymmetric transmission is not automatically a universal
  isolator. Neither establishes an inhabited free-space radiant-return cage.
- The US National Institute of Biomedical Imaging and Bioengineering's
  [tissue-engineering fact sheet](https://www.nibib.nih.gov/sites/default/files/2022-04/Fact-Sheet-Tissue-Engineering.pdf)
  documents the real scaffold and regenerative foothold below total-boundary
  morphogenesis.
- A 2026 [bidirectional hand neuroprosthesis](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-026-04498-0)
  demonstrates limited decoded control and returned sensation below Agreement
  coupling.
- A 2024 [adaptive deep-brain stimulation trial](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-024-03196-z)
  provides a real closed-loop foothold in which measured neural state changes the
  stimulation returned to the patient.

Ordinary known-world photonics, medicine, and neural engineering are mature
extensions of these fields. The forbidden departures remain entry-specific.

### Strange matter

- Witten's [cosmic separation of phases](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.30.272)
  and Farhi and Jaffe's
  [strange-matter analysis](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.30.2379)
  provide the real theoretical foothold.

No cited work establishes dangerous stable strangelets, catalytic conversion, or
reproductive daughtering. Those remain the explicit fictional postulate of The
Rehearsal, not ordinary known-world materials science.
