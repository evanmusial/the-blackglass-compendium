The following are established authorial canon:

- the Apsis Directorate is a human scientific-military lineage that converts
  primordial-state reconstruction into a weapon;
- the complete crown fires once at Apsis Range in Compact Year +271;
- the Apsis Discharge lasts 47 milliseconds and produces a sequence of
  microscopic near-Planck reheating kernels;
- the baryon-rich quench produces a large but unknowable quantity of strangelets;
- at least some possible strangelet states can, under the specified fictional
  conditions, catalyze ordinary baryonic conversion and produce viable daughters;
- a Succession becomes operationally irreversible across accessible ordinary
  baryonic reservoirs only if viable daughters sustain `R_s > 1` and their
  capture paths form a percolating network through those reservoirs; and
- small Houses of the Lower State continue to worship the instrument and its
  demonstrated power.

The firing, convergent energy, 47-millisecond train, terminal destruction, and
numerous massive strange-rich tracks are Grade A in-world. Identifying a large

## Temperature scale and what is projected

The 2022 CODATA value of the Planck temperature is approximately
`1.4168 × 10^32 K`. Two orders of magnitude below it is therefore approximately
`1.4 × 10^30 K`. The pitch's temperature range is usable when stated as an
**effective focal temperature near `10^30 K`**, not as a macroscopic target
temperature or a substance emitted downrange.

At `10^30 K`, `k_B T` is approximately `1.38 × 10^7 J`, or
`8.6 × 10^16 GeV`. At `1.4 × 10^30 K`, it is approximately
`1.2 × 10^17 GeV`, about one hundredth of the Planck energy. Those comparisons
show why ordinary Standard Model extrapolation is inadequate without claiming
that the Planck temperature is a proven hard maximum.

The crown projects separately transported particles and radiation. Temperature
appears only when counterstreams meet, interact, and thermalize at the remote
focus. The paths and projectors are not at `10^30 K`. The Apsis value is inferred
from the outgoing spectrum and the modeled degrees of freedom of each kernel.
Near the Planck regime, even the meaning of equilibrium temperature becomes
model-dependent, so the entry must retain **effective** and **reconstructed**
whenever it reports the value.

## Energy density, kernel size, and self-gravity

A photon-only blackbody estimate at the adopted setpoint
`T_R = 1.4168 × 10^30 K` gives

`u_gamma = aT_R^4 ≈ 3.05 × 10^105 J m^-3`.

Extrapolating the known high-temperature Standard Model particle inventory as an
ideal relativistic plasma instead gives approximately
`u_SM = 1.63 × 10^107 J m^-3`. The difference is a model bracket, not a
measurement. Unknown high-energy degrees of freedom, interactions, chemical
potential, nonequilibrium behavior, and geometry can all move it. Both particle
physics and gravity are being extrapolated far beyond tested conditions.

For a spherical region of energy density `u`, setting its physical radius equal
to its classical Schwarzschild radius gives the heuristic

`r_H ≈ [3c^4 / (8πGu)]^1/2`.

Under the Standard Model extrapolation, the formal crossing occurs at
`r_H ≈ 9.42 × 10^-33 m`, enclosing approximately `5.70 × 10^11 J`, with
a light-crossing time of approximately `3.14 × 10^-41 s`. Under the
photon-only model, it moves to `r_H ≈ 6.88 × 10^-32 m` and approximately
`4.17 × 10^12 J`. Classical general relativity and an ideal equilibrium plasma
are not reliable enough there to make either value a sharp switch. The
photon-only crossing is a lower-particle-count bracket, not permission to operate
a viable `10^-31 m` kernel; such a kernel is already deeply trapped under the
Standard Model extrapolation.

The authoring range is therefore:

- individual open-quench kernels span from a few `10^-33 m` to order `10^-32 m`,
  with no more precise viable radius justified between the model brackets;
- each persists for order `10^-41 s`, at most a few `10^-41 s` in the adopted
  reconstruction, before dispersal, collapse, or quench;
- the 47-millisecond firing is a timed sequence of kernels, not one hot region
  held open for 47 milliseconds; and
- increasing input does not supply an unlimited temperature dial. It increases
  the probability of premature projector destruction, missed thermalization, or
  trapped-surface formation.

These are deliberate science-fiction authoring ranges, not experimentally
validated predictions. No total stored energy, kernel count, firing cadence, or
complete crown geometry is fixed. Later prose should not back-solve them casually
from the temperature alone.

## Canonical firing sequence

The mechanism proceeds through bounded stages:

1. **Accumulation:** separated reservoirs charge over a long interval. No one
   site contains the final focal energy as a near-Planck thermal state.
2. **Crown solution:** projectors distributed across interplanetary baselines
   establish one target, timing, momentum, and polarization solution.
3. **Baryon loading:** the array injects and intercepts finite baryonic matter.
   That mass supplies the baryon-rich plasma and bounds the initial strangelet
   mass account.
4. **Counterstream launch:** particles and radiation travel separately. A beam
   path is energetic and dangerous but is not itself the Rehearsal temperature.
5. **Thermalization:** only at convergence do microscopic kernels acquire the
   reconstructed near-Planck spectrum.
6. **Operating bifurcation:** a kernel may disperse too early, cross a
   self-gravity threshold, or remain briefly in the narrow useful branch. A
   microscopic horizon is a failure or uncertain branch, not the weapon's
   guaranteed product.
7. **Quench:** the surviving baryon-rich plasma expands and cools through QCD
   phase formation. Strangelets form here, not at peak temperature.
8. **Residue:** radiation, ordinary hadrons, leptons, gravitational disturbance,
   possible horizon products, and many strangelets leave with a distribution the
   crown cannot select or count.

The array can guarantee catastrophic energy deposition and loss of ordinary
nuclear matter at the focus. It cannot choose which strangelet states survive.
That inability is the weapon's defining limitation and its terminal risk.

## Real strange-matter foothold and fictional departure

Real high-energy theory permits, but does not establish, the hypothesis that
bulk strange quark matter could be more stable than ordinary nuclear matter
under some parameters. Stability, surface tension, charge, weak equilibration,
minimum baryon number, formation pathway, and survival all remain uncertain.
Some color-flavor-locked models favor positively charged strangelets, which
would be repelled by ordinary nuclei rather than catalyzing them freely.

The adopted prompt formation story does not require weak interactions to convert
up and down quarks into strange quarks during a `10^-23`- to `10^-20`-second
quench. Strong interactions first produce strange-antistrange pairs. During
cooling and hadronization, preferential export of antistrangeness in emitted
hadrons can distill net strangeness into a residual dense droplet. Weak reactions
can move a droplet toward flavor equilibrium only after it has survived long
enough. Whether this pathway produces even one stable strangelet in nature
remains unresolved; Apsis producing many is the fictional commitment.

The Rehearsal makes four explicit fictional commitments beyond established
physics:

1. its baryon-loaded near-Planck quench reaches a strange-matter stability basin
   unavailable to known natural and laboratory collisions;
2. it produces a large strangelet population even though the exact yield is
   unknowable;
3. a rare subset is sufficiently long-lived and sufficiently neutral or
   otherwise able to overcome charge barriers to convert suitable ordinary
   matter; and
4. converted dense reservoirs can fragment or eject more viable daughter
   strangelets than the cascade loses.

The fourth commitment turns conversion into a branching process. Let `N_d` be
the daughters produced by one converted dense reservoir, `f_v` their viable
fraction, `f_e` their escape fraction, and `P_c` the probability that an escaped
viable daughter is captured by and successfully converts another usable
reservoir. Then `R_s = N_d f_v f_e P_c` is the mean number of later reservoirs
successfully seeded by each converted reservoir. `R_s <= 1` describes a chain
likely to die out or remain local. A sustained `R_s > 1` is necessary but not
sufficient for universal-scale spread: those capture paths must also form a
percolating network through accessible baryonic reservoirs. When both conditions
hold and the Apsis yield supplies sufficiently many independent lineages,
extinction of every branch becomes operationally negligible. Canon calls that
state a **supercritical Succession** and treats conversion across that accessible
network as inevitable.

Dense neutron matter is the most credible amplifier because it minimizes the
electrostatic barrier and contains an enormous baryon inventory. Planets, stars,
ships, and diffuse gas present different capture and fragmentation problems.
The entry must not treat every strangelet as equally dangerous or every contact
with one atom as instantaneous universal conversion.

The lowest-energy fictional endpoint is a fragmented or Coulomb-frustrated
strangelet phase rather than one featureless body of bulk quark matter. That
commitment preserves the promised universe “of strangelets.” It remains a
fictional extrapolation even where strangelet-crust models provide a theoretical
foothold.

## Cosmological and causal boundary

“Conversion of the entire universe” is accepted as common speech and Lower State
doctrine. The precise scientific threat is **conversion of ordinary baryonic
matter in the Succession's causally reachable future**.

No strangelet outruns light. Expansion and permanent cosmological horizons can
leave regions unreachable. Photons, neutrinos, dark matter, vacuum energy,
spacetime, and black-hole interiors are not converted into strangelets merely
because the chain is terminal for ordinary matter. A black hole may accrete a
seed or converted matter without becoming a strangelet.

Vacuum decay would be a separate mechanism capable of changing spacetime's vacuum
state. It is not part of The Rehearsal and cannot be introduced merely to make
the popular word *entire* literal. Likewise, delayed observation of a distant
conversion is ordinary light-cone delay, not faster-than-light propagation or a
Manifold contradiction.

## Apsis geography and history

[Apsis Range](../canon/places/apsis-range.md) is a remote, deliberately
uninhabited test volume outside Kelan–Ruva and outside any inhabited planetary
environment. Its exact containing system, star, bodies, coordinates, route, and
present condition remain withheld or unresolved. The range adds one
consequential external location without deciding whether the wider system is
entirely uninhabited or adding another inhabited civilization.

The [Apsis Directorate](../canon/institutions/apsis-directorate.md) grows from a
distributed primordial-state reconstruction program. Surviving records support
the scientific lineage and later militarization, but do not identify one inventor
or one clean moment at which the program becomes a weapon.

In Compact Year +271, the
[Apsis Discharge](../canon/events/apsis-discharge.md) activates the completed
crown for 47 milliseconds. Remote telemetry, missing energy and momentum
accounts, surviving component lineages, and detector records make the firing,
convergence, and numerous massive strange-rich tracks Grade A. Interpreting a
large but unknowable population of those products as strangelets, including any
long-lived yield, remains Grade B. Catalytic escape remains Grade E. The absence
of a recoverable census limits the in-world grade; it does not reverse the
out-of-world authorial decision that many strangelets formed.

No second full firing is accepted. No complete crown is known to survive. No
confirmed second converted reservoir proves Succession. The range remains under
exclusion because absence of returning evidence cannot establish absence of an
escaping seed.

## Law and the Houses

The [Common Matter Interdict](../canon/institutions/common-matter-interdict.md) is
the separate post-Apsis legal framework. It prohibits complete convergence-crown
architecture, deliberate strangelet production or custody, and possession of
the combined synchronization and baryon-injection systems needed to recreate the
discharge. It permits bounded primordial-state research only where independent
physical limits prevent complete focus. The Interdict is not an amendment to the
Sixth Compact, the Articles of Open Radiance, or the Articles of Bodily Dissent.

The [Houses of the Lower State](../canon/institutions/houses-of-the-lower-state.md)
are small, dispersed religious traditions rather than one church. They teach that
ordinary matter is a metastable mistake and The Rehearsal is **the Proof** that
creation can settle into its lower state. A universal Succession is **the
Settlement**. Depending upon the House, **the Performance** means either the
deliberate firing that begins the Succession or the full history of conversion.

Most are Waiting Houses. They preserve telemetry, watch for delayed evidence, and
argue that a genuine ground state needs no human help. The outlawed **Open Yield**
schism seeks viable seeds, crown fragments, or another deliberate quench. Belief,
ritual, and watching are not weapon possession. Attempts to acquire prohibited
matter or reconstruction systems are.

The Houses' most persistent prayer is preserved as culture, not prediction:

> *May it have fired already, far enough away that its light has not yet arrived.*

## Six-way mechanism firewall

- **The Rzez** invalidates relationships by which existing matter remains
  structurally continuous. It does not heat matter into a plasma or favor a quark
  phase.
- **The Unhidden** directs finite living development and repair. A strangelet is
  not an organism, a seed in the biological sense, or a Plain Thing.
- **The Manifold** prevents locally exact spaces and histories from composing one
  global account. Rehearsal uncertainty comes from underdetermined high-energy
  physics and an unmeasured residue, not mutually incompatible histories.
- **The Closed Night** controls radiative transport across an anchored enclosure.
  The Rehearsal neither traps a target's existing heat nor creates near-Planck
  temperature along a radiant boundary.
- **The Agreement** redistributes prediction error across a live reciprocal
  carrier. Rehearsal synchronization coordinates machines but does not couple
  minds, suppress dissent physiologically, or spread through records.
- **The Rehearsal** converges finite transported energy upon finite baryonic
  matter and then loses control of the quench products. Its possible propagation
  is ordinary physical transport and conversion by surviving strange matter.

No cross-system interaction is established. Shared words such as *seed*,
*continuity*, *convergence*, *closure*, *state*, and *propagation* do not create a
shared origin, mechanism, or countermeasure.

## Scientific grounding

The real-physics boundary draws upon:

- NIST's [2022 CODATA recommended values](https://physics.nist.gov/cuu/pdf/JPCRD2022CODATA.pdf)
  for the Planck temperature and constants used in the scale estimates;
- Witten's [cosmic separation of phases](https://journals.aps.org/prd/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevD.30.272)
  for the strange-matter hypothesis;
- Farhi and Jaffe's [strange-matter stability analysis](https://journals.aps.org/prd/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevD.30.2379)
  for its parameter dependence;
- Rafelski and Mueller's [strangeness-production analysis](https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.48.1066)
  for strong strange-antistrange production in a quark-gluon plasma;
- Greiner and Stoecker's [strangeness-distillation analysis](https://journals.aps.org/prd/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevD.44.3517)
  for a model pathway by which a cooling baryon-rich plasma can concentrate net
  strangeness before any later weak equilibration;
- Jaffe and collaborators' [review of strangelets and collider safety](https://journals.aps.org/rmp/abstract/10.1103/RevModPhys.72.1125)
  for why known experiments and cosmic-ray history do not establish a dangerous
  strangelet-production pathway;
- Madsen's [color-flavor-locked strangelet charge analysis](https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.87.172003)
  for the expectation that relevant strangelets can be positively charged;
- Alford and collaborators' [strangelet-crust analysis](https://journals.aps.org/prd/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevD.73.114016)
  for a theoretical foothold for fragmented strange phases;
- the [neutron-star conversion model](https://journals.aps.org/prc/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevC.74.065804)
  as an example of conditional seeded conversion in dense matter, not proof of
  universal contagion; and
- the [cosmological event-horizon analysis](https://journals.aps.org/prd/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevD.66.064009)
  for the limit on what any subluminal chain can reach.

No cited work establishes that dangerous strangelets exist, that a collider can
produce them, or that ordinary matter faces a real strangelet chain reaction.
The large Apsis yield, catalytic neutral or barrier-crossing seeds, reproductive
fragmentation, and the conditions that make conversion inevitable across a
percolating accessible-reservoir network are explicit fiction.

The catastrophe is predictable. The residue is not.
