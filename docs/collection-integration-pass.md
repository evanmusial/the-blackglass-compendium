## Scope and authority

This pass answers the author's request to integrate everything currently in the
Compendium, repair accidental lore and scientific contradictions, and develop
thin causal, institutional, and human consequences. It is an editorial revision
of the existing collection, including the weapon reconciliation deferred by the
[physical-baseline adoption](known-world-physics-and-expansion-integration.md).

The Rzez remains a provisional working draft. The Closed Night, The Agreement,
and The Rehearsal remain adopted and established. The Unhidden and The Manifold
remain developing and reserved except for their existing explicitly shared
cores. New concept-local detail does not silently become regional history. The
Rzez exemplar remains preserved; clarifications belong to its active recension.

Historical integration records retain the decisions and deferrals of their
original stages. Their new subsequent-pass notices point here rather than
pretending those earlier decisions included this work.

## Depth added without power escalation

The expanded material develops care and maintenance after the spectacle:
passenger accommodation, district education and reserve labor, financial claims
that cannot settle before receipts return, Kharosi gravity accommodations, Avaran
atmospheric stewardship, Odrani staffing and independent standards, refugee
triage, long quarantine, witness recovery, bodily dissent, and the preservation
of texts through institutions that do not share a present.

These details create constraints on stories. An independent clinic needs
practicing staff. An unused food line needs renewal. A carriage berth needs heat
and care capacity. A signature authenticates a custodian but does not make a
compromised measurement true. A hazard quarantine requires relief and continued
review. A physically viable atmosphere does not settle who owns the services
that let a particular person breathe.

The systems continue to produce different problems. There is no common canceling
material, universal isolation procedure, shared precursor arsenal, regional
Agreement, controllable Manifold, or safe shortcut around route preparation.

## Scientific checks and their limits

Real references establish the footholds and help locate errors; they do not
validate the fictional departures.

- The [LZSM analysis by Ivakhnenko, Shevchenko, and Nori](https://arxiv.org/html/2203.16348v3),
  especially its general matrix-element expression, supports using the sweep of
  the full diabatic energy separation. It does not establish macroscopic
  Ligature transit or a delayed-response law.
- [NASA's water-recovery demonstration](https://www.nasa.gov/missions/station/iss-research/nasa-achieves-water-recovery-milestone-on-international-space-station/)
  provides a concrete foothold for high recovery with maintenance and remaining
  losses, rather than a perfectly closed ecology. The Compendium's settlement
  thresholds remain authorial engineering assumptions.
- [NASA's thermal-control reference](https://www.nasa.gov/smallsat-institute/sst-soa/thermal-control/)
  distinguishes stored heat, radiation, conduction, and operational heat
  rejection. [Fan and collaborators' nonreciprocal heat analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.03504)
  supports keeping thermodynamic limits when transport is direction-dependent.
  Neither establishes a free-space Closed Night or unlimited seam brightness.
- Experimental work on [agency in brain-machine control](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41562-021-01233-2)
  and [feedback rate in neuroprosthetic control](https://www.nature.com/articles/ncomms13825)
  supplies bounded neural-interface footholds. It does not demonstrate the
  Agreement's reliable coercive suppression across prepared people and machines.
- [CERN's LHC safety assessment](https://cds.cern.ch/record/1111112) and its
  [strangelet addendum](https://cern.ch/lsag/LSAG-Report_add.pdf) constrain claims
  about real strange-matter production and catastrophic growth. Apsis's
  inaccessible stability island, abundant yield, catalytic states, and viable
  daughtering remain explicit fiction.
- [Alford and collaborators' fragmentation analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0604134)
  considers strangelet fragmentation under model-dependent surface conditions;
  it does not establish a self-sustaining catalytic daughter process.
- [Cornell's branching-process notes](https://pi.math.cornell.edu/~grecoe/IHS/2019-03-04_Class_12.pdf)
  distinguish mean reproduction from extinction probability. The Rehearsal's
  many-lineage argument is explicitly conditional on independence and on
  transport and coverage assumptions that the surviving Apsis record cannot
  establish.
- [NIST's 2022 CODATA constants](https://www.nist.gov/publications/codata-internationally-recommended-2022-values-fundamental-physical-constants)
  support the units used in the Rehearsal scale checks. A dimensional estimate
  near the Planck regime is not a validated quantum-gravity model.
- [Perfused tissue research](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7361700/)
  and [vascularized tissue engineering](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3145227/)
  establish real transport constraints below the Unhidden's fictional
  total-boundary physiology.
- [Tong's general-relativity treatment of parallel transport](https://www.damtp.cam.ac.uk/user/tong/gr/grhtml/S3.html)
  makes clear why path dependence alone is ordinary curved geometry, not a
  Manifold diagnosis.

Route throughput, carriage heat, momentum mismatch, and rounded astronomical
figures are checked as arithmetic and analytical estimates. The Kelan–Ruva
architecture remains a provisional dynamical model: no long-duration many-body
integration, coupled climate model, or lunar tidal-evolution solution is claimed.
The same distinction applies to speculative neural and tissue capabilities.

## Intentional openings retained

The review does not fill the Founding transit, Earth's fate or civil year, the
Prior Hands, Blackglass's ultimate distribution mechanism, the associated
Manifold presence, or a confirmed catalytic escape from Apsis. These are bounded
unknowns with evidence and consequences, not blank explanations for arbitrary
powers.

Unassigned Rzez geography, the precise Compact-year duration, the current
inhabited-system census, detailed orbital evolution, and the developing
concepts' eventual dated anchors remain authoring reservations. A future scene
that needs one must make and integrate that decision explicitly. Existing scenes
and engineering examples may not borrow an answer silently.

## Continuing review rule

After any new entry, trace each new capability through preparation, operation,
failure, rescue, maintenance, livelihood, law, and documentary survival. For each
cross-reference ask whether the cited institution or technology could exist and
act at that place and date, through the available physical channel. Preserve a
mystery only when the manuscript identifies what is unknown and what evidence
or consequence remains despite that uncertainty.

Repository checks establish structural integrity. They cannot prove that no
future reader will identify a lore hole; the substantive protection is this
record of the repaired assumptions and the boundaries future prose must honor.
