## Scope

This pass incorporates the author's selected documentary ideas into the existing
weapons and lore: connected working records, a bounded recurring later voice,
distinct institutional perspectives, procedures revised after specific failures,
ordinary human consequences, and smaller cases with completed outcomes. It
uses the collection's own engineering, laws, history, and prose rather than
importing another fictional archive's organizations, classification system,
stock incidents, or narrative template.

The author expressly excluded puzzles, absences, prayers, and religion from
these additions. The new accounts explain their evidence, sequence, and outcome
directly. They introduce no clue trails, concealed reading order, missing-text
devices, devotional scenes, or religious developments. Existing material in
those areas is preserved; the request was applied to the proposed expansion,
not treated as an instruction to remove earlier canon.

## The recurring voice

[Maret Sen](../canon/people/maret-sen.md) is a new shared provisional person: a
Kharosi civil-loss assessor and later Blackglass contributor whose work begins
after Compact Year +274 and receipt of the sources she uses. Her commentaries
on older catastrophes are retrospective. Her own cases and professional
development fit within one later career.

Her sequence is explicit. Early quarry comparisons and the paired-lift case
establish attention to completed work and its continuing costs. In the Odran
tenancy, residents correct her premature completion of a remedy at equipment
purchase. Her later Agreement commentary separates workplace clearance from
rehabilitation. Her Common Matter work limits each conclusion to the claim or
service interval it can actually settle.

Sen has declared clients, a practical vocabulary, and acknowledged corrections.
She relies on specialist findings and does not acquire the mandates of the
Compact Commission, the Articles, or the Interdict. Her persona supplies neither
ancient authorship nor a unifying explanation of the systems. The new
[civil-record frame](../frame/civil-records.md) likewise remains provisional.

## Shared lore and authority

The First Quarry Houses and Compact Commission notes now connect operating
records to physical safety and bounded local findings. The Open Radiance and
Bodily Dissent notes connect the new cases to their existing protected
interests. The Apsis Directorate and Common Matter Interdict notes distinguish
subsystem acceptance, integrated authority, observation custody, and funded
service. These are separate mechanisms and jurisdictions.

The household section of the spaceflight and settlement model follows
interruption through accommodation, treatment, wage loss, mutual help, and
return to use. Corrections travel through existing light-limited or carriage
routes. No service acquires instant regional authority or a new transit method.

The four entries retain their original lifecycle statuses: the Rzez is
draft/provisional; the Closed Night, Agreement, and Rehearsal are
adopted/established. The Unhidden and Manifold remain developing/reserved. Their
new incidents, quotations, evidence grades, and case-specific procedures are
recorded in their editorial companions and are not promoted into shared
history. The new unnamed local cases in the active entries remain entry-local;
their settlements do not establish universal compensation law.
