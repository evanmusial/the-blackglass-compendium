# Three-Weapon Integration Record

## Purpose

This record documents the deliberate integration of the Rzez, The Unhidden, The
Manifold, Kharos, and the Kelan–Ruva system. It is repository process, not an
in-world manuscript.

The normal workflow extracts canon after an entry draft finds its identity. This
integration is an intentional early reservation: the user requested a coherent
three-weapon lore spine while two subjects remain developing concepts. Only
cross-entry facts needed for that spine were promoted, and all are provisional.

## Promoted now

- three distinct primary systems: `entropic`, `morphogenic`, and
  `causal-topological`;
- human provenance and regenerative-medical ancestry for The Unhidden;
- finite ordinary-space anatomy and consciousness for autonomous Plain Things;
- shared Unhidden vocabulary, the reported ward reflex, basic environmental
  countermeasures, and humane-custody requirements;
- extrinsic-unknown provenance for The Manifold and the reality of at least one
  associated presence;
- rare, indirect, unreliable human interference with Manifold establishment;
- the working Manifold sequence of incidence, exploration, boundary-lock, and
  persistence or release;
- concordance bundles and the shared Manifold terms **tachyon shard**,
  **Manifold rupture**, **boundary-lock**, **associated presence**, and
  **single-chart doctrine**;
- provenance as a taxonomy axis separate from original purpose and military
  consequence;
- Kharos as the legal, clinical, and scholarly point of return;
- Avara as the low-gravity medical and infrastructure partner;
- Avara's maintained-atmosphere principle that withdrawal of a viable
  environment is coercive power subject to review;
- Odran as the separated material, clock, ranging, and archive reference;
- the Institute of Applied Continuity tradition as a Rzez-rooted scholarly
  umbrella whose institutional survival remains unresolved;
- the Kelan–Ruva Concordance Service as a distributed, coequal scientific and
  emergency service;
- the broad relative sequence recorded in `canon/chronology.md` while dates,
  names, and incident contents remain reserved;
- and isolated redundancy as the home system's response to three incompatible
  hazard profiles.

## Retained in development

- permanent IDs, volume and book numbers, and final entry scopes;
- named Unhidden origin facilities, autonomous-mantle incidents, conversion
  programs, deployments, commanders, judgments, and surviving communities;
- named Manifold rupture sites, associated-presence morphology or conduct beyond
  observed association, induction programs, strategic purpose, and authorship;
- exact dates and historical placement beyond the broad relative constraints in
  `canon/chronology.md`;
- every proposed cross-weapon interaction;
- the physical mechanism of ordinary interstellar routes;
- and final legal regimes beyond the Rzez-specific Sixth Compact.

The promoted broad sequence consists of medical origin, offworld conversion,
later custody, early Manifold accounts, and contemporary instrumented
confirmation. Names, dates, sites, actors, and the contents of those incidents
remain reserved.

## Mechanism firewall

The Rzez invalidates structural relationships. The Unhidden distributes living
function through finite exposed anatomy. The Manifold prevents locally exact
spaces and histories from converging globally.

Shared words such as *boundary*, *continuity*, and *identity* are insufficient
evidence of a shared mechanism. Associated Manifold presences are not Plain
Things. Unhidden distributed cognition does not validate disputed Rzez identity
theory. Manifold effects do not explain ordinary route physics or every archival
contradiction.

## Geographic allocation

Kharos receives ordinary consequences rather than every spectacle: the first
open wards, technical scholarship, created-person hearings, concordance evidence,
and post-Valec construction. Avara receives low-gravity biomedical scale,
recovery infrastructure, and managed-life-support dilemmas. Odran receives
separated standards, clocks, dry laboratories, and redundant records.

The Unhidden's military conversion and defining deployment remain offworld. The
defining Manifold rupture remains at a frontier or comparably isolated site.
Valec retains the only universally accepted planetary deployment among the three
subjects.

## Metadata deferral

Taxonomy prose now reserves `phenomenon-complex`, provenance classifications,
and temporal-reach concepts. `phenomenon-complex` is not yet a valid entry value.
The metadata reference states that reserved scopes are not yet entry-valid. It
and the entry template also clarify that an unsupported original purpose is
omitted rather than written as `unassigned`. The JSON schema, validator, and
provenance fields in existing entry metadata remain unchanged. A future field or
scope change must update all five metadata surfaces atomically.

## Continuity checks for future drafts

- A Compact year is not automatically Kharos's 299-day orbital year.
- The Sixth Compact remains Rzez-specific.
- No confirmed Manifold rupture occurs in Kelan–Ruva unless deliberately changed.
- Humans do not manufacture, aim, or routinely use The Manifold.
- Unhidden forms grown for Kharos, Avara, and microgravity are not mechanically
  interchangeable.
- Odran is not a routine death-prison for conscious open forms.
- Avara remains a partner world with unresolved jurisdiction, not merely a
  Kharosi facility district.
- No home-system archive or clock is the sole authoritative copy.
