# Kelan–Ruva Natural and Human History Integration

## Purpose

This record documents the expansion of the physically modeled Kelan–Ruva home
system and the explicit adoption of humanity's Earth origin. It is a repository
decision ledger, not an in-world source. The long-form fictional history belongs
in [`canon/history/human-arrival-in-kelan-ruva.md`](../canon/history/human-arrival-in-kelan-ruva.md);
the fixed and derived astronomical architecture belongs in
[`canon/places/kelan-ruva-system.md`](../canon/places/kelan-ruva-system.md).

The decision has two different status layers:

- Humanity's terrestrial ancestry, deliberate migration, plural arrival
  assemblages, Avara-first settlement sequence, and permanently unnumbered
  Earth-calendar correlation are **established authorial canon**.
- The system note remains **provisional** as a whole because important climate,
  rotational, lunar-origin, political, and demographic variables are still
  deliberately open. Its listed masses, radii, orbits, and direct physical
  consequences are nevertheless the current authoring model and must not be
  contradicted casually.

This expansion does not alter the adoption status or mechanism of any weapon.

## Decision superseded

The earlier setting boundary said that Earth was not part of confirmed history
and that humanity's origin remained undecided. The author's later instruction
explicitly supersedes that boundary.

The new decision is narrower than a complete origin story:

1. Human beings and their managed biological inheritance originated on Earth.
2. People reached Kelan–Ruva through a deliberate settlement effort.
3. Uncrewed surveys preceded people.
4. More than one materially distinct inhabited carrier assemblage arrived.
5. Avara preserves the oldest continuously occupied human refuge in the system.
6. Durable settlement of high-gravity Kharos followed over a prolonged Heavy
   Descent; Kharos later became the demographic and civic home world.
7. The Founding Interval began at least several millennia before the earliest
   securely ordered Meridian events.
8. Physical intervals and astronomical epochs are recoverable in principle and
   often in practice. A unique numbered Earth civil year is not.

The reason for departure, exact passenger and carrier counts, direct versus
intermediate routes, passage mechanism, experienced duration, Earth's present
condition, and possibility of return remain open.

## Natural-system model

### Central binary

Kelan is a `0.98 M☉`, `0.97 R☉`, approximately `0.90 L☉` G4 V star. Ruva is a
`0.34 M☉`, `0.35 R☉`, approximately `0.015 L☉` M3 V dwarf. Their relative orbit
has semimajor axis `0.140 AU`, eccentricity `0.05`, and period `16.65 days`.
Kelan supplies approximately 98 percent of their combined bolometric output.

The binary's `16.65-day` inertial period must not be described as Kharos's
observed illumination cycle. Because Kharos advances prograde during each binary
orbit, the apparent stellar geometry repeats on a mean `17.63-day` synodic
cycle. Stellar conjunctions alternate at a mean interval near `8.82 days`, with
unequal actual intervals because of eccentricity.

From Kharos the stars can separate by approximately `8.7 degrees`. Kelan appears
roughly Sun-sized; Ruva is a smaller resolved red disk. A central Ruva-on-Kelan
passage removes about `12.8 percent` of total daylight for several hours. It is
a red-dim day, not night. Kelan occulting Ruva is far shallower. Binary phase can
move top-of-atmosphere flux roughly seven percent to either side of the mean,
but oceans and atmosphere make this weather and energy-system forcing rather
than a global freeze-thaw switch.

### Planetary architecture

Six low-eccentricity, nearly coplanar planets orbit the binary barycenter:

| Body | Orbit | Period | Physical role |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Edras | `0.53 AU` | `122.7 d` | Hot, airless refractory world. |
| Talra | `0.75 AU` | `206.5 d` | Hot volatile-rich sub-Neptune. |
| Kharos | `0.96 AU` | `299.0 d` | Temperate rocky super-Earth and later human home world. |
| Neral | `1.72 AU` | `717 d` | Cold rocky ice-border world. |
| Vaskor | `4.60 AU` | `8.59 y` | Gas giant shaping the Serrin Belt and Trojan populations. |
| Istra | `11.9 AU` | `35.7 y` | Ice giant and outer major planet. |

Kharos receives approximately `0.993` of Earth's mean flux and lies within a
conservative circumbinary habitable zone. This is an energy-budget constraint,
not permission to assume an Earth clone. Surface pressure, composition,
rotation, obliquity, clouds, oceans, continents, and biospheric history remain
materially important and unresolved.

The binary's dynamical exclusion reaches roughly `0.35 AU`; the authoring model
reserves everything within `0.40 AU` against natural long-lived circumbinary
planets. Adjacent major planets are separated generously in mutual-Hill terms.
The architecture is credible without being a claim that an unpublished exact
ephemeris or multibillion-year numerical integration already exists.

### Kharos, Odran, and Avara

Kharos is `2.6` Earth masses and `1.30` Earth radii, giving approximately
`1.54 g` surface gravity and `15.8 km/s` escape speed. Its mass and radius imply
a compressed rocky body rather than a small gas planet.

Odran orbits at `200,000 km` with a `6.37-day` sidereal period and `6.51-day`
mean solar day. Avara orbits at `650,000 km` with a `36.67-day` sidereal period
and `41.8-day` mean solar day. Both are locked to Kharos. Kharos remains fixed,
apart from libration, above their near hemispheres and never rises on their far
hemispheres. It spans approximately `4.75 degrees` from Odran and `1.46 degrees`
from Avara.

The lunar system is intentionally described as plausible but demanding. Avara
lies at about `0.361` of Kharos's planet-only Hill radius, within both the
classical approximately `0.49` and a conservative approximately `0.40` prograde
limit, but with little room for careless changes. Odran and Avara are separated
by approximately `4.23` mutual Hill radii. Their near-circular, near-coplanar
orbits and a successful coupled tidal history are requirements. Their origin is
not reduced to a simple Earth–Moon analogue.

Odran's proximity makes it the dominant lunar tide raiser on Kharos even though
Avara is much larger. The two lunar directions become collinear about every
`3.85 days` and return to the same side about every `7.71 days`. These facts
establish strong compound forcing, not a universal coastal height. Rotation,
bathymetry, continents, ocean depth, and dissipation must be fixed before local
tides can be quantified.

### Formation history

The system's conservative natural history now has a usable sequence:

1. Kelan and Ruva formed together and migrated inward while early gas and disk
   torques damped the close binary.
2. Binary torque opened an inner disk cavity and established the present
   circumbinary stability edge.
3. Edras and Talra formed farther out and migrated inward before gas dispersal.
4. Kharos and Neral completed rocky accretion on wider, calmer orbits.
5. Vaskor formed beyond the snow line, shaped the Serrin Belt, preserved Trojan
   populations, and scattered volatile-rich bodies.
6. Istra, the Ravel Reach, and the Far Cloud retained progressively colder
   material.
7. Young stellar activity drove atmospheric escape and photochemistry. Later
   outgassing and delivery are compatible with Avara's secondary atmosphere.
8. Odran and Avara circularized and spin-locked through a still-unresolved
   coupled impact, capture, accretion, damping, and migration history.

This gives natural history causal texture without pretending one unmodeled moon
origin or atmospheric narrative has been proven.

## Lived consequences of the model

The system is not a decorative orbital table. Directly derived properties now
shape settlement and history:

- Kelan owns ordinary daylight; Ruva stains it, moves the secondary shadows,
  and periodically crosses the primary.
- Kharos's gravity makes falling, lifting, pregnancy, rehabilitation, launch,
  and building form materially different from their Earth analogues.
- Odran-led compound tides make coastlines engineered places, while exact local
  tidal regimes stay open until Kharos's rotation and oceans are designed.
- Avara's long day demands atmospheric heat transport, energy storage, and
  daylight-cycle planning. Its near side receives permanent Kharos visibility,
  planetshine, eclipses, and direct optical access; its far side depends upon
  relays and offers a darker observational sky.
- Odran's airless `6.51-day` solar cycle creates severe thermal work and makes
  its far hemisphere valuable for separated observation while the near side
  faces Kharos and transport activity.
- Kharos's high escape speed gives low-gravity Avara and Odran lasting
  industrial leverage even after Kharos becomes the population center.
- Light-time moves from seconds inside the triworld region, to minutes among
  the planets, hours in the Ravel Reach, and days in the Far Cloud. Outer-system
  machinery must possess real operational autonomy.
- The inner system offers abundant power but hard heat rejection. The outer
  system offers volatiles and cold sinks but weak power and long delay. Resource
  geography therefore follows physics rather than a uniform station template.

These consequences are distributed through the Kharos, Avara, Odran, system,
chronology, institution, and engineering-landscape notes rather than left only
in this decision record.

## Human historical sequence

The Founding Interval is a process, not an anniversary:

1. **Far Survey:** uncrewed assays, reflectors, and beacons enter from the outer
   system and survey inward. Their distribution proves deliberate destination
   selection.
2. **Carrier assemblages:** at least three materially distinct inhabited
   assemblages bring people, reproductive archives, food ecologies, microbes,
   medicine, and fabrication capacity. Archaeology does not decide one convoy
   versus several migrations or lost intermediate settlements.
3. **First Shelter:** sealed occupation on Avara becomes the oldest continuous
   human refuge. Avara offered low gravity, water, volatiles, orbital access,
   and distance from Kharos's biological uncertainty and gravitational risks.
   It was not terraformed rapidly from vacuum, and its prehuman biology remains
   open.
4. **Heavy Descent:** repeated robotic and crewed survey gives way to sealed
   Kharosi surface stations and then multigenerational settlement. Medicine,
   powered assistance, water-supported therapy, low broad structures, and
   evacuation capacity precede heroic independence.
5. **Later arrivals:** new standards, languages, biological stores, and archive
   traditions join an existing Avaran and early Kharosi society. They prevent
   one uncontested founder culture or simple ancestry hierarchy.
6. **Useful dismantling:** carrier hulls become shielding, reservoirs, clinics,
   workshops, foundations, and feedstock. Archives survive unevenly as formats,
   clocks, headers, and conversion software lose practical value.
7. **Severance:** Earth contact ends without one demonstrable final message or
   single archive-destroying event. No known current route points home.
8. **Long foundation:** locally born institutions, calendars, ecologies, and
   interworld economies emerge. Human expansion eventually reaches other
   systems before the Meridian Age, but the order remains open.

Kharos is therefore home without being cradle or first shelter. Avara's priority
supports coequal historical standing without granting hereditary sovereignty.
Odran's old transfer and observation roles anticipate, but are not identical to,
its modern standards and concordance institutions.

## The Earth-correlation boundary

The user-facing premise is not that people have forgotten astronomy. The setting
keeps three different things separate:

| Question | Recoverability |
| --- | --- |
| How much physical time separates two declared events? | Often recoverable within stated scientific uncertainty. |
| What did the sky look like in a defined frame at an observation? | Recoverable from astrometry, proper motion, radial velocity, pulsar behavior, and old catalog residuals. |
| What numbered civil year did an Earth society call that epoch? | Not uniquely recoverable from the surviving chain of records. |

The missing datum is conventional and archival. A civil year number is not
written into stellar motion. Surviving navigation records preserve vectors,
spectra, pulse templates, counter offsets, and partial transforms. Human-facing
records preserve month names, leap rules, dates, eras, and mission counts. No
accepted artifact joins both halves to an authenticated observation location,
time scale, trajectory, and uninterrupted clock transformation.

This remains realistic because even modern astronomical catalogues declare
coordinate reference systems, reference epochs, and time scales as metadata.
Proper motions allow stellar configurations to be propagated through physical
time; they do not identify a religious or political calendar zero. Civil time
likewise requires maintained definitions and published offsets among atomic and
rotational standards. The Compendium's loss is of that complete provenance
chain, not of arithmetic.

The canonical formulation is:

> The stars preserve the interval. They do not preserve the name of the year.

No hidden Common Era date should appear in authorial notes. In-world scholars
may construct mutually incompatible crosswalk families, but no current proposal
exceeds Grade C and none may decide citizenship, ownership, inheritance, or
arrival priority.

## Evidence and interpretation

The history preserves a graduated in-world argument:

- Grade A establishes nonlocal terrestrial biology, the arrival horizon,
  uncrewed survey before people, distinct construction layers, Avara before
  Kharos, and the broad settlement sequence.
- Grade B supports identification of Earth and Sol, deliberate migration, and
  later arrival into an existing society.
- Grade C contains one-convoy and direct-departure claims, candidate calendar
  crosswalks, and intermediate-settlement models.
- Grade D contains particular refuge motives, deliberate archive destruction,
  lost colony survival, and founding political promises.
- Grade E contains Earth government, moral exile, nonhuman selection, and
  prophesied return-fleet stories.

Established authorial canon does not require present characters to know every
established fact with equal confidence. Conversely, an established fact that a
Grade E tradition exists does not make the tradition's claim true.

## Institutional and cultural consequences

The origin decision creates recurring setting texture rather than a single
exposition scene:

- First Shelter observances leave one date or clock deliberately blank rather
  than pretending to know Arrival Day.
- Kharosi Heavy Descent memory makes assistance, rehabilitation, and shared
  infrastructure foundational rather than shameful.
- “Earth year” can name a reconstructed duration without naming the current
  Earth calendar year.
- No priority of arrival gives carrier descent no hereditary political rank.
- Calendar neutrality prevents speculative crosswalks from determining legal
  age, inheritance, title, sovereignty, or treaty order.
- Carrier artifacts and biological archives are treated as common human
  inheritance even when held locally.
- No fragmentary Earth charter or passenger contract governs the present by
  antiquity alone.
- The Kelan–Ruva Concordance Service maintains independent Origin Sky
  reductions and dependency graphs but cannot vote a calendar crosswalk into
  truth.
- Odran preserves independent astronomical and chronometric copies without
  becoming a magically authoritative master clock.

## Scientific grounding

The relevant physical and archival distinctions are grounded in primary or
official sources:

- ESA's [Gaia DR4 documentation](https://www.cosmos.esa.int/web/gaia/dr4)
  explicitly distinguishes catalogue reference epochs, celestial reference
  frames, and coordinate time scales.
- ESA's [proper-motion explanation](https://www.esa.int/Science_Exploration/Space_Science/Gaia/Proper_motion)
  describes how measured stellar motion changes the sky with time; the
  [EDR3 star-trail material](https://www.cosmos.esa.int/web/gaia/edr3-startrails)
  shows the same principle visually.
- BIPM's [time-scale recommendation](https://www.bipm.org/en/-/cctf-recommendation-2017-3)
  distinguishes TAI and UTC and records that their relationship is maintained by
  an explicit integer-second offset.
- The US Naval Observatory's [Julian Date explanation](https://aa.usno.navy.mil/data/JulianDate)
  distinguishes a continuous astronomical day count from conversion to a civil
  calendar.
- Circumbinary stability, habitable-zone, mass-radius, satellite-stability,
  atmospheric-escape, and formation sources are linked directly from the
  [system note](../canon/places/kelan-ruva-system.md#scientific-grounding).

These sources support the logic of the model. They do not imply that a real
historical dataset has been projected into the fiction or that the author has
selected a concealed Earth year.

## Retained boundaries

- No numbered Earth departure, arrival, or present year.
- No selected Earth calendar as the secret correct crosswalk.
- No fixed journey duration or passenger-experience model.
- No fixed founding transit mechanism and no automatic identification with
  present routes or The Manifold.
- No conclusion about Earth's current survival, politics, habitability, or
  knowledge of Kelan–Ruva.
- No single reason for migration.
- No complete list of carrier names, sponsors, passengers, or direct departure
  points.
- No claim that Kelan–Ruva was humanity's first extrasolar settlement.
- No Kharosi evolutionary origin for humanity.
- No reduction of Avara to a later Kharosi colony or a rapidly terraformed
  vacuum body.
- No resolution of Avara's possible prehuman biosphere.
- No resolution of Kharos's possible prehuman biosphere.
- No one mutation or subspecies that makes Kharos's gravity effortless.
- No settled Kharosi rotation, detailed climate, lunar origin, or exact lunar
  tidal history without further modeling.
- No weapon lineage hidden in the founding carriers merely to join the setting's
  technologies together.
