# The Apsis Discharge

## Chronological role

The Apsis Discharge occurs at [Apsis Range](../places/apsis-range.md) in Compact
Year +271. It is the first and only accepted full-energy firing of
[The Rehearsal](../../entries/volume-04/book-01/d-1-01-the-rehearsal.md), and the
event that fixed the Directorate's restricted project name in public usage.

The firing interval lasts approximately **forty-seven milliseconds**. This is
the duration of the synchronized macroscopic pulse train, not the duration for
which one object remains at the quoted temperature. The individual focal
kernels persist for only approximately `10^-41` to `10^-40` seconds. Their
expanding products cool and hadronize over roughly `10^-23` to `10^-20`
seconds before joining the ordinary, longer-lived particle and debris field.

## Supported sequence

The [Apsis Directorate](../institutions/apsis-directorate.md) first delivers a
prepared baryon sleeve and terminal package to the designated focus. Distributed
projectors then emit individually finite particle and radiation pulses. No beam
contains a projected temperature. The extreme condition appears only after the
pulses meet, lose their directed organization, and thermalize.

A **baryon-bias pulse** entrains material from the prepared sleeve and terminal
hardware into the focus. This does not create net baryon number. It gives the
cooling plasma more matter than antimatter so that its final products can
include baryonic fragments rather than annihilating almost completely.

The convergence crown produces a train of microscopic reheating kernels rather
than one macroscopic near-Planck fireball. Some kernels enter the
**horizon-poisoned branch**: overfocus creates a transient trapped surface or a
comparably opaque self-gravitating state, suppressing the recoverable exotic-
matter yield while worsening the local radiation environment. Others enter the
**open-quench branch** and expand before self-gravity closes them. It is in this
cooling branch, not at peak temperature, that strange-rich droplets can survive
hadronization.

The prepared sleeve, most of the terminal crown, and the inner witness platforms
are destroyed. The outer range records an asymmetric burst of photons, leptons,
hadrons, neutrinos, and relativistic debris. Several projectors fail under
returning particle load and loss of timing reference. The coarse fact of
catastrophe is predictable. The partition of energy among jets, trapped
surfaces, ordinary particles, and strange-rich fragments is not.

## Evidentiary ledger

The event's claims do not share one grade.

- **Grade A:** the crown fired, the convergent energy arrived, the forty-seven-
  millisecond train occurred, and the terminal range was destroyed. Independent
  outer stations, surviving power ledgers, physical detector damage, and
  authenticated Directorate telemetry agree.
- **Grade B:** the hottest kernels reached a spectral-equivalent temperature on
  the order of `10^30` kelvin, approximately two orders of magnitude below
  the Planck temperature. The value is inferred from an extrapolated particle
  spectrum and uncertain high-energy degrees of freedom; no thermometer directly
  measured it.
- **Grade A:** the open-quench intervals produced numerous massive, strange-rich
  tracks with abnormal charge-to-mass behavior. The detectors saturated and
  were partly destroyed, making an exact multiplicity unrecoverable.
- **Grade B:** a large but unknowable population of those products were
  strangelets rather than short-lived hypernuclear debris. No accepted physical
  sample survives in declared custody.
- **Grade E:** at least one long-lived, low-charge strangelet occupying a
  catalytic stability island escaped Apsis. This is the decisive claim of the
  Lower State cults and remains unsupported by a recovered seed or an accepted
  conversion event.

Grade A strange-rich tracks do not prove that strange matter is the absolute
ground state of baryonic matter. They do not prove long-term stability,
catalysis, or escape.

## The conditional terminal sequence

Post-Apsis theory names four stages that would follow only if several unresolved
conditions were all met.

1. **Taking:** a sufficiently stable, neutral or weakly charged strangelet
   survives the prompt field and becomes embedded in dense baryonic matter.
2. **Flowering:** conversion of a dense reservoir produces enough energy and
   material rearrangement to eject viable daughter strangelets.
3. **Succession:** each converted reservoir seeds, on average, more than one new
   conversion. The strangelet reproduction number `R_s` has exceeded one.
4. **Settlement:** ordinary baryonic reservoirs in the chain's future light cone
   are progressively replaced by lower-state matter.

**Daughtering** is a fragmentation and conversion process, not reproduction by
a living organism and not creation of mass. Every daughter consists of baryons
injected by the weapon or converted from encountered matter.

If a catalytic stability island exists, if daughtering remains viable, if
`R_s > 1` remains sustained after losses, and if viable capture paths form a
percolating network of accessible reservoirs, the large-scale conclusion is no
longer a matter of blast radius. Conversion becomes asymptotically unavoidable
throughout ordinary baryonic matter connected through that future-causal
network. It does not outrun light, convert photons or dark matter, extract
matter from black holes, alter the vacuum, or reach regions permanently outside
its cosmological horizon. Its timescale may be stellar or cosmological.

The conditional argument is stronger than the evidence that nature satisfies
its premises. No accepted observation establishes Taking, Flowering, or
Succession after Apsis. *The conversion of the entire universe* is therefore a
popular and cultic abbreviation, not a literal claim about all spacetime and
every form of energy.

## Legal and cultural consequence

The Discharge leads to the initial [Common Matter Interdict](../institutions/common-matter-interdict.md)
in Compact Year +274. The law is preventive because no enforcement action taken
after a supercritical Succession could recall every daughter seed.

Unauthorized telemetry from the event also becomes the founding scripture of
the [Houses of the Lower State](../institutions/houses-of-the-lower-state.md).
They call the weapon **the Proof**, the Apsis Discharge its first revelation,
and a universe-scale Succession **the Performance** or **the Settlement**.

The event remains an ordinary causal history despite the inability of present
theory to predict every branch. Its records disagree because instruments were
saturated, destroyed, or model-dependent, not because several incompatible
histories became simultaneously true. It is not a Manifold incident. Its
strangelets are material particles rather than Rzez residual identity,
Unhidden organisms, Closed Night boundaries, or Agreement carriers.

## Deliberately unresolved

- whether the open-quench fraction was intended or exceeded the Directorate's
  design;
- the strangelet multiplicity, charge distribution, mass spectrum, and
  lifetime distribution;
- whether any catalytic stability island exists;
- whether any viable seed escaped the range;
- the identities and casualties of personnel on redacted platforms; and
- whether any present astronomical anomaly is evidence of an Apsis-derived
  Taking rather than ordinary compact-object physics.
