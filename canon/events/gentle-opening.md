# The Gentle Opening

The Gentle Opening is the first controlled cohesion-field trial in the best
supported technical history of the Rzez.

Varek Ilyr and the First Quarry Houses extract a thirty-seven-meter crystal seam
from unstable host rock without conventional blasting or heat. The surrounding
cavern initially remains standing. Seven workers die two weeks later when a
neighboring support formation fails.

The event establishes both the technology's benevolent industrial purpose and
its characteristic delayed consequence. It is therefore the principal origin
anchor for the Rzez family even though older discovery traditions exist.
