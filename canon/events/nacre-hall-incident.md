# The Nacre Hall Incident

## Chronological role

The Nacre Hall Incident occurs on [Kharos](../places/kharos.md) in Compact Year
+203. It begins with 3,184 occupants and staff inside the hall; fourteen
responders are later enrolled through live diagnostic and rescue systems. It is
a contained Agreement event whose records establish two facts later made
central to law: a weapon operator can be incorporated into the same reciprocal
convergence imposed upon a target, and unanimous testimony can be evidence of
contamination rather than corroboration.

The incident directly precedes the Articles of Bodily Dissent in Compact Year
+205.

## Supported effect

During the live response, status reports from occupants, operators, and nominally
separate instruments agree so closely that officials initially treat them as
unusually strong evidence of calm control. Uncoupled exterior records instead
show matching timing and error residuals that the shared event cannot explain.

After a smoke emergency kills 123 people behind the sealed West Gallery doors
and the hall is decoupled, every surviving occupant produces the same
sixty-three-word account, including the same false claim that the gallery was
empty. Investigators treat that post-release unanimity as a consequence of the
event, not as independent confirmation of what happened inside it.

Exterior records show that the active carrier also includes members of the team
attempting to control it. Their later statements are not knowingly false. They
have become part of the system whose success they report.

Uncoupled instruments outside the hall preserve ordinary independent noise and
permit the contamination to be demonstrated. Their disagreement is the stronger
evidence.

## Legal consequence

The Nacre record establishes the phrase **one testimony** for many nominal
witnesses whose responses cannot count as independent because a reciprocal
carrier has suppressed their variance.

Later procedure preserves every statement while refusing to multiply its
evidentiary weight by the number of speakers. Courts examine covariance,
carrier access, response latency, and physical independence before treating
unanimity as corroboration.

The event does not establish telepathy, arbitrary belief transmission, or
permanent merger. Survivors remain separate people and require staged discord
rehabilitation after disconnection.
