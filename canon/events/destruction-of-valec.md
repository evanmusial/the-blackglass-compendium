# The Destruction of Valec

The destruction of Valec is the only universally accepted planetary Rzez
deployment and Year Zero of the Compact Calendar.

The event begins with ordinary infrastructure and health anomalies, advances
through the four-month Gray Season, and culminates in loss of continental,
crustal, and mantle continuity. Valec does not explode; its organized systems
lose the ability to remain a world.

Recognizable geography disappears over eleven days. Fewer than three million of
approximately 6.2 billion inhabitants escape, and some evacuation craft later
fail after carrying exposed material.

The operator remains officially unresolved. The event creates the Sixth Compact
of Kharos and permanently reorganizes engineering law, architecture, forensics,
survivor culture, and political doctrine.
