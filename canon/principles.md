# Canon and Engineering Principles

## Editorial principles

These principles are suitable for the Compendium as a whole.

### The Codex is a reconstruction

Entries synthesize technical fragments, records, testimony, material evidence,
law, scholarship, religious tradition, folklore, and deliberate falsehood. The
editorial voice may weigh evidence strongly without becoming omniscient.

### Uncertainty is structured

Contradiction should not be used as permission for arbitrariness. An entry names
the sources of uncertainty, assigns evidentiary weight where possible, and
distinguishes a disputed explanation from a disputed event.

### Metaphysical truth is not settled by historical confidence

A Grade A experiment can have an unexplained result. A Grade E myth can contain
an accurate technical detail. The evidence scale measures support for a claim,
not whether the universe is material, divine, informational, or something else.

### Technology produces a world around itself

A forbidden instrument should have industrial ancestors, engineering practice,
accidents, doctrine, countermeasures, law, language, trauma, ritual, and misuse.
The entry is a history of consequences rather than a catalog of powers.

### Costs and limits belong beside capabilities

A technology becomes more convincing when characterization, propagation,
failure modes, operator risks, imperfect detection, countermeasures, and delayed
consequences are treated as seriously as destructive scale.

### The Compendium preserves disagreements

If two traditions or schools disagree in a way that deepens the setting, canon
should preserve the disagreement. A later entry may add evidence without being
required to close it.

## Shared provisional engineering principles

The first three systems support several common editorial and engineering rules
without establishing one universal physics.

### Ontological non-equivalence

The Rzez, The Unhidden, and The Manifold must be explained on their own terms.

- Rzez action is entropic failure among relationships inside an existing target.
- Unhidden action is human morphogenic engineering performed by finite living
  matter wholly present in ordinary space.
- Manifold action is an extrinsic causal-topological failure of global spatial
  and historical consistency.

Recurring words such as *boundary*, *continuity*, and *identity* may describe
analogous human fears. They do not demonstrate shared machinery, origin,
susceptibility, or metaphysical truth.

### Scale changes architecture

Scale is not a power setting. A surgical Rzez field, an Avaran recovery mantle,
an open-form boarding organism, and a district-scale Manifold rupture require
different physical arrangements, support systems, risks, and human institutions.
Every entry should show what must be rebuilt when its scale changes.

### Extraordinary effects remain accountable

An arcane system may violate ordinary expectation without becoming a source of
unlimited matter, energy, or options.

- The Rzez uses a target's existing load, stress, and environment to complete
  failure.
- The Unhidden consumes finite biomass, oxygen, nutrients, water, time, and
  nursery capacity.
- The Manifold's unboundedness is configurational; one bounded envelope does not
  export infinite copies, energy, prophecy, or resources.

### Establishment precedes mature consequence

The most dangerous state is normally prepared rather than summoned without
history. Rzez characterization, Unhidden growth and patterning, and Manifold
incidence and exploration create the conditions in which their mature effects
become possible. Detection and countermeasure doctrine should focus on those
transitions as much as on the final catastrophe.

### Capability and vulnerability share an architecture

The structure that produces an effect also creates its characteristic failure.
Rzez propagation needs continuity that can be severed. Total-boundary physiology
gains redundancy by exposing itself to occlusion and environmental attack.
Manifold boundary-lock produces denial but also a rim that can be mapped and
quarantined. Counters should arise from mechanism rather than cancel it by fiat.

## Rzez-family engineering principles

The following are supported for the Rzez family. They are not yet universal laws
of every arcane technology.

### Structural identity

A stable object persists through organized relationships across time. The Rzez
calls this network a **cohesion architecture**. It includes material bonds,
interfaces, load paths, repair processes, and large-scale equilibria.

### Edge invalidation rather than node removal

Classical cohesion graph theory describes material units as nodes and their
load-bearing or identity-preserving relationships as edges. The Rzez primarily
invalidates edges. Surviving paths inherit more load, making failure nonlinear.

### Entropic bias rather than brute-force destruction

The Rzez makes disordered states easier for a target to enter. Gravity, pressure,
residual stress, thermal cycling, and ordinary use provide much of the eventual
destructive force.

### Characterization precedes efficient action

The instrument must establish a relationship with the target's structure.
Homogeneous or repeating systems are easier to characterize; heterogeneous,
changing, turbulent, discontinuous, or rapidly repairing systems are harder.

### Rzez scale changes emitter architecture

Large Rzez systems are not merely more powerful hand instruments. Surgical,
industrial, siege, strategic, and planetary uses require different emitter
arrangements, boundaries, transmission paths, and target knowledge.

### Damage can outlive the emitter

After cascade begins, a target can continue its own disassembly. New material
may inherit instability from damaged material, producing the feared residual
pattern called target inheritance or the butcher's stain.

## Morphogenic-family engineering principles

These principles are provisionally shared from The Unhidden's medical and
military lineage.

### Total boundary is finite anatomy

A total-boundary organism places every indispensable macroscopic physiological
function in tissue accessible through channels continuous with the surrounding
environment. It does not possess infinite surface area, a remote true body,
extra-dimensional organs, phasing matter, or unaccounted mass.

### Distributed function is not invulnerability

No single heart, brain, lung, or gut is an easy kill point, but useful tissue can
still be destroyed faster than it remaps. Broad occlusion, heat, desiccation,
hostile chemistry, and loss of wet-cradle support attack the same environmental
access that makes the form viable.

### Grown environment becomes body plan

Gravity, pressure, medium, and nursery geometry are developmental inputs. An
Avaran clinical mantle, a Kharos-rated support form, and a microgravity boarding
form are not interchangeable configurations of one generic organism.

### Autonomous forms are conscious

The autonomous organisms called Plain Things are authorially conscious. Their
legal status, memories, continuity across division, degree of coercion, and
relationship to patients or tissue donors remain disputed in-world. Ambiguity
about identity must not be used to retreat from the consequences of creating a
person capable of pain, speech, refusal, learning, and choice.

## Causal-topological engineering principles

These principles describe human analysis of The Manifold. They do not establish
its builders, purpose, or preferred vocabulary.

### Locally exact does not mean globally reconcilable

Inside one path or sequence, familiar physical law may remain intact. The
contradiction appears only when valid paths, measurements, or histories are
compared and no single geometry or chronology can contain them all.

### Boundedness can contain non-finite description

A rupture has a finite exterior envelope and finite local matter and energy. Its
retained family of valid configurations can nevertheless resist finite global
description. This is not permission to duplicate resources or select arbitrary
outcomes.

### Human induction is not control

Human programs cannot manufacture, program, routinely summon, or precisely aim
The Manifold. At most they may spend years arranging immature conditions and
biasing an approximate spatial or temporal envelope. Credible attempts are rare
because unintended boundaries, epochs, survivors, objectives, and associated-
presence events are normal consequences rather than exceptional malfunctions.

### Association does not establish authorship

At least one genuinely extrinsic associated presence is real, but observation at
incidence, boundary-lock, or release does not show whether it is creator,
operator, passenger, prisoner, custodian, consequence, witness, or something for
which those categories fail.

### There is no exportable paradox economy

The Manifold does not provide routine time travel, controllable prophecy,
instantaneous communication, infinite computation, or safe duplication. A
contradictory local history is a containment and evidence problem, not a resource
that can be ordered from outside.

## Candidate universal principles not yet adopted

The three systems suggest, but do not establish for the whole setting, that:

- identity may be an engineerable property;
- the history of remaining together may contribute to present structure;
- observation or external records may stabilize continuity;
- institutions, laws, memories, and names may possess cohesion architectures;
- and arcane engineering may manipulate relationships or information more
  fundamentally than matter.

These ideas should remain candidates until other entries independently require
or contradict them.
