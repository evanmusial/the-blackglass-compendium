# Physics of the Known World

## Status and authority

This document is the established authorial baseline for ordinary nature,
ordinary advanced engineering, interstellar transit, and the boundary between
those subjects and the six forbidden systems.

It is not one in-world textbook. No civilization, university, court, or Codex
editor possesses every statement in the same vocabulary or with the same
confidence. It records what future authors may treat as physically true, what
people in the setting can actually engineer, and which explanations remain
disputed even where the apparatus works.

The baseline is dated against real-world knowledge in **2026**. That date keeps
the work honest: a present-day scientific foothold, a far-future engineering
achievement, and an explicit fictional postulate are different claims.

This adoption does not revise any existing Compendium entry. The Rzez, The
Unhidden, The Manifold, The Closed Night, The Agreement, and The Rehearsal must
later be audited against this background individually. Until that audit, their
established mechanism firewalls remain controlling wherever language differs.

## Core decision: capability can outrun explanation

The known world does not possess a final theory of everything.

Its ordinary physical account is much broader and more accurate than the
twenty-first-century Standard Model and general relativity, but several of its
most consequential technologies were engineered before their deepest ontology
was settled. Scientists may possess a repeatable transfer function, calibrated
safety envelope, and mature industrial practice while continuing to disagree
about what the instrument changes fundamentally.

This is the strict meaning of **arcane engineering** in the setting. It does not
mean magic, ritual causation, or freedom from measurement. It means engineering
performed where operational control is stronger than explanatory closure and
where the missing explanation materially affects risk.

The route weighmaster, cohesion forensicist, morphogenic clinician, and Apsis
historian therefore ask different questions:

1. **Was the effect observed independently?**
2. **Were the variables that predict it characterized?**
3. **Can the initiating condition be prepared on demand?**
4. **Can the outcome be selected rather than merely provoked?**
5. **Can the effect be scaled without changing its mechanism?**
6. **Can it be governed without making ordinary life depend upon an unbounded
   hazard?**

No later claim follows automatically from an earlier one. One occurrence is not
a mechanism. Preparation is not control. Control at one scale is not control at
another. Industrial adoption is not proof of theoretical understanding.

## The three closures

Known-world scholarship commonly distinguishes three kinds of closure. The
terminology varies, but the distinction is established.

### Ledger closure

Mass-energy, momentum, charge, baryon inventory where relevant, entropy, waste
heat, and causal transmission must be accounted. An unexplained residual may be
evidence of new physics, a bad instrument, hidden infrastructure, fraud, or an
incorrect boundary. It is never permission to ignore the account.

### Operational closure

A preparation has operational closure when independent operators can state the
necessary apparatus, initial conditions, range of outcomes, diagnostic
signatures, abort points, and known failures. Operational closure can exist
without agreement about the underlying entity.

### Explanatory closure

A theory has explanatory closure when it derives the effect across the relevant
scales, predicts new observations, and survives attempts to distinguish it from
competing accounts. The Rzez lacks this closure. Civil ligature transit has
operational closure without a final explanation of locality binding. The
Manifold lacks even one globally closing account by definition.

The practical maxim is:

> A closed machine is not necessarily a closed explanation.

## Invariants of ordinary nature

### Conservation remains the default

Ordinary technology does not create matter, energy, momentum, or usable work from
nothing. Storage, transport, conversion, and concentration can be spectacular;
the invoice remains physical.

- Reactors consume fuel and reject heat.
- Radiant-return systems reduce one loss path and increase the burden upon
  another.
- A route exchanges two conserved carriages and requires endpoint work to
  localize them into useful local frames.
- A morphogenic body consumes biomass, gases, water, nutrients, time, and nursery
  capacity.
- A high-energy focus receives transported energy; it does not make temperature
  at a distance for free.
- Rzez collapse is paid largely by stress, gravity, pressure, heat, vibration,
  and the target's own stored energy after the accessible state paths have been
  changed.

### Entropy is not an aesthetic rule

Local order can increase by exporting entropy. Life, computation, fabrication,
repair, and route preparation all do so. No ordinary apparatus provides a cyclic
all-downhill process. Claims of perfect recycling conceal an external sink, a
degrading store, an omitted material stream, or fraud.

### Local relativity governs ordinary uncoupled propagation

Matter, radiation, and information moving through ordinary spacetime obey local
light cones to every routinely measurable precision. Gravity cannot be screened.
There is no reactionless drive, inertial cancellation, or local faster-than-light
beam.

Ligature transit adds a deeper causal structure without invalidating this local
account. A route carriage changes which stellar locality supports it, but the
exchange is ordered by the sequence field and cannot form a closed causal loop.
The Manifold remains a separate exterior failure of global composition and is
not evidence that ordinary causality is optional.

### Information requires a physical history

Information is instantiated in matter, radiation, fields, living states, or
their correlations. Quantum entanglement supplies no controllable faster-than-
light message. A description of an event is not the event's physical carrier.

This principle does not make knowledge harmless. A record may reveal how to
construct an apparatus, identify a dormant artifact, complete a model, or defeat
a safeguard. Blackglass is dangerous by those specific paths, not because prose
is a universal contagion.

### Scale changes architecture

No important technology scales through one dimensionless power dial. Large
systems require new geometry, control, cooling, timing, maintenance, political
authority, and failure containment. A route letter, passenger carriage, and
founding weight are not the same machine at different settings. A planetary
array is not an enlarged hand tool.

### Measurement paths are part of the experiment

One thousand instruments sharing one hidden clock, model, power supply, carrier,
or calibration history do not provide one thousand independent observations.
Known-world metrology therefore records physical independence, provenance, and
the ways an instrument can alter or join the system it measures.

## The incomplete frontier

The known world has not solved everything that was open in 2026.

- It has no complete, experimentally closed theory of the Planck regime.
- It characterizes additional particles and dark-sector behavior beyond the
  twenty-first-century model but does not use dark matter as fuel, structure, or
  reaction mass.
- Cosmic acceleration is measured with extraordinary precision but provides no
  local engineering handle and no reservoir of extractable vacuum energy.
- Neutrino astronomy and tomography are mature; neutrinos remain inefficient
  carriers for power or routine communication.
- Dense and strange phases of matter can be produced and studied under bounded
  conditions. Stable catalytic strange matter is not ordinary material science.
- Consciousness has operational and legal markers but no universally accepted
  reduction that turns a recording into a person.
- Locality binding and the sequence field are measured through routes, but their
  ultimate cosmological origin remains unknown.
- No theory explains why the Manifold and its associated presence exist.

Advanced civilization is not omniscient civilization. A discovery is kept only
when it changes a story, an institution, an engineering constraint, or a human
cost.

## The ordinary energy economy

### Generation is plural

No single source powers the known region.

- **Solar collection** dominates hot inner-system industry, orbital yards, and
  places with abundant area and predictable light.
- **Fission** remains valuable at isolated facilities because fuel is compact,
  equipment is robust, and output is not conditional upon local sunlight or a
  complex fusion supply chain.
- **Fusion** is mature civil infrastructure. Large magnetic and pulsed systems
  power cities, yards, and high-demand industries. They remain neutron, blanket,
  magnet, fuel-cycle, maintenance, and radiator problems rather than miniature
  stars in boxes.
- **Stored chemical and thermal energy** remains indispensable for vehicles,
  emergencies, and facilities whose safe shutdown matters more than peak
  efficiency.

Station-scale fusion plants commonly produce hundreds of megawatts to several
gigawatts of usable output. Larger complexes exist, but their magnets, blankets,
fuel processing, maintenance exclusion, and waste heat grow with them. A small
reactor is not automatically a light reactor.

### Storage is not generation

Grids and vessels use batteries, reversible chemical stores, hot and cold mass,
phase-change stores, flywheels, compressed fluids, pumped reservoirs where
geography permits, and superconducting magnetic systems. High-field stores can
release enormous power briefly; they require cooling, shielding, controlled
discharge paths, and material that survives the forces.

Antimatter is a laboratory reagent and occasional ignition aid in tiny inventories.
It is not a bulk fuel economy. Producing it costs more energy than its later
annihilation returns, and containment failure is an ordinary catastrophe.

The Rehearsal therefore implies no hidden stellar generator. Apsis accumulated
energy through many separated reservoirs over a long interval and released it
through a unique strategic geometry.

### Waste heat is geography

Power density is limited by the ability to reject heat. In vacuum, radiator area,
orientation, spectral condition, micrometeoroid exposure, and repair access shape
every serious installation. An atmosphere, ocean, or crust can absorb heat only
by changing. Planetary heat sinks are large environmental systems, not deleted
ledger entries.

## Ordinary propulsion and gravity

### Every ordinary vehicle pushes something

Surface launch uses chemical stages, reusable lifting vehicles, nuclear-thermal
systems where permitted, electromagnetic assist, and orbital infrastructure.
Electric and plasma drives move bulk cargo efficiently. Nuclear-electric and
fusion-electric craft serve high-value freight and passengers. Short-duration
fusion torches exist as expensive licensed vessels, but sustained one-gravity
travel is not routine.

Mass drivers and beam lanes move prepared cargo from airless bodies. They are
also prealigned weapons and are governed as trajectories, not merely industrial
machinery.

Routine sustained acceleration is measured in thousandths to hundredths of a
gravity. Higher performance consumes disproportionate reaction mass, power,
radiator capacity, engine life, and human tolerance. Inner-system journeys take
days to weeks; outer-system work takes weeks to months; distant cloud missions
may remain underway for years.

### There is no artificial gravity field

Habitats rotate. Accelerating ships provide thrust gravity. Coasting vessels use
rotating living sections, exercise, medication, or accept microgravity injury.
There are no inertial dampers. A tank, organ, tool, and conscious engineered body
all feel the same acceleration.

This preserves the physical identities of Kharos, Avara, and Odran. Kharos's
gravity makes bulk launch expensive and shapes bodies and buildings. Avara's low
gravity favors yards, large clinical forms, and orbital access while requiring
conditioning for travel to Kharos. Odran remains an airless, low-gravity work
environment whose inhabitants depend upon exercise, rotation, medicine, and
protected infrastructure.

### Sublight precursor flight

Small uncrewed precursor craft can cross interstellar distance through ordinary
space. Beamed sails, fusion stages, and long-duration autonomous repair permit
fractions of light speed sufficient for voyages of decades to centuries. These
missions are expensive, slow, and frequently lost. They are not a passenger
transport system.

Acceleration does not solve arrival. Every seed carries or declares a braking
architecture: a fusion stage, magnetic or electric sail against the destination
wind and interstellar medium, photonic braking, staged disposable braking mass,
or a deliberately slow terminal approach. A beamed sail with no capture account
is a flyby probe, not a receiver seed.

Their importance is foundational: a new ligature shore cannot be opened from one
side. A receiver seed must first reach the conjugate system by ordinary sublight
flight, by another already opened route, or through infrastructure whose earlier
delivery history survives. The long precursor makes regional expansion a work of
generations rather than a sequence of heroic jumps.

Modern civilization has no operationally closed century-scale passenger
foundation. No crewed vessel has demonstrated the ecological, radiation,
industrial-replacement, demographic, educational, and civic continuity needed to
arrive as a functioning society without destination support. Embryos and archives
are not parents and institutions. This modern limitation does not decide what the
Founding carriers were or how they crossed.

## Ligature transit

### Core physical decision

Present interstellar routes exploit **reciprocal locality exchange**.

Matter is normally supported by one macroscopic locality basin: the stellar
gravitational system in which its center of mass is physically present. Rare
pairs of stellar basins possess a natural two-site localization mode. That mode
is called a **ligature**.

A ligature is not a tunnel, folded road, hidden dimension, shortened geodesic, or
Manifold rupture. It is not a wormhole, warp metric, or negative-energy bubble.
It has no traversable middle. Paired endpoint stations slowly exchange the
locality support of two closed, mass-matched carriages. At completion, each
carriage is wholly present at the other shore.

The common formulation is:

> A route does not join two places. It lets two conserved burdens exchange which
> place is local.

### Conjugate locality

Ordinary position is not wholly fundamental. A bounded material system has its
internal spatial relations, its position in local spacetime, and a weaker
locality binding that selects the stellar basin supporting the system as a whole.

For almost every pair of systems that binding is diagonal: matter travels from
one to another only by crossing the distance. A few systems are **conjugate**.
Their mass distributions, motion, wider gravitational environment, and sequence
order permit an off-diagonal locality coupling.

The simplest engineering basis contains two complete paired assignments, not one
carriage at two independent positions:

- `|12>` means carriage `C1` is supported at shore `A` while matched carriage
  `C2` is supported at shore `B`;
- `|21>` means `C2` is supported at `A` while `C1` is supported at `B`.

Those assignments form the avoided crossing:

`H_AB = [[E_12 + Delta(Theta), g_AB], [g_AB*, E_21 - Delta(Theta)]]`

The natural coupling `g_AB` belongs to the pair. Weighhouses control the local
assignment detuning `Delta`. If `g_AB` is zero, no amount of power creates a route. If it is too
weak, the affinity can be measured but not used safely for a macroscopic
carriage.

The controlled state begins as `|12>`, becomes a coherent support of both paired
assignments during equalization, and finishes as `|21>`. There is no allowed
single-carriage transport sector. Without a matched countercarriage, no bound
macroscopic route mode exists.

Internal matter is not scanned, copied, disassembled, or reconstructed. Each
carriage's quantum and biological history evolves continuously through the
exchange.

### Why the network is sparse

A ligature is a pair property of whole stellar basins, not a beam aimed at chosen
coordinates.

- Most nearby systems have no useful coupling.
- A more distant system may be more strongly conjugate than a nearer one.
- A system normally possesses only a few viable ligatures.
- Machinery can stabilize and broaden an existing mode but cannot manufacture a
  missing stellar pair.
- Artificial masses are far too small to create a new basin.
- Two points inside one system are not independent locality states, so ligatures
  provide no in-system shortcut.

The known region is therefore a sparse, historically opened graph rather than an
any-to-any travel volume.

As an authoring constraint, serious locality survey finds roughly one potentially
macroscopic conjugacy among every twenty to fifty stellar basins examined. Most
rooted systems have no unopened usable candidate; a minority have one; no
established system currently has more than two unopened macroscopic candidates.
This is a scale boundary, not an omniscient in-world census.

### Reciprocal passage and the mass book

Macroscopic transit is intrinsically reciprocal. A ligature cannot carry a net
stress-energy occupancy from one stellar basin to another. It exchanges two
closed packets.

**Locality occupancy is a superselected quantity:** the usable two-shore sector
contains one closed carriage support at each basin. A one-carriage matrix element
is exactly zero. The ligature may exchange which carriage occupies each support;
it cannot add or remove an occupied support.

Every passage requires:

- one carriage at each shore;
- matched invariant mass-energy totals;
- matched exterior charge and low-order stress-energy multipoles;
- endpoint capacity to normalize and localize into local orbital frames;
- and schedules committed before the crossing begins.

The true selection rule applies to the complete **route-visible boundary state**:
total invariant energy, conserved charges, locality occupancy, and every exterior
degree of freedom to which the ligature couples. Low-order multipoles are the
principal engineering certification surface, not a claim that unmatched hidden
route-visible structure is harmless. Internal cargo detail that does not alter
that boundary state remains private until localization.

Fuel, stored heat, rotating equipment, batteries, compressed gas, living bodies,
and active reactors all enter the mass book. Adjustable ballast closes the
remaining difference. Internal transformations may occur during passage, but a
carriage cannot discard mass or radiate energy outside its boundary while
bilocally supported.

The route also provides no free orbital insertion. Each shore defines a standard
route four-momentum in its own comoving tetrad. The paired assignment states are
matched so that swapping the carriages leaves the route-visible stress-energy at
each shore unchanged. The ligature therefore does not compare an absolute
gravitational potential between stars or pay the full relative stellar velocity.

Local momentum works bring a departing carriage from its actual orbit into the
shore standard and take the arrived carriage from that standard into a useful
local orbit. At each shore the ordinary account is:

`W_shore = delta_E_local + Q_loss`

No closed itinerary produces net work after those local normalization and
localization losses.

> Every arrival is paid for by a departure of equal weight.

### Shores and weighhouses

The physically permitted endpoint region is a **shore**. Its active infrastructure
is a **weighhouse**, usually distributed through several separated orbital
stations rather than contained in one gate.

A complete shore needs:

- isolated carriage volumes;
- adjustable ballast banks;
- reference masses and exterior multipole shapers;
- independent sequence clocks;
- energy stores and heat sinks;
- momentum sinks and local orbital-localization machinery;
- long-baseline system ranging;
- autonomous completion reserves;
- traffic, rescue, customs, and quarantine facilities; and
- enough ordinary spacecraft to move arrivals away from the shore.

Destroying one control room may halt a scheduled crossing. Replacing a complete
shore takes years. Alternative weighhouses can reach one ligature only inside a
small family of permitted volumes, making them conspicuous and strategically
finite.

### Opening an otherwise unreachable shore

One endpoint cannot exchange with infrastructure that does not exist.

If the conjugate system is already reachable through another ligature, people and
machinery may build the new cross-link from inside it. If an abandoned or orphaned
weighhouse survives, surveyors may reopen it. The receiver-seed process is the
slower **frontier opening** used when no other access exists.

Astronomers first identify a candidate conjugate basin from weak locality
spectroscopy, stellar motion, and the response of reference masses. An uncrewed
**receiver seed** then crosses ordinary interstellar space over decades or
centuries. It must arrive, find the predicted shore volume, establish power and
local industry, construct independent clocks and ballast, and build the far
weighhouse before the first microscopic reciprocal exchange can occur.

One-ended locality spectroscopy measures a static invariant of the stellar pair.
It reveals no remote machinery, cargo, operator choice, or current shore state
and cannot be modulated into a live signal.

The seed carries a sequence-clock ensemble and a long table of recurring
**Answer windows** calculated before launch. Beginning after the earliest
plausible bootstrap date, the home shore prepares a small countercarriage at each
window. If the far shore is not ready, the reserved cycle returns only refusal or
fault at the normal decision time. If it is ready, the first Answer localizes. An
ordinary-light readiness message may eventually confirm the project but is not
required to start the prearranged attempts.

The launching society must therefore build the near experimental weighhouse and
maintain an institutional **vigil** across the seed's flight and bootstrap span.
If either shore loses the common Answer calendar, the far seed can send an
ordinary-light readiness notice and wait for a light-speed reply, adding as much
as one ordinary round trip before exchange.

The first return is therefore often received by descendants or successor
institutions of the people who launched the seed. Many seeds fail silently. A few
complete shores after their sponsoring state, company, or religion has vanished.
Such an installation is an **orphan shore** until a living jurisdiction accepts
or contests it.

This process explains why a route can be understood and still take generations
to open. It does not explain the Founding migration. No current shore reaches
Earth, and no surviving evidence proves that the carriers used ligature transit.

### The seven-stage crossing

1. **Declaration:** Both shores publish carriage mass, multipoles, hazard class,
   destination frame, and sequence window.
2. **Weighing:** Ballast closes the paired mass-energy account. Unmatched charge,
   motion, heat, and radiation are brought within tolerance.
3. **Normalization:** Each carriage enters a certified local inertial and orbital
   state. Shore machinery assumes the work that the ligature will not provide.
4. **Sequence lock:** Both weighhouses align precommitted programs to the
   ligature's sequence time. No live interstellar handshake is required.
5. **Commitment:** Before this threshold, refusal leaves both carriages local.
   The opposite shore cannot read that refusal before the route-depth decision
   window. After commitment, completion is safer than abort.
6. **Equalization:** The carriages become maximally bilocally supported. This is
   not a midpoint or a place that can be visited.
7. **Localization:** Locality support finishes exchanging. Each weighhouse absorbs
   remaining frame debt before opening its arrived carriage.

A carriage cannot steer, stop between systems, change destination, receive a
recall, or communicate outside itself during passage. A multi-route journey must
localize, clear quarantine, and be weighed again at every hub.

### Why passage takes days or weeks

The exchange must remain adiabatic. Driving the detuning too quickly makes the
carriage leave the supported state. Increasing power cannot make a weak ligature
arbitrarily fast; beyond its safe sweep, extra drive increases **spill**.

The qualitative relation uses sequence time `Theta`:

`P_spill ~ exp[-2 pi |g_AB|^2 / (hbar |dDelta/dTheta|)]`

Strong routes complete certified carriages in several days. Deep routes take
weeks. Fading routes may require months and become commercially useless before
becoming physically impossible. Navigators therefore speak of route **depth**,
not route length.

Certification also bounds the carried lapse `d tau_car / dTheta` away from zero
and keeps the two assignment branches matched. Route depth is therefore days or
weeks both in sequence and for the people inside, subject only to an ordinary
published redshift margin.

Each ligature also supports only a small number of carriage harmonics. A crossing
occupies its mode until localization and recovery. Strong hubs have several modes
or several ligatures; a frontier shore may exchange only one carriage at a time.

### Sequence and causality

Ligature arrival is spacelike relative to ordinary metric distance. Route physics
therefore reveals a global time function not known in 2026. It is called
**sequence** and written `Theta`. In ordinary spacetime, `grad Theta` is
everywhere future-directed timelike and every ordinary worldline satisfies
`dTheta / dTau > 0`.

Every supported ligature edge satisfies `Delta Theta >= Theta_min(AB) > 0`.
Chained routes, moving endpoints, and relativistic frame changes cannot reverse
the inequality. The sum of
`Delta Theta` around every supported route circuit is positive. A proposed
schedule that would close a causal loop loses its bound transport state before
commitment.

Different ordinary observers may disagree about the coordinate-time separation
of a passage. None can arrange for a record to arrive before its physical cause
in sequence. Local Lorentz symmetry remains accurate for ordinary matter and
radiation; it is not the complete global causal account of locality exchange.

Sequence supplies order and route lapse, not a privileged civil calendar. Its
zero point is conventional, old records often lack sequence calibration, and no
measurement translates an astronomical epoch into the lost name of an Earth
year. It therefore does not solve the Earth-correlation problem.

### Communication boundary

An inactive ligature has no propagating signal mode. Light, radio, neutrinos,
gravitational waves, and quantum correlations do not enter one shore and emerge
at the other.

Ordinary records and every high-bandwidth message cross inside a committed
carriage. A small **letter carriage** can carry urgent data faster than the next
passenger or freight schedule, but it still needs:

- a matched countercarriage;
- a complete mode cycle;
- both shores' precommitted operation;
- the route's full crossing time; and
- localization before its record can be read.

There is no partial-progress signal. Before the route-depth decision time, remote
readiness, detuning, cargo, equipment loss, or operator choice cannot change any
measurable local impedance, energy draw, phase, noise, or outcome distribution at
the other shore.

At the decision time, a reserved cycle exposes one **interlock outcome**:
exchange, refusal, or fault. An operator can deliberately encode one bit by
participating or refusing, but doing so consumes a prearranged mode cycle and
takes the route's full depth. Repeating the act can technically carry a very slow
bitstream. It is almost never faster or cheaper than placing a complete record in
a letter carriage.

The interlock reveals no cargo, text, live telemetry, or intermediate state.
After commitment, intervention cannot send an arbitrary update or change any
pre-decision observable. Deliberate sabotage may force the already scheduled
terminal interlock toward fault, risking both carriages and shores. Under a
prearranged code this is one destructive bit chosen during passage, observed only
at the fixed decision time. It cannot carry text, a new schedule, a recall, or a
safe continuation message. A dead shore may remain old news for an entire missed
exchange.

Interstellar communication is therefore faster than ordinary light through
scheduled ligature modes: normally as a physical record in a carriage, and at
most as one interlock status per otherwise empty cycle. It is never instantaneous,
continuous, high-bandwidth without a carriage, or free of traffic control.
Families, courts, markets, and militaries live by mailfall rather than a shared
present.

### Thermal and human passage

A bilocally supported carriage cannot use an external radiator. Its total
stress-energy boundary must remain closed. Transit vessels therefore carry cold
stores, phase-change mass, regenerative air systems, and large thermal margins.
They reduce reactor output, stop nonessential industry, and lock large moving
masses before commitment.

People experience ordinary days or weeks inside. Clocks tick, bodies age, food is
consumed, and heat accumulates. The carriage may rotate internally if its mass
compensation system can keep exterior multipoles within tolerance, but many
vessels use low-motion transit arrangements and restore comfortable rotation
after localization.

Each carriage carries its own comoving tetrad, internal Hamiltonian, and clock
ensemble through the paired assignment state. Normalization matches the sequence
lapse and gravitational redshift of the two assignment branches closely enough
that the carriage accumulates one internal proper-time history. A mismatch appears
as phase shear, clock disagreement, heat, and eventual spill; it does not create
two versions of the occupants.

Ligature passage is not suspended animation. Therapeutic hypometabolism may
reduce medical or life-support burden for selected travelers, but it does not
erase experienced duration or make century-long stasis routine.

### Failure modes

- **Refusal:** A shore declines sequence lock. Both carriages remain local, and
  the other shore learns the outcome only at the normal route-depth decision
  time.
- **Misweighing:** The invariant totals or multipoles do not close. Trim systems
  absorb small differences; large ones prevent commitment.
- **Snapback:** Early loss of drive returns both carriages to their original
  shores with mechanical and thermal stress.
- **Spill:** An overfast sweep or unstable internal mass plan leaves the supported
  mode, producing radiation, impulse, heat, and material loss at one or both
  shores.
- **Shorefall:** Completion becomes incoherent across the two permitted shores.
  The ledger closes, but the carriages may arrive as plasma or fragments. Unitary
  normalization and conserved global charges permit only the original total
  carriage support. Shorefall can partition or destroy it; duplication is
  physically excluded.
- **Momentum debt:** Exchange succeeds but the destination frame is wrong. A
  saturated weighhouse can receive the carriage on a destructive trajectory.
- **Thermal overrun:** The closed carriage exceeds its heat budget before
  localization.
- **Carriage chatter:** Sloshing fluids, rotating machinery, active weapons,
  adaptive structures, or unsecured cargo change the exterior multipoles faster
  than trim systems can compensate.
- **Mode crowding:** Two exchanges attempt one harmonic and interfere.
- **False shore selection:** Corrupted phase tables at a hub commit a carriage to
  the wrong valid ligature only if that shore has a precommitted matched
  countercarriage. It cannot appear at an arbitrary coordinate.
- **Operational de-rating:** Aging equipment, heat limits, damaged clocks, lost
  ballast, or inadequate phase tables reduce certified mass or increase depth on
  human timescales without changing the natural conjugacy.
- **Secular route fade:** Stellar and sequence evolution weaken the natural
  coupling over centuries or millennia. Safe mass shrinks and route depth grows
  before traffic ends.
- **Abrupt anomalous loss:** A well-characterized coupling disappears faster than
  secular models allow. Such a loss is rare evidence requiring investigation,
  not routine actuarial weather.
- **Dead shore:** The ligature remains physically possible but one weighhouse no
  longer exists.

Every failure has one mass-energy history. Ligature transit never duplicates a
person, exports infinite resources, creates two authentic arrival histories, or
opens a Manifold path.

### Identity through passage

No scan, destructive readout, transmitted blueprint, or destination copy is part
of a crossing. The carriage's state evolves continuously and unitarily from one
local support to the other while its inhabitants accumulate ordinary proper
time. Known-world law therefore treats the arriving traveler as the same person.

Religious and philosophical objections remain possible. They do not change the
physical evidence: no abandoned original, new duplicate, or interruption of
internal causal history has ever been observed in a successful passage.

### Mechanism firewall

Ligature transit is ordinary established infrastructure and not a seventh
forbidden system.

- It does not invalidate cohesion or propagate through material continuity.
- It transports finite living bodies without morphogenic remapping.
- It preserves two named shores, one global state evolution, and one causal
  history. It is not Manifold nonconvergence.
- Its sealed carriage retains heat by ordinary isolation, not radiant return.
- Its synchronized machinery follows a precommitted schedule; it does not couple
  prediction error through people.
- It creates no near-Planck state and no exotic material residue.

## Materials and fabrication

Future materials are excellent rather than invulnerable.

- High-performance steels, light alloys, ceramics, metallic glasses, fiber and
  gradient composites, carbon-rich coatings, and active thermal surfaces are
  common.
- Practically cooled superconductors support fusion magnets, motors, storage,
  transmission, and sensors. Ambient-condition superconductivity is not a
  universal commodity.
- Self-healing seals, polymers, coatings, and matrices repair bounded damage.
  They do not regrow missing pressure hulls.
- Micromachines and nanostructures are ordinary inside factories, catalysts,
  diagnostics, and medicine. Free-ranging universal assemblers are absent.
- Fabricators consume certified feedstock, tooling, energy, calibration, and
  time. They cannot reproduce every semiconductor, reactor blanket, optical
  standard, biological catalyst, or route component from raw dirt.

Post-Valec construction favors visible seams, heterogeneous layers, sacrificial
joints, independent foundations, removable carriers, local stores, and material
genealogy records. Resistance to characterization and vulnerability after
establishment are separate properties: an amorphous material may be difficult to
map and still fail violently once its stored stress becomes accessible.

## Computation and machine agency

Powerful machine competence is mundane. Machine personhood is not assumed.

Local systems translate, diagnose, navigate, schedule, model structures, manage
fabrication, and operate remote facilities for long periods without instruction.
Far-system probes can reprioritize science, isolate faults, and preserve
themselves across light-time. Quantum and photonic processors solve important
specialized problems without abolishing computational complexity.

No computer predicts an unrestricted society, solves a Manifold, or simulates a
person with legally presumed continuity. A model of a mind is evidence about a
mind, not automatically that person. Whether any artificial system is conscious
remains a scientific and legal dispute; competence and eloquence do not settle it.

Safety-critical infrastructure keeps nonadaptive fallbacks, local shutdown
authority, independently powered witnesses, and physically severable links.
Agreement history makes adaptive-system provenance a public interest rather than
an implementation detail.

## Sensing and communication

Known-world sensors are precise but never omniscient.

- Coherent optical links provide enormous in-system throughput at unavoidable
  light-time.
- Radio remains valuable for acquisition, emergency broadcast, bad weather, and
  low-complexity fallbacks.
- Navigation combines stellar and pulsar references, inertial systems, laser
  ranging, and locally maintained clocks.
- Large installations perform deep radar tomography, neutrino observation,
  gravitational sensing, calorimetry, and long-baseline interferometry.
- Medical and industrial systems can infer preparation, stress, composition, and
  physiology. They cannot read arbitrary private semantic thought or see through
  unlimited mass.

Quantum correlations support measurement and cryptographic practice but do not
carry messages. Between systems, communication travels by ligature carriage or
ordinary light. The faster carriage is physical transport with route depth,
counterweight, schedules, customs, and failure risk.

## Habitats, atmospheres, and ecology

No inhabited environment is perfectly closed.

Stations recover most water and atmospheric gases but continually lose trace
materials, catalysts, nutrients, organisms, and equipment. Durable habitats use
several pressure districts, local stores, independent shutdown, and more than one
heat path. Water, regolith, and distance remain the principal radiation shields.

Terraforming is not quick or complete. Avara's atmosphere is maintained by
measurement, volatile handling, ecological stewardship, and replenishment. It is
not conjured from an airless moon. A native biosphere is not automatically edible,
compatible, or safe to alter.

## Medicine and longevity

Ordinary medicine includes cultured organs, tissue patches, somatic gene repair,
advanced prostheses, neural rehabilitation, cancer control, immune matching, and
microbiome management. Germline intervention is conservative and regulated.

Healthy adulthood commonly extends beyond a century. Well-supported people may
live roughly 120 to 140 years; exceptional lives approach 160 with increasing
cognitive, vascular, and social cost. Infection, radiation, trauma, organ failure,
and neglect still kill. Death cannot be reversed from an archive.

Therapeutic hypometabolism can support surgery, disaster evacuation, and selected
journeys for hours to weeks. It is not proven century-scale suspension and does
not solve the Founding transit.

Total-boundary recovery mantles are specialist morphogenic medicine. Autonomous
conscious descendants are persons under dispute, not ordinary clinical tools.

## Capabilities that are not established

Unless a later adopted entry deliberately earns them, the setting has no:

- reactionless thrust;
- gravity shielding or artificial gravity fields;
- inertial dampers;
- arbitrary point-to-point teleportation;
- route without a conjugate shore and matched countercarriage;
- instantaneous or unscheduled high-bandwidth faster-than-light communication;
- time travel or selectable prophecy;
- force shield detached from matter, fields, power, and heat;
- matter replicator or universal autonomous nanotechnology;
- bulk antimatter economy;
- macroscopic negative-energy reservoir;
- traversable wormhole or controllable warp metric;
- vacuum-energy extraction;
- perfect stealth while a high-power spacecraft operates;
- omniscient scanner;

- perfect closed ecology;
- quick planetary terraforming;
- complete quantum gravity;
- controllable Manifold;
- or shared hidden force that explains all six forbidden systems.

## Authoring rule

Every future technology should state:

1. its ordinary scientific foothold;
2. the exact discovery that the known world added;
3. what people can observe, prepare, control, and scale;
4. its mass-energy, momentum, heat, information, and time account;
5. its required infrastructure and human labor;
6. its characteristic failure and countermeasure;
7. its civil ancestor or reason no such ancestor exists;
8. what remains disputed in-world; and
9. which convenient capability it explicitly does not provide.

The world becomes coherent not by explaining every mystery, but by refusing to
let any mystery erase its own consequences.
