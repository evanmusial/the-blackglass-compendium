# Articles of Bodily Dissent

## Status and origin

The Articles of Bodily Dissent are the principal multijurisdictional framework
governing involuntary Agreement coupling. They enter the Compact-relative record
in Year +205 after the Nacre Hall proceedings made it impossible to treat
convergent testimony as ordinary coercion, equipment interference, or collective
panic.

The Articles are not an amendment to the Sixth Compact of Kharos. They borrow
its inspection and custody procedures where useful while preserving a separate
offense, evidentiary model, and enforcement authority.

## Protected interests

The Articles recognize **bodily dissent** as the capacity of a person to sustain
and express a sensorimotor or communicative response that is not physically
forced toward an adaptive lattice's reference behavior, whether that behavior
comes from another person, a machine controller, or a retained or simulated
model.

The protection is narrower than a right never to be persuaded, coordinated,
trained, or contradicted. It addresses active reciprocal systems that make
independent response physiologically or mechanically unsustainable.

Under the Articles:

- consent to coupling must be specific, time-bounded, and revocable through a
  physically independent disconnect;
- emergency participation does not authorize later military, clinical, or
  investigative reuse of a participant's coupling model;
- safety-critical facilities must retain one-way shutdown paths and at least one
  control observer outside the reciprocal carrier;
- a person may not be compelled to remain connected merely because disconnection
  reduces group performance;
- unanimous testimony produced under suspected convergence is preserved but may
  not be treated as independent corroboration;
- and survivors are entitled to staged discord rehabilitation rather than rapid
  reintegration into another synchronized environment.

## Variance keepers

The Articles establish the protected role commonly called the **variance
keeper**: an observer, instrument, or control cell deliberately excluded from
the shared feedback architecture. A variance keeper must possess an independent
clock, stimulus source, recording path, and physical means of disconnection.

The role is not ceremonial opposition. Its value is that it can disagree for
reasons the active lattice has not already absorbed.

Facilities may rotate the duty because long isolation has psychological and
professional costs. They may not replace physical independence with a software
process running on the same coupled control substrate.

## Evidence and rehabilitation

Courts applying the Articles examine covariance before content. Identical words
do not alone prove contamination; impossible matching among response latency,
sensor noise, hesitation, and residual error supports a finding of active or
recent convergence.

Medical recovery uses **discord rehabilitation**: small, controlled exposures to
safe disagreement, independent timing, asymmetric tasks, and unsynchronized
social contact. Placing several survivors into synchronized care too early can
reenact conditioned convergence after the original carrier has been removed.
That recurrence is learned injury, not active coupling or contagion.

## Jurisdictional boundary

The Articles do not govern Rzez possession, Plain Thing personhood, Manifold
quarantine, ordinary propaganda, shared cultural belief, or every coordinated
machine. Their subject is involuntary reciprocal coupling and its durable
neurophysiological and evidentiary consequences.
