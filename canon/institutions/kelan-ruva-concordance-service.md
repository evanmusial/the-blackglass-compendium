# Kelan–Ruva Concordance Service

## Purpose

The Kelan–Ruva Concordance Service is a distributed scientific and emergency
service that compares independent clocks, ranging observations, sensor records,
and archive custody across [Kharos](../places/kharos.md),
[Avara](../places/avara.md), and [Odran](../places/odran.md).

It exists to determine whether reports that disagree are erroneous, fraudulent,
locally damaged, or mutually authentic in a way that ordinary chronology cannot
reconcile. It can authenticate a contradiction without deciding which history
is metaphysically primary.

## Distributed design

The Service deliberately has no single master clock or sole archive. Each world
collects and signs records locally, and Kharos, Avara, and Odran are coequal
custodians rather than headquarters and subordinate stations. Ordinary
high-volume exchange uses the Kelan–Ruva optical backbone. Physical link
severance is reserved for local shard conditions or infrastructure suspected of
participating in establishment; receiving evidence of a distant rupture does not
spread one. Independent observation and later comparison remain normal practice.

Its duties include:

- maintaining separated time standards and known-path ranging baselines;
- receiving exterior sensor records from suspected rupture sites;
- assembling concordance bundles without harmonizing away incompatible facts;
- advising rescue and quarantine authorities on single-chart operations;
- preserving every authenticated casualty or survivor chronology;
- and distinguishing suspected Manifold signatures from instrument fault,
  propaganda, route disturbance, or Rzez damage.

## Authority boundary

The Service is not a Manifold operator, weapons command, universal court, or
successor to the Compact Commission. It possesses no demonstrated way to create,
aim, close, or predict a rupture. A positive finding can justify emergency
isolation under the relevant local authority, but does not itself establish
that a presence intended an attack.

No confirmed Manifold rupture is placed within the Kelan–Ruva system. The
Service's home-system baselines are valuable precisely because they can remain
outside a distant incident and compare what returns.

## Deliberately unresolved

- when the Service acquired its present form;
- whether it is a treaty body, federation of observatories, or emergency compact;
- which authority may order the optical backbone disconnected;
- how its findings are admitted in courts beyond Kharos;
- and how many external stations participate in a full concordance bundle.
