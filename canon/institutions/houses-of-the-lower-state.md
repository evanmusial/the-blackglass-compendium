# Houses of the Lower State

## Identity

The Houses of the Lower State are a small, decentralized family of cults that
worship [The Rehearsal](../../entries/volume-04/book-01/d-1-01-the-rehearsal.md)
and the power demonstrated by the [Apsis Discharge](../events/apsis-discharge.md).
They possess no common priesthood, headquarters, authenticated succession, or
single doctrine. *Houses* is both their preferred local form and the name used
by investigators for networks that share Apsis-derived scripture.

Their common claim is that ordinary matter is not creation's intended substance
but a long-lived excited phase. The Rehearsal is sacred because human hands
briefly forced matter toward first conditions and allowed another possible
ground state to appear. Most Houses call the instrument **the Proof**.

Their best-known sentence is:

> *The universe is not ending. It is settling.*

This is theology, not an accepted physical conclusion.

## Origin in the Apsis record

The earliest Houses form around leaked Directorate telemetry, witness packets,
particle-spectrum reconstructions, and images of the ruined range. The first
adherents include bereaved staff families, dismissed analysts, range salvagers,
and people who encounter edited recordings without their technical context.

The founding archive is irreducibly mixed. Some packets are authentic. Some are
correct reconstructions from incomplete measurements. Others omit scale,
replace detector saturation with a smooth curve, or add a long-lived fragment
where the source recorded only an unidentified track. The Houses' existence and
use of those records are established. No House archive proves that a catalytic
seed escaped.

The overwhelming scale of the firing matters as much to the faith as its
possible consequence. Devotional accounts describe the forty-seven-millisecond
train as a moment in which every ordinary category of matter became provisional.
Spectral plots are copied as icons. Broken diagrams of the convergence crown
appear above meeting places. Technical language is recited without requiring
that every worshipper understand the underlying model.

## Shared sacred vocabulary

The Houses use several terms more confidently than physicists do:

- **the Proof** — The Rehearsal as artifact and demonstration;
- **the First Revelation** — the Apsis Discharge;
- **the Taking** — capture of a catalytic seed by dense matter;
- **the Flowering** — conversion and daughtering of the first reservoir;
- **the Succession** — a self-sustaining chain with strangelet reproduction
  number greater than one;
- **the Settlement** — the eventual conversion of all matter the chain can
  reach; and
- **the Performance** — either the deliberate firing that begins the Succession
  or the full history of conversion, depending upon the House.

Physicists use *Taking*, *Flowering*, and *Succession* conditionally. The cultic
*Settlement* is not the technical settlement stage of The Agreement and does
not imply a shared mechanism. It is an eschatological name for a baryonic phase
transition unfolding through ordinary causal contact.

## The Waiting Houses

The **Waiting Houses** teach that a true lower state needs no human assistance.
If Apsis released one viable seed, or if another natural or secret event has done
so, the Succession is already proceeding somewhere beyond present observation.
Their duty is to wait, preserve the record, and refuse the arrogance of a second
firing.

Waiting Houses maintain pulsar catalogues, collect public cosmic-particle data,
and hold vigils when compact objects glitch, cool unexpectedly, or disappear
behind ordinary observational limits. Their conclusions are usually poor
astronomy, but the observations themselves may be meticulous. Some lawful
observatories accept their raw reports while rejecting their interpretation.

Their characteristic prayer is:

> *May it have fired already, far enough away that its light has not yet
> arrived.*

Most Waiting Houses neither possess weapon components nor seek them. Their
fatalism can still cause harm: members have abandoned care, inheritance,
maintenance, or political action in the belief that all ordinary futures are
already void. That harm does not make every House a weapons conspiracy.

## Open Yield

**Open Yield** is the principal activist schism. Its cells argue that waiting is
cowardice and that the Apsis firing was only a rehearsal. They seek to recover a
candidate seed, reconstruct an open-quench firing packet, or place a future
convergence crown near dense matter. They call deliberate initiation **bringing
forward the Performance**.

Open Yield is proscribed in most jurisdictions applying the
[Common Matter Interdict](common-matter-interdict.md), but the legal offense is
material preparation: trespass, procurement, sample trafficking, target
selection, and firing conspiracy. Members exploit public fear by selling
ordinary dense alloys as relic strangelets and harmless timing components as
pieces of the crown. Some cells are frauds. A fraudulent relic market can still
finance a genuine approach attempt.

Open Yield has no demonstrated access to a viable seed or complete Rehearsal
design. Its danger lies in persistent search under a doctrine that treats any
failure as permission to try again.

## Houses outside the schism

Not every House accepts the Waiting/Open Yield division. Some venerate the
forty-seven-millisecond firing but reject universal conversion. Some interpret
the lower state morally, as the end of hierarchy and private possession. Others
are grief fellowships built around Apsis deaths and use the cosmology only as a
language for irreversible loss.

Authorities often flatten these groups into one conspiracy. The error obscures
both protected belief and actual weapons preparation. It also gives Open Yield
the prestige of a movement much larger and more unified than it is.

## Relationship to Blackglass devotion

Some Houses treat the
[Blackglass Compendium](../../frame/blackglass-tradition.md) as a damaged record
of the First Revelation. Others reject it as a frightened institutional text
that witnessed the Proof without understanding it. Waiting Houses may preserve
Blackglass fragments because lawful archives discarded cultic sources; Open
Yield cells seek margins that might restore crown timing or seed custody.

The Houses are not a branch of the broader
[Blackglass devotional movements](blackglass-devotional-movements.md). They
worship The Rehearsal and the lower state, not necessarily the collection.
Overlap in members, relic markets, or language is historically important but
does not make the Compendium, strange matter, and the other forbidden systems
one revelation or one physical process.

## Physical and metaphysical boundary

No rite, text, image, recording, or shared declaration produced by a House can
initiate strangelet conversion. The faith does not spread by exotic physics.
Its members coordinate through ordinary persuasion, kinship, secrecy, and
imitation; their agreement is not evidence of The Agreement.

The Houses do not know whether strange matter is stable, whether daughtering can
produce `R_s > 1`, whether Apsis released a viable seed, or whether any present
astronomical anomaly marks a Taking. Their certainty is the point at which they
depart from the evidence.

Even under their preferred physical model, the Settlement is neither
instantaneous nor literally universal. Strangelets follow causal paths and can
convert only accessible baryonic matter. Photons, dark matter, vacuum, matter
inside inaccessible black holes, and regions beyond a permanent cosmological
horizon do not become strangelets. Many Houses preserve the phrase *entire
universe* because liturgy is not a boundary condition.

## Deliberately unresolved

- the identity of the first House and which founding telemetry it possessed;
- how many Houses remain active in the current era;
- whether any former Directorate member became an early theologian;
- which Waiting House observations are scientifically useful despite their
  interpretation;
- whether Open Yield has ever crossed from fraudulent relic trade into custody
  of genuine Apsis hardware; and
- whether a House would recognize the first credible evidence of Succession or
  reinterpret any result to preserve belief.
