# First Quarry Houses

## Role

The First Quarry Houses are a federation of mining guilds dominating early
Meridian mineral extraction. They employ Varek Ilyr and provide the industrial
setting for the Gentle Opening and early Rzez development.

## Institutional legacy

The Houses preserve detailed output records but poor casualty records. Modern
reconstruction associates at least twenty-nine early collapses with uncontrolled
cohesion propagation.

They privately create isolation protocols decades before publicly admitting
that a field can propagate beyond its intended boundary. This establishes an
important recurring Compendium pattern: industrial knowledge and safety practice
can precede political acknowledgment of danger.

## The working record

Later comparative accounts distinguish the Houses' unit of completed work from
the physical system that continued bearing its consequences. The Gentle Opening
payroll records payment for an achieved extraction. The later support failure
requires a separate account of the surrounding works and the people still
using them. The Rzez manuscript's
[extraction and support account](../../entries/volume-01/book-07/a-7-01-the-rzez.md#the-extraction-account-and-the-support-account)
keeps the two dates and populations explicit.

The retained isolation memorandum likewise governs the next use of separated
stone and the location of sleeping quarters. It is an operating instruction
whose scope exceeds the moment of extraction. Later Compact procedures build
their own forensic and receiving-capacity requirements around that broader
problem; those later standards are not attributed retrospectively to the Houses.

## Unresolved questions

Whether the Houses were one polity's guilds or a transnational federation
remains unresolved. So does what they knew before the Gentle Opening and who
converted their industrial equipment into the Deren Keep weapon. No settled
answer identifies whether any House preserved prohibited lineages after the
Compact.
