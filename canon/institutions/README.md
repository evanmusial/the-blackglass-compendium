## Named organizations retained in the Rzez entry

- **Crown Army of Merovar:** confirmed operator at Deren Keep.
- **Veyran League:** rival port-city coalition accused in the Glass Fleet event.
- **Marrowhouse Clinical Laboratory:** later-dissolved military medical facility.
- **Kharosi Assembly:** body before which Olan Vey is assassinated.
- **Forty-First Valec Commission:** source of the accepted Valec field record.

Polities and armies are temporarily indexed here when their institutional role
matters more than their geography. The taxonomy can split them later if several
entries demonstrate the need.

The three adopted post-Compact frameworks are independent of the Sixth Compact.
Shared investigative procedure does not merge radiant-enclosure law,
autonomy-denial law, common-matter protection, created-person law, Manifold
quarantine, or Rzez prohibition.

The religious movements likewise remain distinct. Blackglass devotion, Rzez
liberation theology, the Houses of the Lower State, and Concordant Temple taboo
may exchange texts and imagery without implying common doctrine or common
weapon physics.
