# Sixth Compact of Kharos

## Origin

The Sixth Compact is the multinational treaty framework created after the
destruction of Valec. Valec defines Year Zero of its calendar.

The ordinal “Sixth” implies five earlier Compacts or agreements, but their names,
subjects, continuity, and relationship to the Sixth are not yet defined.

## Rzez prohibitions

The Compact prohibits more than construction. The Rzez entry identifies:

- manufacture, possession, reconstruction, instruction, or simulation;
- computational modeling of planetary propagation;
- cohesion-focusing cores above industrial tolerance;
- complete field equations and emitter synchronization protocols;
- unsupervised study or import of Valec material;
- and symbolic or identity-based Rzez experiments.

Industrial thresholds may exist under tight control, creating a deliberate
tension between absolute language and necessary defensive or civil practice.

## Governing dilemma

Isolation can prevent propagation but can also destroy infrastructure and enable
false-alarm warfare. The Compact therefore requires independent confirmation
before regional disconnection except when delay would create planetary risk.

This conflict—when to cut and when to investigate—is a defining political legacy
of the Rzez.

## Unresolved questions

- What were the first five Compacts?
- Which states signed, rejected, or enforce the Sixth?
- How are tightly controlled industrial thresholds licensed?
- What rights do accused researchers possess?
- Does the Compact govern only Rzez technology or a wider class of forbidden
  engineering?
