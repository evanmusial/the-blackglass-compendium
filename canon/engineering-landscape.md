# Three-System Engineering Landscape

## Status and purpose

The Rzez, [The Unhidden](../concepts/developing/the-unhidden.md), and
[The Manifold](../concepts/developing/the-manifold.md) establish the collection's
first three contrasting engineering systems. This note promotes only the facts
that must be reusable while the latter two subjects remain developing concepts.

The three are **not expressions of one force**. Kharosi scholarship often places
them beneath the broad civic language of continuity because each creates a
different crisis of persistence, identity, or evidence. That legal and cultural
umbrella is not a grand unified physical theory.

## The three systems

| Subject | Primary system | Governing manipulation | Provenance | Defining consequence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **The Rzez** | `entropic` | It invalidates selected relationships by which existing matter remains structurally continuous. | `known-human-lineage` | A thing can remain apparently intact after it has become unable to remain whole. |
| **The Unhidden** | `morphogenic` | It directs how living matter assigns form, function, and organismal continuity during growth and repair. | `known-human-lineage` | A conscious, distributed body is wholly exposed to ordinary space, with no hidden true form or protected central organ. |
| **The Manifold** | `causal-topological` | It prevents locally coherent adjacencies and event sequences from composing one global geometry and history. | `extrinsic-unknown` | A bounded place or interval can support precise observations that cannot all belong to one possible world. |

These classifications answer different questions from military consequence. The
Rzez has a reconstructable industrial lineage. The Unhidden descends from human
regenerative medicine even where its military conversion is concealed. The
Manifold has no known originating culture, construction history, or recoverable
ordinary worldline; **suspected epoch denial** names a human interpretation of
its effects, not its purpose.

### The Manifold is not an arsenal

“Use” is misleading when applied to human contact with The Manifold. Humans
cannot build it, program shards, select a resolved outcome, or command an
associated presence. A few programs claim that years of preparation let them
provoke incidence or bias an approximate spatial and temporal envelope. None has
shown repeatable control.

Credible attempts are rare because unintended results are ordinary: the rupture
may take the wrong site or epoch, include the operators or their command chain,
open a corridor instead of denying one, preserve the event it was meant to
erase, or bring an associated presence into the boundary. A suspected anchor is
therefore not a weapon in storage. It is an opportunity to begin an incident
whose eventual target cannot be guaranteed.

Whether an associated presence uses The Manifold strategically is unknown. A
humanly important site may be target, transit surface, quarantine wall,
construction boundary, message, accident, or debris.

## Mechanism firewall

Future entries must preserve the following distinctions:

- Rzez damage is entropic relationship failure inside one ordinary geometry and
  chronology.
- An Unhidden organism is finite, mass-accountable living matter. Its total
  boundary is physiological, not topological.
- A Manifold rupture is globally nonconvergent. Its associated presence or
  presences are not Plain Things, and neither subject explains the other.
- Shared terms such as *boundary*, *continuity*, *identity*, *distributed*, and
  *separation* do not establish a common origin, mechanism, vulnerability, or
  metaphysics.
- No interaction among the three systems is canon until a manuscript produces
  an incident and evidence model that require one.

The Rzez subtracts continuity. The Unhidden redistributes the work of remaining
alive. The Manifold prevents continuity from resolving into one account.

## Kharosi continuity jurisprudence

Kharos is the region's most visible venue for the consequences of all three
systems, but it is not their universal owner or point of origin.

| Crisis | Kharosi response | Jurisdictional boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Structural continuity under the Rzez | Compact law, cohesion forensics, segmented construction, and suspected-unmaking procedure. | The Sixth Compact remains Rzez-specific. |
| Embodied identity under The Unhidden | Boundary medicine, tissue-lineage records, created-person hearings, surrender doctrine, and humane-custody standards. | A conscious organism is not automatically Compact contraband or military property. |
| Historical and spatial consistency under The Manifold | Independent clocks, concordance bundles, rim quarantine, plural-evidence rules, and single-chart rescue doctrine. | Human authorities do not possess, aim, or understand The Manifold merely because they study its aftermath. |

The legal systems may borrow procedure from one another. Borrowed procedure
does not merge their mandates. In particular, emergency isolation expertise from
the Compact Commission does not give it automatic authority over created persons
or every alleged Manifold site.

## Kelan–Ruva defensive geography

The [Kelan–Ruva system](places/kelan-ruva-system.md) gives the three responses
different physical homes:

- [Kharos](places/kharos.md) supplies civilian medicine, public courts,
  scholarship, and the ordinary social consequences of dangerous engineering.
- [Avara](places/avara.md) supplies low-gravity clinical scale, atmosphere and
  life-support engineering, recovery facilities, and some defensive industry.
- [Odran](places/odran.md) supplies physically separated material standards,
  chronometry, dry laboratories, ranging stations, and redundant archives.
- Stations farther into the system can extend observation baselines without
  becoming new population centers or establishing the mechanism of interstellar
  routes.

Kharos, Avara, and Odran compare records through a high-capacity optical
backbone, but each maintains independent timebases and archives. The network is
designed to be physically severable when local shard conditions implicate a link
in establishment. Receiving a record of a distant rupture does not transmit the
rupture. Agreement after independent collection is more useful to concordance
science than one master clock whose corruption would make every record fail
together.

## Defenses that do not unify

The three hazards reward conflicting responses:

- Suspected Rzez propagation favors separation, sacrificial joints, and rapid
  disconnection of continuous utilities.
- A hostile open form can be impeded by occlusion, compression, desiccation, or
  hostile chemistry, while a surrendering Plain Thing requires wet,
  non-occluding life support. The same sealed cell can be containment or slow
  execution depending on its environment.
- Local Manifold incidence favors decoupling synchronized or route-linked
  infrastructure suspected of participating in establishment and evacuating
  along one continuously observed path. A mature
  rupture favors quarantine, one ingress path, continuous tethering, and teams
  that do not divide into incompatible charts.

Kelan–Ruva engineering therefore favors **isolated redundancy**: modular wet
services rather than one uninterrupted clinical network, independent clocks
whose records are compared after collection, and inhabited districts able to
survive when bridges, data trunks, or atmospheric services are intentionally
cut.

## Boundaries retained for later entries

- No confirmed Manifold rupture is placed in the Kelan–Ruva system.
- The Unhidden's military conversion and first deployment occur away from
  Kharos; their states, dates, and locations remain unresolved.
- The associated Manifold presence has no assigned species, civilization,
  motive, authorship, number, or strategic doctrine.
- Ordinary interstellar routes are not defined by Manifold physics.
- Kharos is not humanity's cradle, a regional capital, or the sole authority on
  forbidden engineering.
- Volume numbers, book numbers, permanent entry IDs, and final legal statuses for
  The Unhidden and The Manifold remain unassigned.
