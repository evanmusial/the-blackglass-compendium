# Five-System Engineering Landscape

## Status and purpose

The Rzez, [The Unhidden](../concepts/developing/the-unhidden.md),
[The Manifold](../concepts/developing/the-manifold.md),
[The Closed Night](../entries/volume-02/book-04/b-4-01-the-closed-night.md), and
[The Agreement](../entries/volume-03/book-06/c-6-01-the-agreement.md) establish
the collection's current contrasting systems.

The Closed Night and The Agreement are adopted entries with established
authorial status. The Rzez remains the complete founding working manuscript.
The Unhidden and The Manifold remain developing concepts from which only the
narrow facts listed in the earlier integration record have entered shared canon.

The five are **not expressions of one force**. Kharosi scholarship often places
them beneath broad civic language of continuity, persistence, and agency because
each creates a crisis ordinary law must address. That civic umbrella is not a
grand unified physical theory.

## The five systems

| Subject | Primary system | Governing manipulation | Provenance | Defining consequence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **The Rzez** | `entropic` | Invalidates selected relationships by which existing matter remains structurally continuous. | `known-human-lineage` | A thing can remain apparently intact after becoming unable to remain whole. |
| **The Unhidden** | `morphogenic` | Directs how living matter assigns form, function, and organismal continuity during growth and repair. | `known-human-lineage` | A conscious distributed body is wholly exposed to ordinary space, with no hidden true form or protected central organ. |
| **The Manifold** | `causal-topological` | Prevents locally coherent adjacencies and event sequences from composing one global geometry and history. | `extrinsic-unknown` | A bounded place or interval can support precise observations that cannot all belong to one possible world. |
| **The Closed Night** | `radiative` | Makes electromagnetic transport direction-selective across a powered, anchored enclosure. | `known-human-lineage` | An intact place can receive light and energy while becoming unable to return a signal or shed its own heat. |
| **The Agreement** | `neurocybernetic` | Redistributes prediction error among actively coupled nervous systems and machine controllers. | `known-human-lineage` | Separate conscious people and nominally independent machines can lose the ability to sustain different actions or errors. |

These classifications answer different questions from military consequence. The
Rzez, The Unhidden, The Closed Night, and The Agreement have reconstructable
human lineages in quarrying, medicine, heat recovery, and cooperative control.
The Manifold has no known originating culture, construction history, or ordinary
worldline; **suspected epoch denial** names a human reading of its effects, not
its demonstrated purpose.

### The Manifold is not an arsenal

“Use” remains misleading when applied to human contact with The Manifold. Humans
cannot build it, program shards, select a resolved outcome, or command an
associated presence. A few programs claim that years of preparation let them
provoke incidence or bias an approximate envelope. None has shown repeatable
control.

This boundary becomes more important now that The Closed Night and The Agreement
are genuine human arsenals. A Closed Night anchor or Agreement carrier can be
manufactured, possessed, inspected, and prohibited. A suspected Manifold anchor
is an opportunity to begin an incident whose eventual target cannot be
guaranteed.

## Mechanism firewall

Future entries must preserve the following distinctions:

- Rzez damage is entropic relationship failure inside one ordinary geometry and
  chronology.
- An Unhidden organism is finite, mass-accountable living matter. Its total
  boundary is physiological, not topological or radiative.
- A Manifold rupture is globally nonconvergent. Its associated presence or
  presences are not Plain Things, and neither subject explains the other.
- A Closed Night is an active ordinary-space radiative enclosure. It does not
  erase energy, halt time, make its interior impossible, or prevent matter from
  crossing.
- An Agreement lattice couples prediction and correction. It does not transmit
  arbitrary beliefs, merge minds, spread through recordings, or cause truly
  isolated instruments to agree.
- Shared terms such as *boundary*, *continuity*, *identity*, *distributed*,
  *agreement*, *closure*, and *convergence* do not establish common origin,
  mechanism, vulnerability, or metaphysics.
- No interaction among the five systems is canon until a manuscript produces an
  incident and evidence model that require one.

The Rzez subtracts continuity. The Unhidden redistributes the work of remaining
alive. The Manifold prevents continuity from resolving into one account. The
Closed Night retains what an enclosed system must discard. The Agreement makes
independence bear the cost of coordination.

## Kharosi continuity and autonomy jurisprudence

Kharos is the region's most visible venue for the consequences of all five
systems, but it is not their universal owner or point of origin.

| Crisis | Kharosi response | Jurisdictional boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Structural continuity under the Rzez | Compact law, cohesion forensics, segmented construction, and suspected-unmaking procedure. | The Sixth Compact remains Rzez-specific. |
| Embodied identity under The Unhidden | Boundary medicine, tissue-lineage records, created-person hearings, surrender doctrine, and humane-custody standards. | A conscious organism is not automatically Compact contraband or military property. |
| Historical and spatial consistency under The Manifold | Independent clocks, concordance bundles, rim quarantine, plural-evidence rules, and single-chart rescue doctrine. | Human authorities do not possess, aim, or understand The Manifold merely because they study its aftermath. |
| Thermal egress under The Closed Night | Inspectable return bands, permanent material exits, rescue authority, and the Articles of Open Radiance. | Lawful civil heat recovery is not hostile closure; the Compact Commission does not acquire general radiant authority. |
| Bodily dissent under The Agreement | Protected variance keepers, covariance forensics, contaminated-testimony rules, hard disconnects, and the Articles of Bodily Dissent. | Ordinary coordination, persuasion, and cultural consensus are not Agreement exposure. |

The legal systems may borrow procedure from one another. Borrowed procedure
does not merge their mandates. Emergency isolation expertise from the Compact
Commission does not give it automatic authority over created persons, radiative
industry, autonomy law, or every alleged Manifold site.

## Kelan–Ruva defensive geography

The [Kelan–Ruva system](places/kelan-ruva-system.md) gives the five responses
different physical homes. Humanity reached that system from Earth during the
long [Founding Interval](history/human-arrival-in-kelan-ruva.md): Avara preserves
the oldest continuously occupied refuge, while Kharos became the later home
world. That shared ancestry explains common biological and technical lineages;
it does not imply that any forbidden system came from a founding carrier.

- [Kharos](places/kharos.md) supplies civilian medicine, public courts,
  protected-dissent procedure, rehabilitation, scholarship, and the ordinary
  social consequences of dangerous engineering.
- [Avara](places/avara.md) supplies low-gravity clinical scale, atmosphere and
  thermal engineering, the Three-Hand Rescue tradition, recovery facilities,
  and some defensive industry.
- [Odran](places/odran.md) supplies physically separated material standards,
  independent controllers, clocks, vacuum calorimetry, ranging stations, and
  redundant archives.
- Stations farther into the system extend observation and thermal-reference
  baselines without becoming new population centers or defining interstellar
  route physics.

Kharos, Avara, and Odran compare records through a high-capacity optical
backbone, but each maintains independent timebases, control loops, and archives.
The network is physically severable when local shard conditions or reciprocal
carrier evidence implicate a link in establishment. Receiving a recording of a
distant Manifold or Agreement event is safe. Independent collection matters
because identical records gathered through one hidden carrier are one testimony,
not many.

## Defenses that do not unify

The five hazards reward different and sometimes conflicting responses:

- Suspected Rzez propagation favors separation, sacrificial joints, and rapid
  disconnection of continuous utilities.
- A hostile open form can be impeded by occlusion, compression, desiccation, or
  hostile chemistry, while a surrendering Plain Thing requires wet,
  non-occluding life support. The same sealed cell can be containment or slow
  execution depending on its environment.
- Local Manifold incidence favors decoupling implicated infrastructure and
  evacuating along one uninterrupted path. A mature rupture favors one ingress
  path, continuous tethering, and teams that do not divide into incompatible
  charts.
- A Closed Night favors motion, broad anchor disruption, material heat export,
  and multiple permanent egress paths. One narrow opening can become the lethal
  white seam that a rescue team must avoid.
- An Agreement favors hard disconnection, one-way channels, asynchronous clocks,
  randomized local stimuli, small isolated cells, and observers whose variance
  is physically protected. Reconnecting too early can restore learned
  convergence after the original carrier is gone.

Kelan–Ruva engineering therefore favors **isolated redundancy** with preserved
variance and material egress: modular wet services, independent clocks and
controllers, protected radiator paths, locally authoritative shutdowns, and
inhabited districts able to survive when bridges, data trunks, reciprocal
carriers, or atmospheric services are intentionally cut.

## Boundaries retained for later entries

- No confirmed Manifold rupture is placed in the Kelan–Ruva system.
- The Unhidden's military conversion and first deployment occur away from
  Kharos; their states, dates, and locations remain unresolved.
- The associated Manifold presence has no assigned species, civilization,
  motive, authorship, number, or strategic doctrine.
- Ordinary interstellar routes are not defined by Manifold physics.
- The Closed Night has no confirmed planetary deployment and does not enclose
  Kharos, Avara, or an entire world.
- The Agreement cannot bridge ordinary interstellar latency, propagate through
  records, or eliminate the continued private consciousness of its victims.
- The Opening of Pelar Habitat and Ninth Holding occur outside Kelan–Ruva; their
  withheld placement does not create a new celestial body in shared canon.
- Kharos is not humanity's cradle, a regional capital, or the sole authority on
  forbidden engineering.
- Volume numbers, book numbers, permanent entry IDs, and final legal statuses
  for The Unhidden and The Manifold remain unassigned.
