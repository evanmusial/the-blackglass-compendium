# Varek Ilyr

## Established by the Rzez draft

Varek Ilyr is a quarry engineer employed by the First Quarry Houses and the
figure traditionally credited with the first controlled cohesion field. He
proposes weakening cohesion along the boundary of a valuable crystal seam rather
than applying greater mechanical force.

The resulting trial, the Gentle Opening, extracts a thirty-seven-meter seam
intact. Seven workers later die when a neighboring support formation fails.

## Attributed legacy

Later sources place antiwar warnings and technical reflections in Ilyr's voice.
The draft explicitly disputes the authenticity of several such lines and of the
collected Ilyr fragments.

The historical person, the traditional credit, and the existence of attributed
writings are safe continuity anchors. The exact wording and authenticity of
those writings remain disputed in-world.

## Unresolved questions

- Did Ilyr understand propagation risk before the First Quarry Houses admitted it?
- Which attributed fragments are contemporary?
- Did he die in exile, or is the story of his self-disassembly purely legendary?
- How much of the later moral image of Ilyr was constructed by antiwar scholars?
