# Olan Vey

## Established by the Rzez draft

Olan Vey is an Engineer-Magistrate and jurist-engineer associated with early
Rzez controls. Seventeen years before the Sixth Compact, he dies while testifying
before the Kharosi Assembly.

The lectern and chair separate while the surrounding floor remains intact. His
connective tissue shows selective structural failure. The event is Grade B and
demonstrates that the technology may be tuned with alarming specificity.

His murder accelerates passage of the first comprehensive prohibition.

## Attributed words

The Rzez draft attributes several influential statements to Vey, including the
entry epigraph and his reported final warning that a principle cannot be
regulated by pretending it is an object.

The epigraph is associated with a later Kharosi reconstruction context and has
an uncertain date. Treat its attribution as part of Vey's legacy rather than an
unqualified transcript.
