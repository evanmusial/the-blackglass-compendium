# Compendium Glossary

These definitions are drawn from the Rzez working entry and the shared
provisional core of The Unhidden and The Manifold. Terms are shared only to the
extent stated here; similar language does not imply a shared mechanism, and
disputed theories remain disputed.

## Technical terms

### Characterization

The process by which a Rzez instrument establishes a resonant relationship with
a target and maps the continuities, interfaces, load paths, defects, and repair
systems that the target cannot afford to lose.

### Cohesion architecture

The relationships through which an object remains one persistent structure:
bonds, material domains, interfaces, load paths, redundancy, active repair, and
large-scale equilibrium.

### Cohesion graph theory

A classical model representing material units as nodes and their structural
relationships as edges. Rzez action is described as progressive edge
invalidation followed by nonlinear load redistribution.

### Cohesion sonography

A detection practice developed through the Bell Chamber experiments. It uses
acoustic divergence to identify loss of stable structural identity before visible
failure.

### Entropic bias

A localized condition that makes disordered configurations easier for a target
to enter. The target's ordinary stresses provide much of the force of collapse.

### Residual pattern

Instability remaining after exposure, potentially allowing the damaged target to
impose similar failure upon new repairs or attachments.

### Target inheritance

The formal name for transmission of a residual Rzez pattern into newly attached
material. Informally called **the butcher's stain**.

## Morphogenic and clinical terms

### Total boundary

A finite physiological arrangement in which every indispensable macroscopic
function is represented in tissue accessible through channels continuous with
the surrounding environment. It does not mean infinite surface area or
extra-dimensional anatomy.

### Total-Boundary Organism

The technical designation for a mature organism built upon total-boundary
physiology. Civil versions include patient-coupled scaffolds and recovery
mantles. Autonomous weaponized descendants are commonly called Plain Things.

### Open form

A body whose essential physiological surfaces remain directly accessible to the
environment rather than enclosed behind one protected exterior. **Open-form
combat organism** is the military classification associated with The Unhidden.

### Plain Thing

One conscious autonomous organism of The Unhidden's weaponized lineage; **Plain
Things** is the common plural. The name arose from testimony that the forms were
“plainly there” and later became derogatory by implying that complete bodily
visibility made a person a thing.

Clinical scaffolds attached to patients should not routinely be called Plain
Things.

### Wet cradle

A nursery, recovery, and maintenance system that supplies cultured tissue with
water, gases, nutrients, temperature control, waste removal, patterning
chemistry, and physical support. It is logistics and life support, not a hidden
body or remote controlling intelligence.

### Ward reflex

The reported tendency of a damaged or conflicted Plain Thing to resume the
therapeutic behavior of its medical ancestry: stabilizing wounded bodies,
sharing physiological support, or treating a battlefield as an open ward. Its
interpretation as developmental reversion, ethical choice, or military defect
remains disputed.

## Causal-topological and concordance terms

### Tachyon shard

An observational term for a sharply bounded Manifold-associated signature whose
detections cannot be reconciled with one subluminal worldline. It does not prove
that the phenomenon is a broken object, particle, probe, or ammunition.

### Manifold rupture

A bounded place or interval in which locally coherent geometry and causal order
fail to compose one global atlas and chronology. It is not an alternate universe,
illusion, or permission for arbitrary reality alteration.

### Boundary-lock

The stage at which a developing Manifold disturbance acquires a persistent rim
and ceases to admit one globally consistent geometry and history. Human
observers can sometimes identify the transition; they cannot therefore choose
the rupture's contents or final result.

### Associated presence

The cautious term for the genuinely extrinsic presence or presences observed in
connection with Manifold incidence, boundary-lock, or release. Association does
not establish creator, operator, motive, number, species, or civilization.

### Concordance bundle

A group of independently authenticated and internally coherent records whose
histories cannot all occupy one causal chronology. Each source retains its
ordinary evidentiary grade. Concordance records incompatibility; it is not a
sixth evidence grade and does not select one history as real.

### Single-chart doctrine

Rescue and survey practice inside a suspected rupture that keeps a team on one
continuously observed path, preserves physical tethering where possible, and
forbids division merely to compare routes. It accepts one locally coherent chart
for the duration of the operation instead of trying to reconcile the rupture
from within.

## Operational and forensic terms

### The Quiet

A suppression or localization of sound, vibration, and visible motion shortly
before terminal failure in many major Rzez events. Its accepted mechanical
explanation and more radical informational explanation remain unresolved.

### Suspected unmaking

The post-Orel doctrine that a structure which cannot be proven untouched must be
treated as lost. It protects against delayed collapse while enabling destructive
false-alarm warfare.

### The third tone

Acoustic disagreement in a repeatedly struck object, especially exposed steel.
To “hear the third tone” becomes a cultural expression for recognizing hidden
failure.

## Cultural and clinical terms

### Cohesion ledger

A Valeci memorial record preserving not only individuals and objects but the
relationships among them. The practice is associated with the belief that what
is remembered remains more difficult to butcher.

### Continuity dread

Persistent fear that objects, bodies, institutions, or relationships merely
appear intact. The condition may be traumatic, neurological, culturally
mediated, rational under entropic threat, or some combination.

### Hollow armor

An institution or authority that preserves appearance after losing function.
The phrase derives from the Night of Hollow Armor.

### Institutional Rzez

Originally a metaphor for social self-destruction caused by suspected
contamination. A forbidden school claims institutions literally possess cohesion
architectures that can be attacked. The literal interpretation is unverified.

### Valec stone

Something materially genuine but incapable of bearing structural or social
trust. The expression derives from post-destruction Valec material.
