# Odran

## Role in the home system

Odran is the airless inner moon of [Kharos](kharos.md). It is approximately
3,475 kilometres in diameter and orbits the planet at approximately 200,000
kilometres every 6.37 days. It is tidally locked to Kharos and has a mean solar
day of approximately 6.51 days.

The moon supports scattered industrial, forensic, observational, and
chronometric installations. It does not contain one continuous underground city.
Its value comes from physical separation, low environmental noise, vacuum, and
the ability to place one facility beyond the material and utility paths of
another.

Odran also belongs to the human founding interval in Kelan–Ruva. Its earliest
known infrastructure is contemporary with the broad arrival-and-refuge period
that produced continuous occupation on Avara and preceded durable settlement on
Kharos. This establishes historical depth without making Odran the oldest
continuously inhabited world; early occupation may have been intermittent.
The broader evidence and calendar limits are recorded in the
[human-arrival history](../history/human-arrival-in-kelan-ruva.md).

## Fixed sky and thermal geography

Kharos is fixed over Odran's near hemisphere and never visible from the far
hemisphere. Its disk is approximately 4.75 degrees across, nearly ten times the
apparent width of Kelan. It changes phase across Odran's 6.51-day solar cycle,
brightening near-side darkness when illuminated and producing deep, predictable
eclipse tracks during favorable alignments.

The fixed planet divides infrastructure by geometry without dividing it into two
simple cultures. Near-side sites gain direct optical contact, a large ranging
target, and shorter relay paths to Kharos. Far-side sites gain shielding from
planetary radio spill and a sky suitable for sensitive observation. Polar and
limb stations trade a low Kharos horizon against difficult terrain and relay
coverage. Ownership and political authority do not follow automatically from
any of these advantages.

Airlessness gives Odran no atmospheric buffer. Sunlit equipment receives direct
paired-star flux; shadowed equipment radiates to space, while Kharos and local
terrain obstruct part of that view. Away from polar, terrain, and eclipse
complications, daylight and darkness each persist for roughly 3.25 days and are
long operational states, not brief shifts. Habitats use thermal mass, buried or
shielded stores, independent radiator fields, and work schedules that avoid
treating the next sunrise as an emergency rescue.

Ruva remains visibly separate from Kelan through much of the paired-sun cycle,
but contributes little heat. Its red secondary illumination is useful for
calibration and surface orientation precisely because it is unequal. Odran's
vacuum and stable horizons let instruments separate the stars' motions from
Kharos's own barycentric motion more cleanly than weather-bound observations on
the planet.

## Founding interval and economic development

The first Odran installations were practical rather than monumental: ranging
references, transfer points, material stores, landing support, and observation
sites associated with Earth-origin arrival into the system. Surviving layers do
not yield one accepted first pad or exact date. Later construction repeatedly
reused favorable ground and replaced exposed equipment, while some isolated
markers may never have supported continuous crews.

Odran's proximity to Kharos, shallow gravity, and vacuum made it useful before
the planet became the demographic center. As durable Kharosi settlement grew,
Odran became a transfer and standards world rather than a competing open
habitat. By the middle-to-late Meridian Age, its vacuum processing, ranging, and
separated laboratories were integrated with Avaran yards and Kharosi markets.
The precise institutional order remains unresolved.

Post-Valec reconstruction did not invent separation on Odran. Distance between
pads, removable carriers, and local stores were already sensible on an airless
surface. Valec turned those conveniences into doctrine: no shared tunnel, power
spine, clock, or archive should be necessary for every facility's survival and
truth at once.

## Reference architecture

Odran's standards vaults and laboratories occupy independent pads with separate
power, landing, thermal-control, and data systems. No vault is the sole custodian
of an archive. Material references and records are copied to Kharos and Avara so
that isolation cannot become either accidental erasure or quiet revision.

Post-Valec practice treats a continuous foundation, service tunnel, or buried
utility spine as a possible propagation path. Facilities that need to exchange
samples use removable carriers and stand-off transfer points rather than
permanent physical continuity wherever practical.

Vacuum laboratories also maintain nearly complete calorimeters and independent
thermal references used to reconstruct Closed Night events. Their value is not
that Odran can contain a live enclosure safely, but that it can close an ordinary
energy ledger without hiding atmospheric or biological heat paths.

Odran's later reconstruction made the Calorimeter Deaths legible in the shared
chronology. It does not place the fatal experiment on Odran. No accepted source
assigns the event's location to the moon.

## Clocks and distant comparison

Odran maintains independent clocks, optical ranging stations, and exterior
sensor baselines for the Kelan–Ruva Concordance Service. Its vacuum and stable
instrument platforms make it useful for detecting timing and path residuals
that would be obscured by Kharosi weather or Avaran life-support activity.

The Odran clocks are not a master timebase. Their value is that they can disagree
independently. Records are authenticated locally and compared later with Kharos,
Avara, and more distant stations. Optical links are physically broken only when
local shard conditions or participating equipment implicate them in
establishment, not because a distant report is dangerous to receive.

Agreement forensics adds controlled-variance instruments to the same
architecture. Test controllers receive deliberately different local stimuli and
retain separate timing and recording paths. Matching residual error among those
systems is evidence that nominal independence has been compromised, not proof
that the measurements have become more reliable.

Odran also supports remote white-seam and gray-release rescue training using
ordinary radiant simulators. Live military anchors are not required and no
occupied, hostile, or full military Closed Night trial is assigned to the moon.

## Biological limits

Odran can store sealed nonconscious tissue lineages and dry components used by
boundary-medicine facilities. It is not a routine prison for conscious Plain
Things. For an environment-coupled open form, a failed pressure seal or deliberate
exposure to the surface would be an execution, not neutral containment.

The same fact creates continuing political pressure to use Odran as an easy
answer to Unhidden custody. Kharosi and Avaran humane-care doctrine rejects that
answer unless a later legal decision explicitly changes it.

## Deliberately unresolved

- the names, ownership, and exact locations of Odran installations;
- the exact dates, sequence, sponsors, and occupancy pattern of its founding-era
  infrastructure;
- the moon's early industrial history after the founding interval;
- whether any high-risk countermeasure trials have occurred there;
- which facilities maintain controlled-variance and calorimetric references;
- the relationship between civil observatories and military ranging stations;
- and the political authority under which its reference vaults operate.

No confirmed Manifold rupture, live Rzez experiment, occupied or hostile Closed
Night trial, or weaponized Agreement deployment is assigned to Odran.
