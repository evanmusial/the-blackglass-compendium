# Odran

## Role in the home system

Odran is the airless inner moon of [Kharos](kharos.md). It is approximately
3,475 kilometres in diameter and orbits the planet at approximately 200,000
kilometres every 6.37 days.

The moon supports scattered industrial, forensic, observational, and
chronometric installations. It does not contain one continuous underground city.
Its value comes from physical separation, low environmental noise, vacuum, and
the ability to place one facility beyond the material and utility paths of
another.

## Reference architecture

Odran's standards vaults and laboratories occupy independent pads with separate
power, landing, thermal-control, and data systems. No vault is the sole custodian
of an archive. Material references and records are copied to Kharos and Avara so
that isolation cannot become either accidental erasure or quiet revision.

Post-Valec practice treats a continuous foundation, service tunnel, or buried
utility spine as a possible propagation path. Facilities that need to exchange
samples use removable carriers and stand-off transfer points rather than
permanent physical continuity wherever practical.

## Clocks and distant comparison

Odran maintains independent clocks, optical ranging stations, and exterior
sensor baselines for the Kelan–Ruva Concordance Service. Its vacuum and stable
instrument platforms make it useful for detecting timing and path residuals
that would be obscured by Kharosi weather or Avaran life-support activity.

The Odran clocks are not a master timebase. Their value is that they can disagree
independently. Records are authenticated locally and compared later with Kharos,
Avara, and more distant stations. Optical links are physically broken only when
local shard conditions or participating equipment implicate them in
establishment, not because a distant report is dangerous to receive.

## Biological limits

Odran can store sealed nonconscious tissue lineages and dry components used by
boundary-medicine facilities. It is not a routine prison for conscious Plain
Things. For an environment-coupled open form, a failed pressure seal or deliberate
exposure to the surface would be an execution, not neutral containment.

The same fact creates continuing political pressure to use Odran as an easy
answer to Unhidden custody. Kharosi and Avaran humane-care doctrine rejects that
answer unless a later legal decision explicitly changes it.

## Deliberately unresolved

- the names, ownership, and exact locations of Odran installations;
- the moon's early industrial history;
- whether any high-risk countermeasure trials have occurred there;
- the relationship between civil observatories and military ranging stations;
- and the political authority under which its reference vaults operate.

No confirmed Manifold rupture or live Rzez experiment is assigned to Odran.
