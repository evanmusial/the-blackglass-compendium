# Apsis Range

## Setting role

Apsis Range is the remote, deliberately uninhabited test volume in which the
unique instrument called [The Rehearsal](../../entries/volume-04/book-01/d-1-01-the-rehearsal.md)
was assembled and fired. It lies outside the Kelan–Ruva system and outside any
inhabited planetary environment. The exact containing system and route are
withheld in the records available to the Blackglass recension.

*Apsis* names an engineered range, not a world, star, state, or new center of
settlement. Personnel lived on removable work platforms at its outer margins.
There was no native population and no civil ecology whose later history must be
invented to explain the site.

## Physical arrangement

The range was built around a sparse focal volume selected for its low dust
density, long clear lines of sight, and distance from planets, habitats, and
compact stellar remnants. Its principal structures were distributed rather
than collected into one station:

- outer power stores and timing platforms;
- long-baseline projectors and diagnostic stations;
- the terminal **convergence crown**, whose projectors defined the final firing
  geometry;
- removable target and **baryon-bias** packages near the focus; and
- remote witness instruments placed outside the crown's expected debris and
  radiation cones.

No one projector could produce the claimed focal condition. Each carried a
finite, destructive but comprehensible pulse. The extreme state existed only
where the synchronized pulses thermalized together. This distinction is why the
range extended across a large volume even though each Rehearsal kernel was
microscopic.

## Why Apsis was empty

Directorate records give two compatible reasons for the site's isolation. The
public program required a clean background for primordial-state measurements.
The restricted program required a place where a failed convergence would not
strike an inhabited world.

Later defenders of the project treated the absence of inhabitants as proof of
responsible design. The [Apsis Discharge](../events/apsis-discharge.md) showed
the limit of that argument. Isolation reduced the immediate casualty field. It
did not answer whether an unknown long-lived fragment could leave the range or
whether a remote experiment could impose risk upon matter far outside it.

## Condition after the Discharge

The Compact Year +271 firing consumed the prepared baryon sleeve, most terminal
hardware, and the inner diagnostic platforms. Radiation, relativistic debris,
and the failure of several projectors made immediate approach impossible. Some
outer power and timing structures survived, but no accepted inspection has
found a complete operable Rehearsal.

The present interdiction volume is larger than the original range. Warning
beacons mark approach corridors without broadcasting a complete map of the
surviving crown geometry. Remote instruments watch for energetic debris,
unusual charge-to-mass tracks, and unauthorized power synchronization. Their
silence is evidence that nothing was detected within their sensitivity; it is
not proof that no strangelet escaped.

The site is dangerous for ordinary reasons—stored energy, damaged projectors,
radiation, high-velocity debris, and possibly exotic baryonic matter. No
persistent failure of geometry, chronology, or structural identity is assigned
to Apsis. It is not a Manifold rupture, a Rzez-contaminated place, or a Closed
Night enclosure.

## Jurisdiction and access

The [Common Matter Interdict](../institutions/common-matter-interdict.md)
prohibits salvage, sample return, projector synchronization, and entry into the
inner range without a narrowly defined remote-observation mandate. No single
Kharosi office owns the site. Enforcement is maintained by several signatory
authorities whose identities and division of responsibility remain partly
redacted.

The most dangerous scavengers are associated in public accounts with the
[Houses of the Lower State](../institutions/houses-of-the-lower-state.md), but
ordinary weapons programs, illicit laboratories, and collectors have also
attempted approach. A religious claim is not treated as evidence that a vessel
is carrying a seed or firing component.

## Deliberately unresolved

- the name and astronomy of the containing system;
- the complete dimensions and original number of crown stations;
- the number of people present on removable platforms during the Discharge;
- which surviving structures are genuine projectors rather than decoys or
  ordinary diagnostics;
- whether any material fragment escaped the expanded interdiction volume; and
- whether the authorities withholding the range map retain enough information
  to reconstruct the instrument they prohibit.
