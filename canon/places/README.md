## Named places retained in the Rzez entry

The following remain entry-local until reused:

- **Keth Aru:** abandoned salt and obsidian city associated with the black-wedge
  artifact tradition.
- **Orsa:** highland region of dry-stone fortifications and anti-imperial clans.
- **Deren Keep:** frontier fortress on the western grain road.
- **Kareth:** location of the dynastic ossuary destroyed during the War of Seven
  Heirs.
- **Serevan:** maritime city-state known for ceramic-composite ships.
- **Orel:** river and city served by the Seven Bridges.
- **Avel Taran:** militarized plateau kingdom.
- **Leth Var:** university city associated with biological engineering.
- **Meren:** post-Compact desert city lost above a buried research complex.

This index records spelling and role without prematurely inventing geography or
political relationships. See [`../setting-scope.md`](../setting-scope.md) before
assigning any entry-local place to a world or system.
