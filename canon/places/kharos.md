# Kharos

## Home-world role

Kharos is the setting's designated home world: the recurring point of return for
ordinary life, institutions, politics, and scholarship when those elements are
not better served by another place.

This is an authorial and narrative designation, not an origin claim. Humanity
originated on Earth and entered the Kelan–Ruva system from elsewhere. Avara
preserves the system's oldest continuously occupied human refuge, while durable
settlement on Kharos belongs to a later phase. Kharos became *home* through
population, civic depth, and repeated return rather than because it was first.

The designation does not make Kharos the capital of a unified interstellar
civilization or the assumed place where the Blackglass Compendium was assembled.

## Provisional placement

Kharos is a surviving inhabited core world, separate from Valec. It is the
principal current legal and academic center associated with Rzez control. The
Sixth Compact was negotiated, ratified, or institutionally centered there.

This placement is an authorial setting decision supported by the Rzez's repeated
Kharosi legal and academic references; it is not a geographic description found
verbatim in the exemplar.

## Planet and system

By out-of-world authorial comparison, Kharos has a mean diameter of approximately
16,600 kilometres, or about 1.3 Earth diameters. Its working mass is 2.6 Earth
masses, consistent with a compressed rocky planet, and its surface gravity is
approximately 1.54 g. These comparisons use terrestrial units for authorial
clarity; Earth-origin history does not require every current culture or archive
to retain the same standards.

Kharos orbits the barycenter of [Kelan and Ruva](kelan-ruva-system.md), a G4 V
primary and M3 V red dwarf. Its low-eccentricity orbit has a semimajor axis of
0.96 AU and a period of approximately 299 days. It receives almost exactly Earth's
mean stellar flux. Kelan and Ruva complete their inertial mutual orbit every
16.65 days. Because Kharos moves prograde around the same barycenter, the paired
stars return to the same observed presentation from Kharos approximately every
17.63 days.

## Settlement history and the making of a home world

The [founding sequence](../history/human-arrival-in-kelan-ruva.md) is known more
securely than its dates. Earth-originating people maintained a continuous refuge
on Avara before any durable Kharosi settlement survived. Odran acquired ranging,
transfer, storage, and observational infrastructure during the same broad
Founding Interval. Kharos was settled later. The surviving record does not
establish one first landing, one founding vessel, or one authority responsible
for the transition.

The order is physically consequential. A refuge already adapted to managed air
and low gravity could receive people and equipment before communities were ready
to make ordinary life under 1.54 g durable. Kharos then offered a much larger
world on which settlements, transport networks, archives, and civic institutions
could accumulate. Over time it became the demographic and political point of
reference even though Avara remained older and Odran retained founding-era
infrastructure.

The prolonged change from supported survey stations to self-sustaining surface
communities is remembered as the **Heavy Descent**. It was not one landing.
Sealed coastal, lakeside, and upland stations remained dependent upon orbital and
Avaran support while people tested Kharosi air, water, local chemistry, and the
medical limits of sustained high gravity. Durable settlement begins historically
when evacuation to Avara stops being the assumed remedy for every serious
failure, not when the first person touches the surface.

By the middle-to-late Meridian Age, the three bodies formed an integrated
economy. Kharos supplied the largest inhabited market and institutional base;
Avara supplied low-gravity yards, maintained-environment expertise, and orbital
industry; Odran supplied vacuum processing, ranging, material references, and
physically separated work sites. This was interdependence, not proof of one
planetary government.

Valec changed the shape of that integration. Shared utilities and data trunks
that had once advertised efficiency came to look like propagation paths. Later
construction kept trade and communication dense while making survival local:
sectional services, sacrificial disconnects, independent clocks, and stores that
could outlast isolation. The modern triworld system is therefore both highly
connected and deliberately capable of becoming three systems for a time.

## Lived high gravity

Kharos's approximately 1.54 g surface gravity is not decorative astronomy. It
shapes transport, construction, clinical practice, and the ordinary sense of
weight.

Loads that a person might casually carry elsewhere belong on carts, hoists, or
powered frames. Public routes favor shallow rises, frequent rests, handholds, and
lifts whose loss does not make an occupied level unreachable. Furniture is low
and stable. Storage above head height is treated as a fall hazard, and dropped
tools are controlled with the seriousness that lower-gravity workplaces reserve
for powered machinery. A stumble has more energy and leaves less time to recover.

These practices are ordinary infrastructure rather than a planet-wide medical
exception. A visitor notices them immediately; a long resident notices their
absence elsewhere. Heavy-world competence is expressed through careful load
paths and economical movement, not through pretending that mass has stopped
mattering.

Post-Valec public works tend toward low, broad, cellular structures with visible
inspection seams, independent load paths, sectional utilities, and lifting
systems designed for routine use rather than exceptional disability. Cohesion
sonography and acoustic inspection are common in consequential structures
because modest hidden weakness becomes dangerous quickly under Kharosi load.

Crushing injuries, circulatory stress, and difficult rehabilitation create
strong civil demand for regenerative support. This pressure helps explain why
the first open wards of total-boundary medicine belong on Kharos without making
the planet the site of the lineage's later military conversion.

No human biological adaptation to Kharosi gravity is presently fixed. People may
depend upon training, medicine, assistive structures, inherited physiology, or a
mixture that later entries must establish deliberately.

## The paired-sun sky

Kelan and Ruva are not equal suns. Kelan supplies approximately ninety-eight
percent of the system's visible and thermal stellar energy. Ruva is a smaller red
presence that can stand several degrees away from it, tint the light that remains
inside a Kelan-cast shadow, and produce a weaker second shadow of its own.

The approximately 17.63-day observed cycle changes the separation and direction
of those shadows and shifts instantaneous irradiation by roughly seven percent
to either side of the mean. The atmosphere and oceans buffer the thermal
response, but solar output, weather models, exterior work, and power reserves all
track paired-sun phase. It is a forecast variable, not a recurring global winter.

The nearly coplanar geometry also produces alternating stellar conjunctions at
intervals of roughly 8.82 days. When Ruva crosses Kelan, daylight can fall by
approximately ten to thirteen percent for several hours. When Kelan hides Ruva,
the loss is much smaller. Exact visibility depends upon location and eclipse
season, but the asymmetry is unmistakable: one crossing visibly dims the day;
the other removes its red edge.

Public schedules and ceremony may use these events, and old records can be
checked against their geometry. No named civil calendar is fixed from them. The
17.63-day presentation cycle, the moons' periods, and Kharos's 299-day orbit are
not commensurate enough to supply one effortless calendar, and a Compact year is
not automatically a Kharosi orbital year.

## Moons, tides, and near space

Kharos has two natural moons:

- [**Odran**](odran.md) is an airless, barren rocky body broadly comparable in size and
  character to Earth's Moon. It orbits approximately 200,000 kilometres from
  Kharos every 6.37 days.
- [**Avara**](avara.md) has a mean diameter of approximately 6,250 kilometres, or about 1.8
  lunar diameters, a mass of approximately 0.11 Earth masses, and surface gravity
  near 0.46 g. It orbits approximately 650,000 kilometres from Kharos every 36.67
  days. Most of its surface remains unsettled and undeveloped. Permanent civilian
  settlement is limited, while industrial, military, and scientific installations
  are extensive both on the surface and in orbit.

Both moons are tidally locked to Kharos. Avara's approximately 41.8-day solar day,
low gravity, and exposure during the stars' active youth make its present
habitability consequential rather than automatic. Its breathable atmosphere is
secondary and is now actively monitored and replenished.

From Kharos, Odran appears approximately one degree across and Avara roughly 0.55
degrees across. Odran therefore looks nearly twice as wide as either stellar
disk, while cloud and weather movement can make Avara visibly a world rather
than a fixed mark. Their nearly coplanar paths produce conspicuous
occultations and eclipse seasons without making every passage an eclipse.

The moons also make coastal time complicated. The close Odran supplies the
stronger and faster tidal forcing; massive Avara adds a slower component whose
alignment with Odran continually changes. Local water levels still depend upon
Kharos's unresolved rotation, basin shape, winds, and pressure, so no universal
tide height is canon. What is fixed is that a single generic "high tide" is not a
useful operational category.

Ports publish both lunar phases, predicted local response, and uncertainty.
Coastal refuges, movable barriers, pumps, and transport schedules are designed
for compound extremes. Post-Valec shore works avoid one continuous defensive
spine where independent cells can serve: a wall that saves every district when
intact may also connect every district when continuity itself becomes dangerous.
Tide records and eclipse observations provide long historical baselines, though
lunar migration prevents them from becoming effortless exact dates for the
founding interval.

Kharos, Odran, and Avara share a redundant laser-communications backbone. Direct
and relayed planet-to-moon and moon-to-moon links provide extraordinary aggregate
throughput with one-way light times of approximately 0.67 seconds to Odran, 2.17
seconds to Avara, and 1.5–2.8 seconds between the moons. This is intrasystem
infrastructure and does not imply faster-than-light communication or equivalent
capacity between star systems.

The light-time is part of ordinary life. A direct Kharos–Avara conversation
contains an unavoidable round-trip pause of roughly 4.3 seconds even under ideal
conditions. Institutions designed around immediate interruption or one central
operator therefore behave differently across the pair.

Turn-taking across that link is a learned courtesy rather than a formal silence.
Courts, clinics, traffic desks, and families leave room for an answer already in
flight. A pause cannot by itself be treated as refusal or confusion. Odran's
roughly 1.34-second Kharos round trip is shorter but still long enough to make
tightly reciprocal remote control unsafe without local authority.

## Continuity jurisprudence

Kharos is the principal public venue in which the five current engineering
systems acquire law, testimony, and ordinary consequence. The shared word
*continuity* describes a civic problem, not a shared physical mechanism.

- Rzez cases concern structural proof, propagation, Compact prohibition, and
  whether apparently intact matter can still bear trust.
- Unhidden cases concern the rights and custody of conscious human-made bodies,
  the ownership of tissue lineages, and whether environmental control is care,
  restraint, or coercion.
- Manifold cases concern mutually authentic incompatible evidence, quarantine,
  and how a court records a life whose supported events have no single order.
- Closed Night cases concern thermal egress, rescue authority, lawful civil
  return systems, and whether a surviving enclosure was physically escapable.
- Agreement cases concern bodily dissent, concealed reciprocal coupling,
  contaminated unanimous testimony, and the independence of an observer.

These matters do not automatically fall under one statute or agency. The
[Sixth Compact](../institutions/sixth-compact-of-kharos.md) remains rooted in the
Rzez. Created-person law, Manifold quarantine, the
[Articles of Open Radiance](../institutions/articles-of-open-radiance.md), and the
[Articles of Bodily Dissent](../institutions/articles-of-bodily-dissent.md)
require separate authority even when they borrow Compact procedure.

The [Institute of Applied Continuity](../institutions/institute-of-applied-continuity.md)
is the historical source of the most visible scholarly tradition spanning those
arguments. Whether present schools are the same legal institution or
post-Valec successors remains unresolved. Its name is an umbrella for cooperating
disciplines and a source of controversy, not a claim that it possesses a
universal theory.

## Medical and evidentiary role

The first open wards on Kharos establish the civil medical lineage from which
The Unhidden later descends. Large-scale growth and rehabilitation extend to
Avara, where low gravity changes the architecture of a mantle rather than simply
making a Kharosi form lighter.

Kharos also participates in the
[Kelan–Ruva Concordance Service](../institutions/kelan-ruva-concordance-service.md).
Its courts and archives can receive concordance bundles assembled from
independent clocks and records without pretending that one Kharosi master clock
decides which history is real.

After the Nacre Hall Incident, public buildings used for evidence, emergency
command, and high-risk coordination preserve physically independent witness
cells commonly called protected dissent chambers. Their clocks, stimuli,
records, and shutdown paths cannot all traverse the system being observed.
Kharos also supports discord rehabilitation for Agreement survivors; rapid
return to synchronized civic or clinical environments is treated as a recurrence
risk rather than efficient discharge.

Kharosi application of the Articles of Open Radiance treats an inspectable heat
exit as part of occupied-volume safety. Courts may authorize anchor destruction
or material heat bridges during rescue while separately reviewing the white-seam
risk created by a narrow breach.

No confirmed Manifold rupture is placed on Kharos or elsewhere in the home
system. Kharos studies exterior incidents; it does not possess or control The
Manifold.

## Supported associations

Kharos is the recurring geographic or political name associated with:

- the Institute of Applied Continuity;
- the Kharosi Assembly before which Olan Vey testifies;
- Kharosi military law;
- a later Kharosi reconstruction period;
- and the Sixth Compact of Kharos.

It is also associated with the Nacre Hall Incident, the Articles of Bodily
Dissent, the Articles of Open Radiance, protected variance procedure, and
discord rehabilitation.

It is also provisionally associated with the first open wards, created-person
and humane-custody proceedings, and the Kelan–Ruva Concordance Service.

Kharos is also associated with the later phase of Earth-origin settlement in
which the system's largest population and civic institutions moved beyond the
older Avaran refuge. That centrality does not cancel Avara's seniority or turn
Odran's founding infrastructure into a Kharosi possession.

## Deliberately unresolved

Kharos's rotation period, obliquity, population, governmental hierarchy, and the
boundaries of any wider Kharosi polity remain unassigned. The origin and tidal
history of its unusually massive moon system require further modeling, and
whether Kharos or Avara possessed prehuman biospheres remains open. The dates,
vessels, route, and institutional sponsors of the Earth-origin arrival and later
durable settlement also remain unassigned. Do not assume that every Compact
institution is governed directly by Kharos.

Kharos did not originate the Rzez, is not assigned responsibility for the
Unhidden's military conversion, and is not the site of the defining Manifold
encounter, the Pelar deployment, or Ninth Holding. Its home-world role should
make consequences familiar, not make all history provincial.
