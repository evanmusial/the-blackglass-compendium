# Historical Canon

This directory records historical processes whose scope is larger than one
incident, person, institution, or celestial body. A history note may establish
a sequence while preserving uncertainty about dates, motives, names, and
interpretation. It should distinguish authorial truth from the strength of the
evidence available to people inside the setting.

## Established histories

| History | Scope | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Human Arrival in Kelan–Ruva](human-arrival-in-kelan-ruva.md) | Earth's human and ecological inheritance, the Founding Interval, first settlement of Avara and Kharos, and the unrecoverable Earth-calendar crosswalk. | Established |

## Filing boundary

Individual catastrophes and discoveries belong in [`../events/`](../events/).
World and system properties belong in [`../places/`](../places/). Institutions
created by a historical process belong in
[`../institutions/`](../institutions/). A history note may connect those records,
but it should not replace their detailed physical, legal, or organizational
canon.
